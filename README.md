<summary><strong>✦ Three powers, three different ways of life</strong></summary>

Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu each have their own governments, cultures, resources, factions, and ambitions. Their differences shape trade, education, technology, prejudice, diplomacy, and the balance of power—without any one power controlling the whole world.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>✦ Grow through more than raw strength</strong></summary>

Martial Souls, Spirit Rings, Spirit Beasts, techniques, and the nine Spirit Realms shape cultivation. Thinkers follow a separate path through knowledge, formulations, and Artificial Artifacts. Ancient ruins, hidden factions, dangerous routes, and hard-to-find resources reward curiosity—but discovery is never guaranteed.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>✦ The world keeps moving when you do</strong></summary>

NPCs have their own priorities; trade, festivals, rivalries, and political tensions continue beyond a single scene. Travel has real routes and risks, resources depend on location, and actions can leave lasting consequences. Rumors aren't automatically facts, and strength doesn't erase every mistake.

</details>

- ✦ **Unified NPC Discovery Order Across INDEX and Authority**  
  Regional INDEX → local NPC table → registry/official roster → canonical profile, with consistent handling of descriptive references and continuity.
  Unidentified items now use the exact player-facing label `*Unknown* *membutuhkan appraisal untuk lebih pastinya*` across gameplay, character profiles, inventory, equipment, loot, transactions, artifacts, and consumption records. Appraisal reveals only facts supported by its findings; unknown prices, tiers, functions, rarity, and dangers must not be invented.

  Player Converse now requires a seven-character Hash from the active pinned snapshot and the Nightly version from that same snapshot's `NIGHTLY_VERSION.md`. The identity line appears immediately above the Merzkava time header.

- ✦ **Spirit Ring Color Bands and the Heaven Realm Threshold**  
  Spirit Ring colors use distinct age bands through 15,000,000 years. Beyond that threshold, beings are regarded as Heaven Realm beings; higher-age ring colors remain unspecified.

- ✦ **Beast Age Grade Formula and Open-Ended Age Grade 9**  
  Beast Age Grade uses actual specimen age and classifies age only—not Beast category, Spirit Realm, or appraisal strength. Grade 9 starts at 500,000 years and has no hard upper limit.
