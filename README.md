<details>
<summary><strong>✦ Three powers, three different ways of life</strong></summary>

Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu each have their own governments, cultures, resources, factions, and ambitions. Their differences shape trade, education, technology, prejudice, diplomacy, and the balance of power—without any one power controlling the whole world.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>✦ Grow through more than raw strength</strong></summary>

Martial Souls, Spirit Rings, Spirit Beasts, techniques, and the nine Spirit Realms shape cultivation. Thinkers follow a separate path through knowledge, formulations, and Artificial Artifacts. Ancient ruins, hidden factions, dangerous routes, and hard-to-find resources reward curiosity—but discovery is never guaranteed.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>✦ The world keeps moving when you do</strong></summary>

NPCs have their own priorities; trade, festivals, rivalries, and political tensions continue beyond a single scene. Travel has real routes and risks, resources depend on location, and actions can leave lasting consequences. Rumors aren't automatically facts, and strength doesn't erase every mistake.

</details>

- ✦ **Lawless Captive Trade — Public Secret**  
  Hash: ``2b6169a`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.114``  
  Established Lawless underworld networks as major suppliers of captive half-beasts to corrupt buyers connected to all three powers. The trade remains a public secret despite the three powers' official ban; participation is not assumed for every organization.

- ✦ **Half-Beast Restraint Abuse & Inner Azerion Map**  
  Hash: ``f0bbc2c`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.113``  
  Recorded the ban on half-beast enslavement across all three major powers, corrupt restraint abuse—especially in the Lawless Territories—and the resulting rarity of openly seen half-beasts there unless strong. Added Inner Azerion as a hidden central-forest city on the map/navigation references.

- ✦ **Prison Restraints & Bound Items**  
  Hash: ``07b5277`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.112``  
  Established restraint categories for Martial Soul suppression chains, ornamental sealing collars, physical-strength suppression, boundary binding, fear effects, and whisper-seals. Defined artifact distinctions, custody risks, and the possibility of corrupt charges or framed detainees without treating restraint effects as universal.

- ✦ **Lawless Southern Port & Lanternhook Isles**  
  Hash: ``d6311ec`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.111``  
  Established Southwake Port on the southern Lawless coast and Lanternhook Isles in ordinary Black Tide Sea, with named leisure, black-market medicine, and gambling locations. Connected the route toward Guixu Canghai while preserving the intervening sea boundary and marking unknown travel details.

- ✦ **Lawless Territories — Three-Power Map Integration**  
  Hash: ``0e150eb`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.110``  
  Connected Ashen Borderlands, Black Tide Expanse, and Nine Frost Wastes to the three power perspectives, shared navigation tree, geography, and route network. Preserved jurisdiction boundaries and marked unconfirmed local routes.

- ✦ **Unified Beast Taxonomy and Behavior**  
  Hash: ``746789f`` | Ver: ``Nightly-v2.4.109``  
  Consolidated Ordinary, Spirit, Supreme, Demonic, and Heavenly Beasts. Defined output ranges and behavioral baselines; rarity, mutation, corruption, and age remain separate traits.
