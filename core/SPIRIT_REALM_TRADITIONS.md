# Spirit Realm Traditions — Soul-Nyuma and Soul-Zoun

**Status:** Binding world-canon module  
**Scope:** The two comprehensive intellectual traditions concerned with Martial Soul and every phenomenon belonging to the Spirit Realm domain.  
**Authority:** This file defines doctrine and classification only. Numerical cultivation, awakening, Spirit Ring, compatibility, Sync, mutation, Spirit Beast, combat, and progression mechanics remain governed by their existing dedicated modules.

## 1. The Three Great Knowledge Disciplines

Merzkava recognizes three principal knowledge disciplines:

1. **Arcanistry** — the complete discipline and practice of Arcanists: study, research, teaching, formulation, engineering, and application. Its terminology and progression remain governed by `core/ARCANISTRY_AND_FORMULATION.md`.
2. **Spirit Realm — Soul-Nyuma** — the straight/natural tradition of Spirit Realm study.
3. **Spirit Realm — Soul-Zoun** — the aberrant/deviation-oriented tradition of Spirit Realm study.

Arcanistry is independent of the two Spirit Realm traditions. It is not a third Spirit Realm doctrine, does not sit above them, and does not replace or merge with them. Arcanistry may investigate, support, document, or engineer applications relevant to either tradition, but Arcanist Grade and Spirit Realm progression remain separate.

## 2. Shared Domain of Soul-Nyuma and Soul-Zoun

Soul-Nyuma and Soul-Zoun study the **same complete domain**. Neither is a narrow combat school, a separate power system, or a discipline concerned with only one kind of phenomenon.

Their shared subject includes, wherever relevant:

- Martial Soul, its nature, awakening, form, inherent traits, condition, and development;
- Spirit Realm, cultivation, Qi, stages, progression, and breakthrough;
- Spirit Beast, its nature, age, traits, power, and relationship with a Martial Soul;
- Spirit Ring, acquisition, conversion, absorption, compatibility, synchronization, and resulting Spirit Ability;
- Spirit Ability formation, expression, combination, refinement, and practical use;
- natural development, evolution, mutation, abnormal change, adaptation, and interaction among all of the above.

This list describes the shared field of inquiry; it does not grant either tradition permission to bypass a mechanic or guarantee an outcome.

## 3. Spirit Realm — Soul-Nyuma

**Soul-Nyuma is the straight/natural tradition.** It seeks to understand, cultivate, and develop the inherent nature of the Spirit Realm domain through its underlying order, intrinsic characteristics, and naturally coherent relationships.

Its guiding approach is to:
- understand a Martial Soul according to its actual nature and inherent structure;
- work with the real characteristics and compatibility of Spirit Beasts and Spirit Rings;
- develop Spirit Abilities through coherent interaction, synchronization, training, and cultivation;
- study evolution, mutation, and unusual outcomes as phenomena to understand without presuming that every deviation should be induced or pursued;
- favor methods whose principles, processes, and consequences can be explained within the established natural order.

“Straight” does not mean universally correct, morally superior, infallible, simplistic, or incapable of innovation. Soul-Nyuma can be rigorous, creative, experimental, and intellectually contested. Its defining feature is fidelity to the intrinsic order it seeks to understand, not obedience to a government, academy, religion, or social majority.

## 4. Spirit Realm — Soul-Zoun

**Soul-Zoun is the aberration/deviation-oriented tradition.** It studies departures from the ordinary or naturally coherent order of the Spirit Realm domain: how aberrations arise, how they behave, how they interact with existing structures, and whether they can be understood, contained, redirected, integrated, or deliberately produced.

Its guiding approach is to:
- investigate abnormal states and departures from expected Martial Soul characteristics;
- examine atypical Spirit Ring compatibility, synchronization, and Spirit Ability outcomes;
- study mutation, reconstruction, forced adaptation, irregular evolution, and other departures from ordinary development;
- investigate interactions that natural-order models cannot adequately explain;
- test the limits, risks, and possible applications of aberrant phenomena through methods whose actual process and consequences must be established.

**Aberration is a descriptive concept, not a moral verdict.** Soul-Zoun is not inherently evil, forbidden, demonic, criminal, destructive, or synonymous with apocalypse. An aberrant result may be useful, harmless, unstable, dangerous, or catastrophic depending on its actual nature and consequences. A method may be prohibited by a particular law or institution without that prohibition defining Soul-Zoun as a whole.

Soul-Zoun does not mean that every unconventional technique, regional style, rare Martial Soul, mutation, or experimental practice automatically belongs to it. Classification requires a real aberration-oriented principle or method, not merely unfamiliarity or social disapproval.

## 5. The Fundamental Difference

Both traditions examine the same phenomena, but approach them from different starting principles:

- **Soul-Nyuma:** understand and develop the domain through its intrinsic nature and natural order.
- **Soul-Zoun:** understand and explore departures from that order, including their causes, behavior, limits, and possible applications.

The distinction is methodological and philosophical, not a ranking of power, legitimacy, morality, or social status. Neither tradition automatically defeats, invalidates, or supersedes the other. A person may study the other tradition critically or comparatively; do not assume dual mastery, institutional acceptance, or practical competence without evidence.

## 6. Classification of People, Schools, and Institutions

- The world's general term for a practitioner in this domain remains **ahli Spirit Realm**. Do not invent a universal replacement profession or assume every ahli Spirit Realm belongs to a formal school.
- A school, academy, clan, dojo, sect, teacher, research group, or individual may be associated with Soul-Nyuma or Soul-Zoun only when its actual teachings, methods, records, or declared doctrine support that classification.
- Do not assign a tradition solely from a region, species, Martial Soul type, political allegiance, reputation, appearance, or moral judgment.
- An institution may teach foundational knowledge shared by both traditions without being exclusively affiliated with either.
- An individual may learn methods from more than one tradition, but this does not automatically confer mastery or erase differences between their principles.
- Regional flexibility, traditional education, experimental research, restricted access, taboo, criminality, and legal prohibition are separate descriptors. None is a substitute for the Soul-Nyuma/Soul-Zoun classification.
- If the evidence is insufficient, classify the teaching simply as **Spirit Realm instruction** and leave its tradition unspecified.

## 7. Law, Risk, and Social Judgment

No universal law makes Soul-Zoun illegal. Legal status depends on the actual act, its consequences, local law, and the jurisdiction involved. No universal law makes every Soul-Nyuma method safe or every Soul-Zoun method dangerous.

Institutions and communities may favor, distrust, regulate, restrict, condemn, sponsor, or exploit particular methods for historical, religious, political, ethical, economic, or practical reasons. These are local positions and must be grounded in the relevant faction, regional, and legal canon. Do not convert one group's opinion into the world's objective doctrine.

Danger must be assessed from the actual method, evidence, subject, safeguards, and consequences. Do not grant Soul-Zoun practitioners automatic villain status, nor grant Soul-Nyuma practitioners automatic virtue or safety.

## 8. Aberration and the World Calamity

The ancient world-spanning calamity involved anomalies that exceeded safe limits, opened gates to other worlds, and allowed Tier 7–9 Demonic Beasts to descend. It is a historical example of catastrophic aberrant phenomena.

However:
- the calamity is not the definition of Soul-Zoun;
- Soul-Zoun as a field of study is not established as the cause of the calamity;
- not every aberration opens a gate, attracts a Demonic Beast, or escalates into an apocalypse;
- an AWR check, an anomaly, a gate opening, and a world-scale incursion remain distinct events governed by their dedicated mechanics and historical canon;
- no faction, practitioner, or tradition may be blamed for causing the calamity unless a canonical source or later owner-approved event establishes that responsibility.

The calamity may shape research ethics, public fear, institutional safeguards, historical memory, and political policy toward aberrant phenomena. The specific response varies by institution and region.

## 9. Retired Classification Language

The former four-part global classification—**Orthodox, Unbound, Forbidden, and Supremacy**—is retired and must no longer be used as the world's formal taxonomy of Spirit Realm traditions.

Do not mechanically map each old label onto Soul-Nyuma or Soul-Zoun. Their previous concepts may survive only as:
- a local school's self-description;
- a faction's doctrine, reputation, or political slogan;
- a legal or religious judgment;
- a historical term used by a particular source or speaker.

Such surviving usage must be contextualized and must not recreate the retired four-part global system. In particular, “forbidden” describes a restriction imposed by an authority, not an inherent property of Soul-Zoun; “orthodox” may describe a local institution's established custom but is not a universal Spirit Realm tradition.

## 10. Mechanical Authority and Canon Boundaries

This module does not alter or duplicate mechanics. Use the dedicated authority for each subject:

- `core/CULTIVATION_SYSTEM.md` — Spirit Realm levels, stages, Sync progress, bottlenecks, breakthrough, and slots.
- `core/MARTIAL_SOUL.md` — Martial Soul forms, traits, and lore.
- `core/MARTIAL_SOUL_AWAKENING.md` — awakening, rarity, assessment, and mutation procedures.
- `core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md` — Spirit Ring acquisition and absorption, compatibility, Sync cap, success, permanent defects, Qi, PowerScale, and AWR.
- `bestiary/SPIRIT_BEAST_RULES.md` — Spirit Beast age, tiers, and relevant beast rules.
- `gameplay/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md` — combat resolution.
- `core/ARCANISTRY_AND_FORMULATION.md` — Arcanistry and all Arcanist progression and production mechanics.
- `world/HISTORY.md` and `world/CANON_DECISIONS_AND_PRECEDENCE.md` — established historical events and binding world decisions.

A doctrine cannot override a formula, grant an ability, bypass a prerequisite, guarantee compatibility, reverse a failed process, or create an event by declaration. If a tradition's proposed method has no established mechanical resolution, treat it as a proposal or unresolved practice until the owner approves a rule and the relevant authority is updated.

## 11. GM Application

When a tradition matters to a scene:
1. Identify what the character, teacher, institution, or source actually knows and practices.
2. Classify it as Soul-Nyuma, Soul-Zoun, shared/foundational Spirit Realm instruction, or unspecified only when evidence supports the choice.
3. Explain the difference through the actual principle and method, not generic labels such as good/evil, orthodox/heretical, legal/illegal, or safe/dangerous.
4. Preserve the character's knowledge limits and the institution's access rules.
5. Resolve every actual action through the existing mechanical authority and record only outcomes that truly occur.

Do not force a tradition into every scene. The world remains socially, politically, economically, and culturally diverse; these traditions provide a framework for understanding Spirit Realm knowledge, not a replacement for the world's existing regions, factions, histories, or everyday life.
