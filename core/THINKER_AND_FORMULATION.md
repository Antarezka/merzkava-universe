# XUANLING REALM — THINKER & FORMULATION SYSTEM

**Status:** Draft mechanics integrated into the repository; baseline chance matrix is specified by the owner-provided design. Situational modifiers and resolution method remain undefined until formally approved.

## 1. THINKER: INDEPENDENT PROGRESSION

- Thinker has 10 tiers: Thinker Tier 1 through Thinker Tier 10.
- Thinker is not a cultivation Realm. Progression concerns knowledge, materials, formulations, patterns, and artifact-making processes.
- Thinkers use metals, stone, special materials, patterns, and complex formulations to produce Artificial Artifacts.
- Cultivation Realm and Thinker tier progress independently. High cultivation does not automatically make someone a great Thinker, and high Thinker tier does not automatically make someone a powerful cultivator.
- The first Thinkers originated in Tianhai Yunzhou. The world's strongest Thinkers emerge from this knowledge tradition; Tianhai's advantage includes exceptional knowledge, not only combat strength.
- Thinker tier determines the normal baseline capability and formulation ceiling. It does not guarantee success in every attempt.

## 2. THREE DISTINCT ARTIFACT ORIGINS

### Relic
An inheritance or object left by a deity. Further properties are unknown unless another official source defines them.

### Ancient Artifact
An artifact originating from the golden age of civilization.

### Artificial Artifact
An artifact manufactured by a Thinker using materials, patterns, and formulations.

These categories are distinct by origin and must not be treated as interchangeable. An Artificial Artifact is not automatically a Relic or an Ancient Artifact, and vice versa.

**Established scale example:** a giant island is powered by an Artificial Artifact Tier 9. Its maker, detailed materials, energy source, maintenance, mechanism, and weaknesses are not defined here unless an official source adds them. Do not invent those details as permanent canon.

## 3. KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE AND CULTURAL INFLUENCE

### 3.1 Tianhai Yunzhou
- Origin of the first Thinkers and a major center of Thinker knowledge.
- The Tianhai Maharaja can compete through exceptional knowledge, not solely personal combat strength.
- Thinker knowledge is one source of Tianhai's cultural influence.
- Knowledge and formulation development may become channels for cultural exchange, diplomacy, and rivalry.

### 3.2 Golden Dragon Empire
- The Empire is comparatively open to knowledge exchange and interaction with outside powers.
- Exchanges between Tianhai and the Empire are important routes for the spread of knowledge and cultural influence.
- Exchange may affect diplomacy and relations as well as technology.
- The spread of knowledge does not automatically give every party equal production capability. Thinker skill, materials, and formulation mastery remain limiting factors.

### 3.3 Beiming Gu and the Northern Region
- The North is relatively isolated.
- Its cold climate helps preserve ancient materials.
- Exploration may uncover extensive resources, especially materials useful for engraving Artificial Artifact formations.
- Northern materials are strategically important to artifact technology, not merely ordinary commodities.
- Material availability alone does not guarantee successful production; suitable knowledge, formulations, and processing skill are still required.

## 4. REQUIREMENTS FOR FORMULATION AND PRODUCTION

Producing an Artificial Artifact requires:
- A formulation understood and assembled by the Thinker.
- Focus and concentration throughout the process.
- Mental ability to understand, construct, and maintain complex formulation patterns.
- Sufficient stamina/energy to continue working.
- Suitable materials and applicable patterns.
- A Thinker tier capable of handling the target artifact under the normal tier limits.

Thinker tier limits the artifact tier that can normally be produced. Meeting the tier requirement does not automatically guarantee success.

A successful formulation does not mean the process is free of cost, energy consumption, time, or consequences. Apply only costs and consequences defined by official mechanics. Do not improvise numerical stamina costs, durations, or thresholds.

## 6. FACTORS THAT MAY AFFECT THE ATTEMPT

The GM must assess actual conditions, but must not invent numerical modifiers.

### Focus and concentration
- Stable concentration is the standard condition.
- Minor distraction may impair work or increase failure risk if a defined rule permits this.
- Severe interruption or broken concentration may cause failure when supported by the established resolution rules.
- Do not assume every attempt takes place under ideal conditions.

### Mental ability
Mental ability affects understanding, arranging, and sustaining complex formulation patterns. Limitations may increase error risk, particularly near the Thinker's tier ceiling. Apply numeric effects only when officially defined.

### Stamina/energy
The process requires energy throughout the work.
- Sufficient energy allows normal work.
- Low energy may slow or disrupt work.
- If the Thinker cannot continue, the process may fail under the applicable rules.

Numerical energy costs, work duration, and thresholds are not yet defined. The GM must not invent them.

### Materials
- Suitable materials of adequate quality: standard conditions.
- Low-quality or poorly matched materials may reduce success only under an established rule.
- If a required material is missing, the formulation cannot be completed unless an officially recognized substitute exists.

Rare materials do not automatically produce a stronger artifact. The outcome still depends on formulation and Thinker capability.

### Special talent
Relevant talents may concern pattern comprehension, concentration, processing a particular material, or a particular Artificial Artifact category. A talent applies only when relevant to the task and its effect is explicitly recorded in the character profile, talent system, or official rule. Do not invent numerical bonuses, stamina efficiency, or exceptions. A talent does not automatically bypass Thinker tier limits.

## 7. FORMULATION RESOLUTION PROCEDURE

For each attempt, the GM must:

1. Identify the Thinker's tier.
2. Identify the target Artificial Artifact tier and type.
3. Read the baseline chance from the official matrix.
4. Assess focus, mental ability, stamina, materials, and relevant talents.
5. Apply only modifiers authorized by official rules.
6. Resolve the attempt using an officially established method.
7. Record the actual result and any supported consequences.

Possible outcomes include success and failure. Material damage, energy consumption, and additional consequences may be applied only when supported by official mechanics.

**Unresolved mechanic:** a numeric conversion formula for situational factors and talents, and a formal random-resolution method, have not yet been established. Until approved, the GM must not invent percentage modifiers or pretend a random roll occurred. If a gameplay attempt requires an unresolved method, state the limitation and ask the owner/player for the chosen official resolution rule, or resolve only what the existing rules actually support.

## 8. FUTURE FORMULATION DEVELOPMENT

Artificial Artifact technology may advance through:
- Higher Thinker tiers and improved capability.
- Knowledge exchange between civilizations.
- Discovery of new materials and resources.
- Development and refinement of formulations.
- Mastery of more complex patterns.
- New production methods, only after they are established as official lore.

Knowledge exchange between Tianhai and the Golden Dragon Empire may open paths for scientific development. Ancient materials in the North may support research and formation engraving. Knowledge spreading does not automatically make all civilizations equally capable; Thinker skill, materials, and formulation mastery remain limiting factors.

Mechanics for refining formulations, breakthroughs, or technologies beyond normal limits must be documented before the GM treats them as canon.

## 10. CANON PRINCIPLE

Thinker is an independent knowledge-and-formulation progression path. Thinker tier determines baseline chance and the normal formulation ceiling. Actual conditions, materials, and talents affect the attempt only through official mechanics.

Narration alone does not guarantee success. The GM may not invent bonuses or exceptions, and may not override a valid resolution simply to force a preferred outcome.
