**GM-sensitive hidden history:** During the ancient world-calamity incursion, Beiming Han helped the World Knowledge Tower's effort to suppress the apocalyptic threat and subdued one of the Demonic Beasts that had descended through an otherworldly gate. He succeeded in purifying its mind; its existence then rose to become a rank-9 Heavenly Beast. This is the colossal dragon with a deer-like head, enormous branching antlers, and deep-blue eyes that later dwelled deep in Azerion Forest's Dead Zone. The encounter left a scar on Beiming Han. Its name, the exact battle chronology, the method and limits of purification, and further capabilities remain unknown. Only a very small number of people know this truth; Beiming Xenhwa is among them. This is not ordinary public knowledge and must not be revealed without a justified in-world discovery.

**GM-sensitive family history:** Beiming Han's close friend was a white nine-tailed fox woman who died during the same world-calamity catastrophe. He rescued her daughter, Beiming Xenhwa, and later adopted her. Do not invent the mother's personal name, the exact circumstances of her death, or the exact rescue scene.
