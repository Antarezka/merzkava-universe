## Registry rules

- Add only NPCs supported by a canonical source or explicitly owner-approved.
- Keep local NPC tables beside the location, institution, or faction they belong to.
- Use one canonical full profile per recurring/major NPC; link to it rather than duplicate the biography.
- Record home/usual residence, usual base, last-confirmed whereabouts, and recurring visits separately when established; do not confuse a visit or disguise with residence or official membership.
- Unknown details remain unknown. Do not invent Spirit Realm, Martial Soul, Spirit Rings, formal abilities, current whereabouts, or punishment.
- Use the full name **Beiming Han**; do not substitute a nickname in canonical references.
- Keep Beiming Jingheng's criminal connection and other GM-only details separate from public/player-character knowledge.
