# Merzkava Universe — Tianhai Yunzhou: NPC Power Structure and Officeholder Roster

**Canonical location:** `regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/POWER_STRUCTURE.md`

**Status:** Owner-approved canon roster.
**Scope:** Major and recurring named NPCs who hold offices or recognized leadership roles within Tianhai Yunzhou's sphere of authority. This is not a census of all 85 million residents; background residents remain situational NPCs under `world/NPC_MANAGEMENT.md`.
**Authority:** Use with `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md`, `world/WORLD.md`, `world/FACTIONS.md`, `world/NPC_MANAGEMENT.md`, and `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md`.

## 1. Canon rules for the roster

- **Thinker requirement:** Every newly named recurring or major officeholder in this roster is already a Thinker. The default band is **Thinker Grade 3–7**. Thinker Grades 8–10 exist only among a very small elite.
- **Separate progression:** Thinker Grade and Spirit Realm are independent. Neither determines the other, and neither guarantees victory or success at artifact production.
- **Spirit Realm benchmark:** Use the same practical band as the Golden Dragon Empire's named civil administration: ordinary administrators and officeholders generally Spirit Realm 3–6. Senior military, security, and high-risk command roles may reach Spirit Realm 7. Spirit Realm 8–9 is not assigned to ordinary officeholders; the Maharaja's established Spirit Realm 9 remains an exceptional ruler-level case.
- **Stages:** Every named character's Stage is included where assigned. The listed realm is the character's current established capability, not an automatic promotion from office.
- **Existing canon is preserved:** Ji Haoyuan is Thinker Grade 10 and Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage. Bai Xuanyin was Thinker Grade 10 and Spirit Realm 7 — Spirit Emperor; she is deceased. Ji Zhaoning has not awakened and her Thinker Grade is still unestablished; Ji Ningzhao is awakened at Spirit Realm 3 — Spirit Elder, Early Stage. These established family exceptions are not rewritten to fit the standard roster.
- **Grade 8+:** The high-grade elite is deliberately small. Existing canon records four Thinker Grade 10 individuals associated with Tianhai's Thinker tradition, including Ji Haoyuan; Bai Xuanyin is a confirmed Grade 10 historical figure. Do not assume the other Grade 10 identities, present status, or exact count of living Grade 10s without a later owner decision.
- **No invented private secrets:** Role, public working remit, Spirit Realm, and Thinker Grade are roster facts. Personal secrets, hidden loyalties, exact Spirit Rings, artifact recipes, crimes, and private family relationships remain unspecified unless separately recorded.
- **NPC profile policy:** This roster is the canonical source for these office appointments and core capability fields. If an NPC becomes a major recurring story character, create a full profile using `templates/NPC_PROFILE_TEMPLATE.md` and link it here.

## 2. Chain of authority

```text
Maharaja Ji Haoyuan
├── Maharaja's Council and Grand Secretariat
│   ├── Civil administration and finance
│   ├── Justice, records, and public oversight
│   └── Education, Thinker standards, and research
├── Maritime and Harbor Command
│   ├── Cangqiong Harbor administration
│   ├── Fleet logistics and navigation safety
│   └── Sea-route surveillance and rescue
├── Tianhai Defense Command
│   ├── Floating-island perimeter and protective systems
│   ├── Island garrison and emergency response
│   └── Outer-island / shoal patrol coordination
├── Seven District Administrations
├── Twelve Clans (recognized power centers; not automatically subordinate in every internal matter)
├── Five Dojos (training institutions; separate from civil and military command)
└── Thinker institutions, workshops, and artifact quality oversight
```

This is a functional structure for Tianhai's governing sphere. It does not mean every branch has unlimited jurisdiction, every clan answers to every office, or civilian officials automatically command military units. The Maharaja's lawful appointments and established mandates govern actual authority.

## 9. Thinker institutions and artifact quality control

The Tianhai Thinker Institute is the world's only established institution at this level and breadth of Thinker research. Its exclusive institutional remit covers Formation Engraver development, materials research, formulation and mixing methods, complex formation engraving for Tier 6+, and optimization of engraving and production techniques for Tier 5 and below. It is a research and standards center, not a guarantee of production success; the official Thinker Grade and formulation mechanics still apply. ## 10. Operational staff and local recurring NPCs

Operational positions below are part of the formal structure but are intentionally **unassigned** until a named person is needed. The staff member encountered in a scene may be a background NPC and need not receive a permanent full profile.

- Each district may have clerks, inspectors, porters, registry officers, maintenance workers, and local service staff.
- Harbor teams include cargo registrars, pilot coordinators, shipwright supervisors, customs inspectors, and rescue dispatchers.
- Defense units include watch officers, supply officers, medical support, patrol leaders, and garrison clerks.
- Thinker institutions may employ laboratory assistants, apprentices, material graders, and workshop technicians.
- Clans and dojos may have deputies, elders, instructors, students, and branch representatives.

All **named recurring or major officeholders** added later must be Thinkers. Unless the owner explicitly approves an exception, keep their Thinker Grade between 3 and 7 and their Spirit Realm within the role-appropriate range. Unnamed ordinary residents are not presumed to be Thinkers merely because they live in Tianhai.

## 11. Authority, knowledge, and conflict rules

- A person's Thinker Grade is not a measure of political loyalty, legal authority, or combat victory.
- Office, clan status, and cultivation do not automatically override lawful jurisdiction.
- Do not invent rivalries, corruption, secret factions, or family ties as canon. A role may create plausible competing interests, but actual conflict requires established motives or an event.
- A named NPC's location is not proof they are present in a scene. Use the current state and regional route network.
- The ruling family's profile files remain the canonical source for the four existing family NPCs. This roster does not overwrite their established details.
- For future major/recurring NPCs, create a canonical full profile and link it here and in the regional registry.
