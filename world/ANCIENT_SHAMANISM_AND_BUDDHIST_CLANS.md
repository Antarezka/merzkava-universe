# Merzkava Universe — Ancient Shamanism and Buddhist Mountain Clans

**Status:** Owner-established world canon.
**Scope:** Two distinct, hidden groups inhabiting remote mountain peaks where a vast sea of clouds spreads below the summits.
**Related modules:** `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`, `world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md`, `world/FACTIONS.md`, `core/CULTIVATION_SYSTEM.md`, and `core/MARTIAL_SOUL.md`.

## 1. Shared Geographic Context

The two groups dwell far from the ordinary centers of the three major powers, in remote high mountains whose summits rise above the sea of clouds. They are possible discoveries for players who travel beyond the familiar territories of the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu.

- Their existence is canon; their exact mountain names, coordinates, routes, boundaries, and travel times are not established.
- Do not place either group inside a major power or claim that a state governs them without further canon.
- Their secrecy means a traveler is not guaranteed to find them simply by entering the wilderness. Discovery must follow the actual route, circumstances, and gameplay.
- These are two separate groups. Do not merge their beliefs, membership, Martial Souls, leadership, or combat traditions.

## 2. Ancient Shamanism Clan/Sect

### 2.1 Identity and secrecy

Ancient Shamanism is one of the oldest clans or sects in Merzkava Universe. It is exceptionally secluded and has fewer than nine people in the world. Do not invent an exact total or add members casually; any future change to its population must respect this strict limit unless the owner revises it.

The clan recruits orphans who display the particular inclination and characteristics it seeks, especially a matching Martial Soul nature. Its members regard this work as a **sacred duty**.

### 2.2 Martial Soul: Spirit Bell

Members possess a distinctive Martial Soul known as the **Spirit Bell**. Each individual's bell differs in its form, color, and sound. These individual differences are part of the Martial Soul's identity; do not make every member's bell visually or audibly identical.

The clan borrows power from both **sacred spirits and cursed spirits**. The precise entities, methods of contact, costs, risks, rituals, limits, and combat techniques have not been established. Do not assume that borrowing from a cursed spirit automatically makes a member evil, nor that sacred spirits guarantee that a member is morally good.

### 2.3 Thinker prohibition

Ancient Shamanism firmly rejects the pursuit and mastery of the **Thinker** path. Its members do not study Thinker disciplines and remain devoted to the power of their Martial Souls.

This is a doctrinal rejection of the Thinker path. Do not infer unrecorded mechanics such as immunity to Artificial Artifacts, inability to use ordinary tools, or automatic superiority over Thinkers.

### 2.4 Known hierarchy and cultivation

Use the official term **Spirit Realm** and its established names.

| Position | Established Spirit Realm | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Clan/sect leader | Spirit Primordial (Spirit Realm 9) | Highest authority |
| First Grand Elder | Spirit Sovereign (Spirit Realm 8) | Directly beneath the leader |
| Inner Elder | Spirit Emperor (Spirit Realm 7) | Inner hierarchy |
| Outer Elder | Spirit Emperor (Spirit Realm 7) | Outer hierarchy |
| Roaming senior members assigned to recruitment | Spirit Realm 4–5 | Travel the world to find suitable orphaned children |

The roaming senior members carry out the sacred recruitment duty. The precise number of these travelers and whether a given individual also holds another internal designation are not specified; do not double-count titles as proof of additional people. The clan's total population remains fewer than nine.

The remaining members are inner and outer disciples. Their exact number, individual identities, ages, names, and cultivation levels are not established. Do not generate a complete roster or assign individual details as permanent canon without owner approval.

### 2.5 Distinctive appearance of roaming seniors

The clan's roaming senior members have a recognizable appearance:

- Distinctive black robes.
- Two sashes, one over each shoulder, bearing ancient golden script.
- An umbrella carried as part of their characteristic presentation.

The exact script, umbrella construction, symbolism, materials, and supernatural functions are not yet established. The umbrella must not automatically be treated as a weapon or a magical artifact.

## 3. Buddhist Mountain Clan

### 3.1 Identity and way of life

A separate Buddhist clan lives in secluded high mountains, oriented toward the heavens and a life removed from worldly distractions. Its members value inner cultivation and spiritual self-refinement.

The clan's formal name, exact population, founding history, internal ranks beyond the leader, and relationship with other religious institutions are not established. Do not automatically equate it with Ordo Suci Tianming or any other named organization.

### 3.2 Leader and Martial Souls

The clan's leader is at **Spirit Sovereign (Spirit Realm 8)**.

The clan's Martial Souls are associated with the **heavens and lightning**. Specific Martial Soul names, forms, individual grades, Spirit Rings, and techniques remain undefined unless established for a particular character. The leader's Spirit Realm does not by itself establish the leader's exact Martial Soul or combat ranking.

### 3.3 Philosophy of repentance

The clan is peaceful and welcomes anyone who genuinely wishes to repent of their sins. A person's terrible past alone does not disqualify them from acceptance, provided their intention to repent is sincere.

This does not mean repentance erases past harm, guarantees forgiveness from victims, removes legal consequences, or forces every member of the clan to trust a newcomer immediately. The clan's acceptance is grounded in the person's sincere intent to change.

### 3.4 Combat style

Its combat style is graceful and expressive, using **mudras and deliberate hand gestures** to direct attacks, including lightning-based strikes and other effects consistent with its heaven-associated Martial Souls.

The specific mudras, named techniques, range, damage, costs, speed, and combat mechanics have not been defined. Resolve actual combat using the official gameplay and combat systems; do not treat the aesthetic description as a license to invent numerical effects.

## 4. Discovery and Roleplay Boundaries

1. These groups can be encountered during exploration far beyond the three major powers, especially in remote high mountains above the sea of clouds, but their precise location and discovery conditions are still open.
2. A player character does not automatically know their existence, name, hierarchy, beliefs, or abilities. Knowledge requires a plausible source or an actual encounter.
3. Do not populate either group with invented named NPCs, extra elders, disciples, allies, enemies, or branches and then present them as established canon.
4. Do not infer that Ancient Shamanism is aligned with evil because it borrows cursed-spirit power, or that the Buddhist clan is omniscient or morally infallible because it is peaceful.
5. The two groups' relations with one another, the three major powers, other sects, and Ordo Suci Tianming remain unspecified.
6. Their established lore does not override cultivation, Martial Soul, combat, injury, or time mechanics. Use the relevant mechanics modules when resolving actions.
7. If an encounter requires an unspecified detail, keep it unknown or establish only the minimum provisional detail needed for that scene; do not silently convert improvisation into permanent world canon.
