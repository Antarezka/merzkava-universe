# Merzkava Universe — Ancient Artifacts and Relics

**Status:** Lore catalog and provenance rules.  
**Mechanics authority:** Artifact creation and Thinker checks are defined in `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md`; economic currency restrictions are defined in `core/10_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md`. This file records origins, known examples, and discoveries. It does not replace those mechanics.

## 1. Artifact Origin Categories

Merzkava Universe distinguishes three categories:

### 1.1 Relic
A Relic is an inheritance or object left by a deity. The definition establishes origin category only; a Relic does not automatically grant a known ability, divine authority, immortality, or a specific power tier.

### 1.2 Ancient Artifact
An Ancient Artifact originated during the golden age of civilization. Its creator, exact age, original purpose, current function, and limitations must be verified individually.

An object found in a ruin is not automatically an Ancient Artifact. Provenance requires supporting evidence.

### 1.3 Artificial Artifact
An Artificial Artifact is manufactured by a Thinker using materials, patterns, and formulations. Its production system and success-chance matrix are defined in `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md`.

These categories are distinguished by origin, not merely appearance or strength. A Relic is not automatically an Ancient Artifact or an Artificial Artifact.

### 2.2 Zun Jade
- **Category:** Currency; not a Relic, Ancient Artifact, or crafting material by default.
- **Appearance:** Octagonal coin made from Heavenly Meteorite, with intricate formations emitting white light.
- **Known material property:** The material is extremely old and hard; people of the current era cannot engrave or rework its surface.

### 2.3 Ancient Zun
- **Category:** Currency; not a Relic, Ancient Artifact, or crafting material by default.
- **Appearance:** Hexagonal coin made from an ancient heavenly material; its intricate surface formations emit a purer white light than Zun Jade.
- **Origin fact:** Its source material appeared only once when the Heaven Realm opened in the distant past.
- **Known material property:** Current-era people cannot engrave, alter, or process its surface; the original material cannot be reproduced from a known later source.
- **Use:** Currency only.
- **Restrictions:** Cannot be used as a cultivation tool or as material to create an Artificial Artifact.

## 3. Unknown Artifact Inventory

No individual Relic or Ancient Artifact beyond the facts above has been fully specified in the currently established world canon.

This is an intentional empty catalog, not proof that no other artifacts exist in the world. During play, the GM must not invent a named Relic, its creator, powers, limitations, history, or ownership and present it as pre-existing canon.

New entries may be added when the owner defines them or gameplay establishes a discovery. Unconfirmed discoveries must remain hypotheses until verified.

## 5. Discovery and Knowledge Rules

- Separate the artifact's actual properties from what a character believes about it.
- A seller's description, rumor, appraisal, inscription, or faction record may be wrong or incomplete.
- Do not reveal an artifact's hidden function before the character has a valid way to discover it.
- Do not assign an effect merely because an item is rare, old, divine in origin, or high-tier.
- An artifact's tier alone is not a universal combat multiplier.
- Ownership, possession, access, activation, and mastery are different states.
- If an essential property is unknown, record it as `???` and follow the applicable mechanics.
