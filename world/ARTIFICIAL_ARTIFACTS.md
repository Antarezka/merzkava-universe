# Merzkava Universe — Artificial Artifacts

**Status:** Artificial Artifact reference and catalog.  
**Mechanics authority:** `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md` remains the sole source for Thinker progression, production requirements, and the official baseline success-chance matrix. This file organizes known artifacts and their documented functions.

## 1. Definition

An Artificial Artifact is manufactured by a Thinker using suitable materials, patterns, and formulations. Artificial Artifacts are distinct from Relics and Ancient Artifacts, which are categorized by their origins.

An artifact's tier is not a Spirit Realm or Spirit Ring tier. A high tier does not automatically mean an artifact is universally useful in combat; the documented function, conditions, limitations, ownership, and actual availability determine its use. If an artifact's tier is missing or disputed, use `core/TIER_ASSESSMENT_AND_APPRAISAL.md`: MAA's completed ruling is authoritative for the examined item when canon was uncertain; any GM estimate remains labeled provisional until then.

### Unidentified Artificial Artifacts

When an Artificial Artifact's identity or exact item details have not yet been established by canon or the GM, use the exact player-facing label `*Unknown* *membutuhkan appraisal untuk lebih pastinya*` in Inventory, Equipment, loot, and item descriptions. Do not replace it with `???` or invent a model, creator, function, tier, value, or provenance. Appraisal may establish only the facts supported by its actual findings; hidden functions remain hidden until legitimately discovered.

## 2. Established Technology Context

### 2.1 Tianhai Yunzhou
- The first Thinkers originated in Tianhai Yunzhou.
- Tianhai Yunzhou is a major center of Thinker knowledge and formulation development.
- A Tier 9 Artificial Artifact sustains the giant floating island at the center of Tianhai Yunzhou; its established power source is Tianhai Yunzhou-refined liquefied energy derived from specific imported energy-bearing stone/metal ores.
- Tianhai Yunzhou uses dedicated Artificial Artifact refining systems to process the ores and has a strategic monopoly on this refining method and the resulting standardized energy medium. The ore deposits themselves lie in other territories; named mines, contracts, exact chemical/spiritual composition, yields, prices, and reserve capacity remain unestablished.
- Tianhai Yunzhou also operates automated trains and other modern urban utilities powered by compatible Artificial Artifact engines using the refined liquefied energy. The technology is advanced but depends on maintenance, infrastructure, containment, trained personnel, and supply continuity.
- The island artifact's exact materials, internal mechanism, fuel consumption, maintenance procedure, redundancy, and weaknesses remain unknown.

### 2.2 Golden Dragon Empire
The Golden Dragon Empire participates in knowledge exchange with Tianhai Yunzhou. This can spread ideas and technology, but does not automatically give all Thinkers the same skill, materials, or production capability.

### 2.3 Beiming Gu and the North
The northern region contains ancient materials and resources that may be useful for engraving Artificial Artifact formations. Material availability alone does not guarantee successful production; the Thinker's grade, knowledge, formulation, and suitable processing remain relevant.

### AAF-006 — Emergency Area-Concealment Artifact
- **Tier:** Tier 8.
- **Category:** Artificial Artifact; mass-produced wartime emergency concealment device.
- **Historical context:** Deployed during the ancient world-calamity incursion when Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9 descended across the world.
- **Documented function:** Once activated, it conceals a vast area and disguises the life within it, reducing the chance that invading Demonic Beasts will identify and target that area. It did not guarantee protection, and many areas remained exposed to attacks.
- **Activation and lifespan:** One use only. The artifact destroys itself after activation and cannot be reused; this defect is part of its historical design.
- **Design origin:** The design framework originated with Bai Xuanyin, the late wife of Maharaja Ji Haoyuan and a Thinker Grade 10. A different Thinker Grade 10 in Tianhai Yunzhou copied that framework imperfectly. The mass-produced artifacts were several tiers lower in quality than a perfect reproduction of Bai Xuanyin's framework would have been. No perfect reproduction was achieved.
- **Deployment:** The devices were produced at scale. Thinkers from across the world, including most of Tianhai Yunzhou's Thinkers, traveled to many regions to activate them. Their effort saved many populated areas, but not every area was protected from the rampage of Tier 8–9 Demonic Beasts.
- **Historical legacy:** The artifact and its deployment are taught in academies as part of the world-calamity history, with Bai Xuanyin remembered as one of the great heroes who died during the catastrophe.
- **Unknown details:** Original framework's tier, the identity of the Tianhai Thinker Grade 10 who copied it, the number of artifacts made and activated, the exact radius and duration, materials, activation method, and complete technical formulation remain unspecified.

## 5. Formation Engraver Reference

The Formation Engraver is the specialized precision tool used to carve intricate formation patterns into Artificial Artifact base materials. Its general shape resembles a surgical scalpel, with an exceptionally fine working tip. The relationship between smaller patterns and greater technical difficulty is analogous to semiconductor fabrication: miniaturization and pattern density demand greater precision. This is an analogy, not a claim that the artifacts use modern computer-chip technology.

Manufacturing the tool is exceptionally difficult and requires a capable blacksmith/forger with Spirit Realm 5 or higher and relevant forging ability. The tool is expensive; exact prices, tip dimensions, quality grades, durability, and repair costs remain `???`.

For the authoritative requirements, limitations, and GM rules, see `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md`, §12. This catalog does not establish a separate crafting system.

## 7. Use in Roleplay

- A character cannot use an artifact solely because its existence is mentioned in lore; ownership, access, activation, and relevant knowledge must be established.
- An artifact's function must be relevant to the declared action.
- Do not treat the floating-island artifact as an unrestricted power source or portable combat weapon.
- Do not assume a destroyed, missing, inaccessible, or unknown artifact is available.
- Artifact creation, transfer, damage, repair, and activation must have a valid cause and be reflected in the session state where relevant.
- If an artifact's properties are not documented, keep them unknown instead of improvising canon. For a missing or disputed tier, use the universal assessment/appraisal authority and do not alter an existing official tier without an applicable canon revision.

### AAF-002 — Tianhai Yunzhou Liquefied-Energy Refining Apparatus
- **Tier:** Varies by facility; exact tiers not established.
- **Type / function:** Processes specific imported energy-bearing stone/metal ores into a stable liquefied energy medium for compatible Artificial Artifact engines.
- **Creator / Thinker:** Not established; the refining tradition is maintained by Tianhai Yunzhou specialists and the Tianhai Thinker Institute.
- **Materials:** Imported energy-bearing ore plus facility-specific components; exact recipes and ratios are restricted/unknown.
- **Energy source:** Raw energy-bearing ore is the feedstock; processing requires a functioning refining apparatus and trained operators.
- **Operating limits:** Only suitable ore classes and compatible apparatus can be processed. Not every stone or metal can be refined, and the resulting energy is not automatically compatible with every artifact.
- **Strategic status:** Tianhai Yunzhou controls the known industrial refining method and distribution standards, giving it a major export advantage. This is a monopoly over the established refining capability, not ownership of every source of raw ore.
- **Current location:** Tianhai Yunzhou; individual facilities and their security arrangements are not catalogued here.
- **Weaknesses / failure conditions:** Supply interruption, apparatus damage, contamination, containment failure, and loss of specialist operators can reduce output. Exact failure modes remain unknown.

- **History:** Established as a core part of Tianhai Yunzhou's modern urban transport infrastructure. Do not infer exact speed, capacity, fares, frequency, or safety guarantees without a specific route record.


### AAF-004 — Tianhai Yunzhou Long-Distance Liquefied-Energy Passenger Aircraft
- **Tier:** Exact tier is not established; the transport class uses Artificial Artifact propulsion designed for long-distance passenger service.
- **Type / function:** High-capacity long-distance passenger aircraft operated through Tianhai Yunzhou's Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal. It carries more passengers per journey than an ordinary Qi-powered flying ship.
- **Energy source:** Tianhai Yunzhou-refined liquefied energy derived from imported energy-bearing stone/metal ores, held in a compatible dedicated energy vessel.
- **Propulsion distinction:** Uses an Artificial Artifact engine powered by liquefied energy; it does not use cultivator Qi as its primary propulsion source and is not simply a renamed conventional flying ship.
- **Established service corridors:** Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal in the Golden Dragon Empire ↔ Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal in Tianhai Yunzhou; Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal in Beiming Gu ↔ Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal in Tianhai Yunzhou.
- **Approved passenger fare:** 25 xc per passenger for a round trip on this service. A comparable round trip on an ordinary Qi-powered flying ship costs 86 xc per passenger. These are approved fare baselines; extraordinary disruptions, special classes, cargo charges, taxes, or discounts are not established.
- **Operating limits:** Requires dedicated aircraft, compatible liquefied energy, trained crew and technicians, maintenance, passenger processing, and an operating transit terminal. Flights may be suspended by weather, security, maintenance, or fuel-supply disruption.
- **Unknown details:** Exact tier, aircraft model, seating capacity, speed, travel time, timetable, fleet size, ticket class structure, operators/owners, maintenance cycle, and emergency procedures remain unestablished.
- **Canon boundary:** The existence of the transport class, two corridors, comparative capacity, energy source, and round-trip fares is established. Do not invent precise specifications or assume routes to other cities exist.


### AAF-005 — Portable Spirit Ring Stasis Artifact (Class Reference)
- **Tier / model:** Not established; this is a known artifact class, not one uniquely catalogued specimen.
- **Type / function:** A compact, expensive Artificial Artifact that places a Spirit Ring into a stable stasis state so the ring can be transported and absorbed at another location rather than forcing absorption immediately at the point where the Spirit Beast was defeated.
- **Physical form:** Designed to be pocket-sized or similarly portable; exact dimensions, storage capacity, shelf life, activation method, compatibility limits, and failure conditions remain unestablished.
- **Availability:** Rare and expensive. Known access is normally associated with elite academies, military institutions, and other major powers or high-resource organizations. It is not ordinary equipment available to every cultivator.
- **Mechanics boundary:** Stasis does not itself grant ownership of a Spirit Ring, guarantee safe absorption, remove compatibility requirements, change the ring's age or nature, or bypass core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md. The ring, artifact, custody, transport, and eventual absorption must each be established and resolved correctly.
- **Creator / formulation / exact price:** Unknown. Do not invent a tier, fee, universal storage limit, guaranteed preservation duration, or success bonus.

This portable stasis artifact class is known to appear in high-end Lawless Territories auctions, but an auction listing does not imply the item is genuine, functional, legally obtained, or available at every auction.
