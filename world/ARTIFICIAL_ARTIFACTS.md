# Xuanling Realm — Artificial Artifacts

**Status:** Artificial Artifact reference and catalog.  
**Mechanics authority:** `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md` remains the sole source for Thinker progression, production requirements, and the official baseline success-chance matrix. This file organizes known artifacts and their documented functions.

## 1. Definition

An Artificial Artifact is manufactured by a Thinker using suitable materials, patterns, and formulations. Artificial Artifacts are distinct from Relics and Ancient Artifacts, which are categorized by their origins.

An artifact's tier is not a cultivation Realm or Spirit Ring tier. A high tier does not automatically mean an artifact is universally useful in combat; the documented function, conditions, limitations, ownership, and actual availability determine its use.

## 2. Established Technology Context

### 2.1 Tianhai Yunzhou
- The first Thinkers originated in Tianhai Yunzhou.
- Tianhai is a major center of Thinker knowledge and formulation development.
- A Tier 9 Artificial Artifact sustains the giant floating island at the center of Tianhai Yunzhou.
- The island artifact's maker, materials, energy source, internal mechanism, maintenance, and weaknesses remain unknown.

### 2.2 Golden Dragon Empire
The Empire participates in knowledge exchange with Tianhai Yunzhou. This can spread ideas and technology, but does not automatically give all Thinkers the same skill, materials, or production capability.

### 2.3 Beiming Gu and the North
The northern region contains ancient materials and resources that may be useful for engraving Artificial Artifact formations. Material availability alone does not guarantee successful production; the Thinker's grade, knowledge, formulation, and suitable processing remain relevant.

## 4. Production Rules — Reference Only

Use `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md` for all production mechanics. In particular:
- Thinker Grade and Cultivation Realm progress independently.
- Thinker Grade establishes the normal capability ceiling for artifact production.
- The baseline success-chance matrix must not be duplicated or altered here.
- Production requires a suitable formulation, materials, patterns, concentration, mental capability, stamina/energy, and sufficient Thinker Grade.
- Situational modifiers and a formal random-resolution method remain undefined unless the mechanics source is updated.
- The GM must not invent numeric bonuses, costs, durations, material substitutions, or exceptions.

This file is a catalog and lore reference, not a second crafting system.

## 6. Use in Roleplay

- A character cannot use an artifact solely because its existence is mentioned in lore; ownership, access, activation, and relevant knowledge must be established.
- An artifact's function must be relevant to the declared action.
- Do not treat the floating-island artifact as an unrestricted power source or portable combat weapon.
- Do not assume a destroyed, missing, inaccessible, or unknown artifact is available.
- Artifact creation, transfer, damage, repair, and activation must have a valid cause and be reflected in the session state where relevant.
- If an artifact's properties are not documented, keep them unknown instead of improvising canon.
