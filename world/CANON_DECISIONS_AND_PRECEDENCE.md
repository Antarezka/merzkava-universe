# Merzkava Universe — Canon Decisions and Conflict Precedence

**Status:** Binding continuity rule. Owner-confirmed decisions recorded here are final until the owner explicitly revises them.
**Purpose:** Prevent future AI/GM sessions from treating a settled decision as an open question, expanding institutions by assumption, or letting a newer but accidental contradiction silently overwrite established canon.

## 1. Rules for Resolving Conflicts

Apply these rules before inventing, narrating, or updating world facts:

1. **Explicit owner-confirmed decisions take precedence.** A decision explicitly confirmed by the owner in this file or in a clearly designated canonical module remains binding. Do not reopen it as a suggestion merely because another arrangement seems more realistic.
2. **Specific mechanics files govern their own mechanics.** For example, awakening charges and token rules belong to `core/10_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md` and `core/MARTIAL_SOUL_AWAKENING.md`; Guild contract rules belong to `world/SPIRIT_HUNTERS_GUILD.md`; academy mechanics belong to `core/CULTIVATOR_ACADEMIES.md`.
3. **The Index is navigation, not a replacement for the referenced canon.** Follow its mandatory loading order and fetch the specific authority for the subject.
4. **A newer file does not automatically win merely because its timestamp or commit is newer.** A new file or passage must be reconciled with established canon. If it appears to conflict, preserve the established decision and either correct the conflicting passage or mark the issue for owner review. Only an explicit owner revision or clearly stated superseding rule changes canon.
5. **Do not infer additional institutions or branches.** A role, regional identity, population size, or institution name does not prove that another academy, branch, office, facility, or jurisdiction exists.
6. **Keep confirmed facts distinct from proposals, estimates, rumors, and unresolved details.** Do not silently promote a suggestion or a plausible extrapolation into binding canon.
7. **When editing, update all dependent references.** If a final decision changes, revise the authoritative module first, then synchronize summaries, indexes, regional modules, and templates. Do not leave stale contradictory text behind.

## 1A. Binding Territorial Loading Rules — All Major Powers

These rules apply to Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, Beiming Gu, and—where relevant—the Lawless Territories:

1. Before narrating local facts, answering a lore question, auditing a region, or editing regional data, follow the `Mandatory Territorial Canon Load Path — All Major Powers` in `INDEX.md`. Read the current canonical sources relevant to the task; prior memory or summaries do not replace them.
2. The relevant regional module governs established local facts within its scope, alongside any more specific binding canon. Read linked authority files for government, institutions, factions, economy/services, routes, and established NPCs when those subjects matter.
3. Never transfer a power's administrative structure, counts, institutional network, customs, prices, or social rules to another power by analogy. A fact confirmed for Golden Dragon Empire is not automatically true of Tianhai Yunzhou or Beiming Gu, and vice versa.
4. Regional modules explicitly marked as scaffolds or owner-fillable are incomplete by design. Treat blank/pending fields as unconfirmed. Do not present invented settlements, routes, officials, prices, customs, institutions, or NPCs as already established canon. Any new permanent detail must follow the owner's approval/canon workflow.
5. Travel connections must be verified from `world/LOCATION_AND_ROUTE_NETWORK.md` plus the relevant origin/destination regional modules and `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`. A missing route is not proof of a direct connection and is not proof that travel is impossible.
6. If a required source cannot be accessed, disclose the missing source and limit claims accordingly. Never claim to have read or reconciled files that were not actually fetched.

## 2. Binding Decisions — Golden Dragon Empire

### 2.1 Exactly Two Cultivator Academies

The Golden Dragon Empire has **exactly two named central boarding Cultivator Academies in established canon**:

- Imperial Foundation Academy — Huanglongcheng, Longyuan.
- Imperial Cultivation Academy — Huanglongcheng, Longyuan.

Do not add regional academies, prefectural academies, or village academies as established institutions unless the owner explicitly changes this decision. **Tianfeng (Scholarly Heights) is a place associated with the birth and cultivation of talented people and a source of gifted students for the two academies; it is not a third academy and does not imply a separate regional academy network.** Talented people from Tianfeng and other regions may travel to Huanglongcheng to receive education at the two central academies. Local tutoring, family instruction, mentors, dojos, and self-study are possible learning routes, but must not be mislabeled as additional Cultivator Academies.

The two academies follow established foundational cultivation education. They prioritize formal knowledge and accepted methods concerning Martial Souls, Spirit Realms, Spirit Beasts, Spirit Rings, safe training, and foundational combat. This institutional curriculum must not be automatically classified as Soul-Nyuma unless its doctrine is explicitly established; nor should Soul-Zoun, experimental, or coercive methods be assigned to them without explicit canon.

### 2.2 Grand Martial Soul Awakening Hall and Public Awakening

The **Grand Martial Soul Awakening Hall exists once in each of the three major powers**—Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. Each power has exactly one Hall, located only at that power's established principal civilizational center. These are three power-level institutions sharing the same institutional identity, not a network of branches: do not place additional Halls in secondary cities, districts, prefectures, villages, or other regional centers. Follow each power's regional and governance modules for its established center; do not invent a new capital or center to accommodate the Hall. The Lawless Territories have no additional Hall by default.

Generally once every two years after New Year, the Hall sends designated awakening officials/practitioners on scheduled public visits to villages according to local quotas and schedules. On this public route, the Imperial subsidy pays the awakening service fee, service tax, and documentation. The individual pays **only the official proof-token fee of 10 xs**. The independent paid route has different charges; use `core/10_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md` and `core/MARTIAL_SOUL_AWAKENING.md` for the exact formula. Do not make public awakening wholly free of the token fee, charge the recipient the subsidized service costs again, or invent additional fees. If the person cannot pay the token fee, follow the existing external-lender/debt-note rules; the Golden Dragon Empire and Hall are not the lender.

The biennial schedule is a general public-service cycle, not a guarantee that every village is visited on the same date or that a visit is never delayed. Use an established local schedule, quota, event, or travel constraint when one exists; otherwise narrate the visit as a scheduled service rather than inventing a permanent local Hall branch. Awakening reveals a Martial Soul; it does not automatically grant a Spirit Ring, a high Spirit Realm, academy admission, employment, or a particular rarity.

### 2.3 Spirit Hunters Guild — Broad Contract Guild

The name **Spirit Hunters Guild** must not cause the AI to limit its work to Spirit Beast hunting. It is a broad contract-and-jobs Guild whose boards may offer escort, cargo transport, searches for items, reconnaissance, retrieval, protection, verified information gathering, Spirit Beast tracking/hunting, and other explicitly described work. Temporary contract registration and mixed parties are established features. The name remains as written unless the owner explicitly renames the organization; its practical scope is defined by `world/SPIRIT_HUNTERS_GUILD.md`.

Do not assume the Guild is a universal police force, a world government, a sect, or a branch network present in every settlement. Its local reach, available contracts, staff, fees, and authority depend on established local canon and events.

### 2.4 Official Villages and Local Settlements — Binding Count

The Golden Dragon Empire has exactly **13 officially registered named rural villages**. The canonical names, classification, and regional distribution are governed by `world/GOLDEN_DRAGON_EMPIRE_ADMINISTRATIVE_ATLAS.md`. The atlas separately lists **21 named hamlets/local settlements**; these support local exploration and geography but are not official villages and must not be included in the official-village count.

All seven `world/REGION_*.md` modules must remain consistent with the atlas. When an older or secondary file uses a conflicting count or classification, do not treat the larger list, newer timestamp, or a heading as authority. Preserve useful local places under their correct settlement status and correct the inconsistent reference. Only an explicit owner-approved canon revision may change the official count or promote a hamlet to an official village; such a revision must update the atlas first and synchronize every affected regional module, INDEX routing note, and dependent reference. Until then, **13 official villages + 21 separately classified named hamlets/local settlements** is the binding baseline.

### 2.5 Azerion Forest, Dead Zone, and Unexplored Ruins — Binding Canon

- **Azerion Forest** is the name of the whole forest. **Dead Zone** is the human nickname for its deepest inner/core zone only; never use it as an alternate name for the entire forest.
- Azerion Forest is a major Spirit Beast habitat and principal human hunting ground for compatible Spirit Rings. Its central area contains an enormous lake deeper than one of the world's oceans. Rocks on the lakebed mutated from direct exposure to the power of the ancient Heavenly Beast.
- An entrance within the lake leads to underground ruins and the **Mega Colossal Labyrinth**, whose extent is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire. It is unsuitable for beginners, and its exit connects to the sea on the northern deep-sea coast. The full internal layout and routes remain unknown.
- Inner Azerion is enclosed by extremely dense illusion fog. Only veterans who frequently enter Azerion Forest and local hunter tribes know safe routes into the city; others risk being lost in the fog indefinitely.
- A ruin never explored has no pre-existing complete map. Maps must be based on areas actually surveyed and recorded in play; do not invent a full map of an unexplored ruin.
- Hidden treasure may exist deep inside a ruin, but entry does not guarantee finding it. Where a hidden treasure chamber exists, its trigger conditions must be discovered and fulfilled; do not disclose the conditions prematurely or open the chamber automatically.
- Preserve continuity for explored routes, clues, hazards, mechanisms, and trigger conditions. The labyrinth's scale does not establish that every corridor connects or that its exit is easy to reach.

### 2.6 World-Calamity Incursion, World Knowledge Tower Alliance, and Ruined Archiver — Binding Canon

- In the distant past, anomalies that deviated far beyond safe limits opened gates to other worlds. The sky became a passage through which Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9 descended across the world, creating an apocalyptic-scale threat. The Great Tianhai War and Bai Xuanyin's death were part of this wider catastrophe; the three Tier 9 Demonic Beasts that attacked Tianhai Yunzhou were one major front, not the entirety of the world-spanning incursion.
- Ordo Suci Tianming's world-survival mandate is to help the world detect dangerous anomalies early. Ordo Suci Tianming jointly manages the World Knowledge Tower with an alliance of elite envoys from the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. The elite group may be called upon to seal or destroy a confirmed dangerous anomaly. The alliance's formal name, full roster, and detailed internal structure remain unspecified.
- The Tower remains an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age. The Tower administration council handles relic access, protection, upkeep, and authorized study; the Shen family is one of several parties with rights and responsibilities. The Ordo and the alliance cooperate on world-threat monitoring and response without becoming the Tower's owner or acquiring universal civil authority.
- Ji Haoyuan was an active World Knowledge Tower member during the catastrophe and was away from Tianhai Yunzhou when Bai Xuanyin died. He learned of her death while still on duty and resolved to end active service, but completed urgent containment obligations until the immediate threat was suppressed and the remaining Demonic Beasts had become scattered across the world; only then did he return to Tianhai Yunzhou. Do not invent the exact time he received the news or details of his private reaction.
- During the catastrophe, an Arcanist Grade 10 developed a Tier 8 mass-produced one-use area-concealment Artificial Artifact based on the original design framework created by Bai Xuanyin. The Tianhai Yunzhou Arcanist Grade 10 copied her framework imperfectly; no perfect reproduction was achieved. Arcanists worldwide, including most of Tianhai Yunzhou's Arcanists, activated the devices to conceal populated areas from invading Demonic Beasts. The devices self-destructed after one activation, saved many areas but not all, and are part of history taught in academies. See `world/ARTIFICIAL_ARTIFACTS.md` and `world/HISTORY.md`.
- Some high-level criminal organizations in the Lawless Territories exploited the catastrophe's chaos. Their specific identities and actions remain intentionally undefined; do not invent the details until the owner develops them.
- Beiming Han helped suppress the catastrophe and subdued one of the invading Demonic Beasts. He purified its mind, after which its existence rose to become the rank-9 Heavenly Beast that later dwelled in Azerion Forest. It is the colossal dragon with a deer-like head, branching antlers, and deep-blue eyes that left a scar on Han. Beiming Xenhwa is among the very few who know this hidden history.
- Beiming Xenhwa is the daughter of Beiming Han's close friend, a white nine-tailed fox woman who died during the catastrophe. Han rescued Xenhwa and later adopted her. Her mother's name and exact death circumstances remain unspecified; this is restricted knowledge, not common public information.
- Ruined Archiver is an established specialist branch of Ordo Suci Tianming, with a branch in every city and matching light-brown uniforms. It registers expeditions, verifies Ruined maps and reports, pays fairly for reliable information even when no treasure is found, and keeps records highly confidential. Its full operating rules are in world/RUINED_ARCHIVER.md. Exploration experience counts officially only when the expedition was registered before entry; a staff officer accompanies the group only to the entrance gate.
- Ruined Archiver does not subordinate independent guilds and has no universal authority to ban access to Ruined. Recommendations and enforcement depend on local law. Do not treat every Ruined as anomalous, every anomaly as apocalyptic, or every Ruined as publicly listed.
- More than one thousand Golden Age Ruined are scattered around the world. Unexplored Ruined have no complete map in advance; hidden treasure requires discovery and fulfillment of its own trigger conditions. Mapped sites may have exhausted principal rewards and still provide ordinary finds or training opportunities where their actual hazards allow.

### 2.7 Festival Tiga Kekuasaan — Binding Recurring Event

- Festival Tiga Kekuasaan is a canonical recurring event held every **six in-world months (twice per year)**, hosted in turn by Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. It is not a monthly event.
- Its purpose is peaceful ties and cultural exchange among the three powers. The festival brings representation from the three powers and their known sects, clans, factions, and institutions; hidden mountain groups may be represented only when their existence/access is known or discovered in the story.
- Its grounds showcase regional goods and culture, including characteristic textiles and products, Artificial Artifacts, engravers, locally relevant Spirit Beast catalogs, regional food, clothing, customs, and differences in fighting styles. The three powers also present characteristic dances and other established performances.
- Friendly sparring is a headline event. Killing is prohibited; designated referees and festival security intervene against lethal escalation. Incidents are still resolved under the relevant official combat, injury, law, and faction rules.
- The night lantern release is especially associated with couples, but anyone may release a lantern as a prayer or expression of goodwill and hope. A wish has no automatic supernatural effect.
- The festival is a natural way to reveal broad world and regional context to players without forcing attendance or overwhelming a scene with exposition. Small or medium crimes, prejudice, cultural friction, and other tensions may occur when supported by circumstances; a major incident requires an established trigger. Do not force a crime into every edition.
- The exact next host, fixed rotation order, venue, date, duration, and detailed event program are not established by this decision. Resolve them only from a confirmed prior edition/calendar, relevant regional canon, or a later explicit owner decision.
- The event's canonical registry record and GM operating guidance are maintained in `world/DYNAMIC_EVENTS.md`. Do not duplicate edition-specific incidents here; register persistent outcomes through the normal event workflow.

### 2.8 Spirit Beast Above Seven Million Years — Anomalous World Rejection

- When a Spirit Beast whose **actual age exceeds 7,000,000 years** enters Spirit Ring conversion/absorption, the official AWR check in `core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md` §6.1 applies. This is a risk threshold, not an age-based guarantee that a gate will open.
- AWR is separate from the recipient's Spirit Ring rejection, absorption success, Sync cap, and permanent Martial Soul defect. Recipient preparation and voluntary handover do not erase AWR; age, Spirit Beast aggressiveness, and the calculated rejection level determine its probability under the official formula.
- AWR triggering does not automatically open a gate to another world. Any gate-opening event must be resolved as a separate anomaly escalation consistent with the established world-calamity history. Do not invent a gate-opening probability unless the owner establishes one.
- This decision does not establish that Spirit Beasts above seven million years are common. They remain exceptional stress cases; the 7,290,422-year Heavenly Beast in Azerion is a known example, not a population baseline.


### 2.9 Three Knowledge Disciplines and Spirit Realm Traditions — Binding Canon

Merzkava's three principal knowledge disciplines are **Arcanistry**, **Spirit Realm — Soul-Nyuma**, and **Spirit Realm — Soul-Zoun**. Arcanistry remains independent and is governed by `core/ARCANISTRY_AND_FORMULATION.md`; it is not a third Spirit Realm doctrine.

Soul-Nyuma and Soul-Zoun study the same complete Spirit Realm domain. Their defining difference is how they treat and process the soul:

- **Soul-Nyuma** respects the soul's continuity and intrinsic nature. It does not deliberately shorten the process, weaken or seal the soul for convenience, and allows the ordinary Spirit Ring formation process to complete before absorption. Resulting abilities tend to preserve the Spirit Beast's default characteristics and use Qi more efficiently and consistently.
- **Soul-Zoun** intervenes in the soul's process through methods such as process compression, weakening, sealing, suspension, or reconstruction. It may produce aberrant Spirit Ability characteristics that depart from the Beast's default nature and tend to require additional Qi for formation, maintenance, stabilization, or control.
- The two traditions are not moral categories. Soul-Zoun is not automatically evil, criminal, forbidden, demonic, or responsible for the ancient calamity. Soul-Nyuma is not automatically virtuous, infallible, or superior in every situation.
- Their methods do not create separate absorption rules or bypasses. A sealed, weakened, or suspended soul is not automatically a completed Spirit Ring; absorption requires a formed ring and must follow `core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md`.
- Institutions may teach either tradition, both comparatively, or shared foundational instruction. Their affiliation must be supported by actual curriculum, methods, records, or declared doctrine. Their judgments of the opposing tradition are local perspectives, not universal facts; members may disagree internally.
- The only recorded achievement in Merzkava's history of a Spirit Ring with full potential and a **100% Sync cap** was attained by an ahli Spirit Realm of Soul-Nyuma. This historical fact grants no automatic bonus or guarantee to Nyuma practitioners and does not permanently bar Soul-Zoun from future achievement.
- Illustrative 12%/19% Qi costs are examples, not a universal table. No fixed Qi multiplier, aberration tier, structural-collapse threshold, or tradition-based absorption modifier is established here.
- The former global four-part taxonomy Orthodox / Unbound / Forbidden / Supremacy is retired; do not map those labels onto the two traditions. Their surviving use must be explicitly local, historical, legal, religious, or factional.
- The ancient calamity involved catastrophic anomalies, but it is not the definition of Soul-Zoun and no causal responsibility is assigned to Soul-Zoun or its practitioners without separate canon evidence.

`core/SPIRIT_REALM_TRADITIONS.md` owns doctrine and classification. `core/CULTIVATION_SYSTEM.md`, `core/MARTIAL_SOUL.md`, `core/MARTIAL_SOUL_AWAKENING.md`, `core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md`, and `bestiary/SPIRIT_BEAST_RULES.md` retain exclusive authority over their respective mechanics.

## 5. Binding Decisions — Spirit Realm Traditions and Education

1. **Two traditions, one shared domain.** Soul-Nyuma and Soul-Zoun are the two comprehensive traditions for the same Spirit Realm domain. Soul-Nyuma respects soul continuity and intrinsic characteristics; Soul-Zoun intervenes in the soul's process and may produce aberrant characteristics.
2. **Spirit Ring process remains mechanically unified.** Their methods may differ before and during Spirit Ring formation and in the resulting manifestation, but neither tradition has a separate absorption formula, automatic success, Sync bonus, exemption, or bypass. `core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md` remains the sole mechanics authority.
3. **Power expression and Qi trade-off.** Nyuma abilities tend to preserve the Spirit Beast's default characteristics and use Qi more efficiently; Zoun abilities may deviate from those characteristics and tend to require additional Qi for aberrant formation, maintenance, stabilization, or control. These are qualitative tendencies, not universal numerical modifiers. The illustrative 12%/19% costs are not a global table.
4. **Institutions shape interpretation.** Academies, dojos, clans, sects, teachers, and research groups may adopt either tradition, compare both, or teach shared foundations. Their affiliation requires actual evidence in curriculum, methods, records, or declared doctrine. Their opinions of the opposing tradition are local perspectives and may include contempt, disgust, criticism, or claims of backwardness; they are not universal facts and do not make every member think alike.
5. **Historical Sync achievement.** The only recorded achievement of a Spirit Ring with full potential and a 100% Sync cap in Merzkava's history was attained by an ahli Spirit Realm of Soul-Nyuma. This is historical canon, not a mechanical bonus, universal guarantee, or permanent prohibition against future Soul-Zoun achievement.
6. **No invented numerical boundaries.** No universal aberration levels, structural-collapse thresholds, Qi multiplier, or tradition-based absorption modifier are established by this decision. Add them only through explicit owner approval and updates to the relevant mechanical authority.
7. **No retroactive faction assignment.** Do not assign existing academies, clans, dojos, sects, or named NPCs to either tradition merely because a new example resembles their name, region, reputation, or combat style. Illustrative names such as White Cloud Clan and Peerless Mountain Clan do not create new canon institutions.

The doctrine authority is `core/SPIRIT_REALM_TRADITIONS.md`. Education references are `core/CULTIVATOR_ACADEMIES.md` and `world/OPEN_LEARNING_PATHS_AND_WANDERING_INSTRUCTORS.md`. Mechanical authorities remain the dedicated Spirit Realm, Spirit Ring, Spirit Beast, Qi, and combat modules.
