# Merzkava Universe — Cosmology Framework

**Status:** Foundational framework. This file establishes the scope and vocabulary of the setting; it does not yet canonize individual divine realms, dimensions, primordial worlds, rulers, or travel methods.

## 1. Scope of the Universe

**Merzkava Universe** is the umbrella name for the complete cosmological setting. It includes the main world, divine realms, distinct dimensions, and primordial worlds. These are parts of one broader setting, but they are not assumed to be equally accessible, equally powerful, or governed by identical laws.

The existing **Xuanling Realm** world-lore files remain the current reference for the established main-world setting unless the repository owner explicitly renames or replaces that world's in-universe name. The repository name and the name of an individual world are separate concepts.

## 2. Cosmological Categories

| Category | Working definition | Canon boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Main World | The primary inhabited world covered by the existing world, geography, factions, history, and regional modules. | Use the current world modules for established facts. Do not infer that the main world contains the entire universe. |
| Divine Realms | Realms associated with divine beings, divine authority, or forms of existence beyond ordinary mortal society. | Their names, hierarchy, inhabitants, and access conditions remain unset until recorded in a dedicated canon module. |
| Dimensions | Distinct spaces or layers of reality that may differ from the main world in structure, laws, time, or access. | Do not assume every dimension is a separate universe or that travel between them is easy. |
| Primordial Worlds | Worlds associated with the earliest or most fundamental layers of existence. | Their origin, age, laws, inhabitants, and relationship to divine realms remain unset until established in canon. |

## 3. Rules for Consistent Worldbuilding

1. Do not treat the main world, a divine realm, a dimension, and a primordial world as interchangeable terms.
2. Do not invent names, rulers, creation myths, power rankings, portals, travel costs, or cosmological laws and present them as established canon.
3. A new location or realm becomes canon only after the repository owner approves and records it in the appropriate module.
4. Existing cultivation, Martial Soul, Spirit Beast, Spirit Ring, economy, combat, and character-state rules remain authoritative within their documented scope. Do not assume those mechanics automatically operate identically in every cosmological category unless the relevant canon says so.
5. A character only knows cosmological facts that they have learned through a supported source or event. The GM's access to repository lore does not automatically make that lore common knowledge.
6. When an action crosses between cosmological categories, identify the origin, destination, method, time cost, risks, and any relevant rule source. If those details have not been established, do not invent a definitive outcome.

## 4. Expansion Order

Develop the cosmology in this order to avoid contradictions:

1. Establish the relationship between the main world and the universe as a whole.
2. Define the nature and hierarchy, if any, of divine realms.
3. Define dimension types and the rules for entering or leaving them.
4. Define primordial worlds and their relationship to the other categories.
5. Record named locations, known routes, inhabitants, factions, historical events, and access requirements in dedicated modules.

## 5. Canon Status

This framework is official as a structural guide. The specific names, number, hierarchy, rulers, origins, and travel rules of divine realms, dimensions, and primordial worlds are **not yet established by this file**. Do not fill those gaps with invented facts during gameplay.
