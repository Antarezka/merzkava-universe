# Merzkava Universe — Energy-Bearing Raw Materials

**Status:** World resource reference. This module records the established dual economic and material context of certain energy-bearing stone/metal ores. It does not define cultivation, Spirit Ring, or Artificial Artifact production mechanics.

## 1. Scope and Established Context

Certain stone/metal ore deposits possess energy-bearing properties and occur in territories outside Tianhai Yunzhou, including supplier territories under the Golden Dragon Empire and Beiming Gu. These materials are relevant to two distinct fields:

1. **Natural-state applications:** The raw material retains value because of its inherent properties. It may be sought, studied, or used by qualified ahli Spirit Realm for matters connected to Spirit Rings and other specialist needs.
2. **Artificial Artifact energy supply:** Suitable ore can be processed through Tianhai Yunzhou's specialized refining apparatus into a stable liquefied energy medium for compatible Artificial Artifact engines.

These are separate value pathways. The existence of one does not establish the method, efficacy, or availability of the other.

## 2. Natural-State Value

- The unprocessed material has value beyond being industrial feedstock. Its inherent energy-bearing nature makes suitable raw specimens potentially relevant to specialist research and applications pursued by ahli Spirit Realm, including matters related to Spirit Rings.
- This establishes a field of demand and a reason to preserve, study, trade, or acquire the material in its natural state. It does **not** establish a universal effect, a guaranteed benefit, or a standard procedure.
- The exact natural properties, applications, handling requirements, compatibility, risks, limits, and degree of rarity remain unassigned until supported by owner-approved development or recorded findings.
- This section does not define or modify Spirit Realm cultivation, Spirit Ring formation, Spirit Ring absorption, Sync, absorption chance, AWR, defects, ring age, or any other Spirit Ring mechanic. Those matters remain exclusively governed by their existing authoritative modules. No mechanical inference may be drawn from the material's market value or from this broad natural-state reference.
- Do not assume every specimen, deposit, grade, or superficially similar stone/metal has the same properties. Identification and claims about a particular specimen require appropriate evidence and appraisal.

## 3. Tianhai Yunzhou Refining Pathway

- Tianhai Yunzhou controls the established specialized industrial method for processing certain imported energy-bearing stone/metal ores into a stable liquefied energy medium.
- The refined medium is intended for compatible Artificial Artifact engines and dedicated systems. It is not a universal fuel, and not every energy-bearing stone/metal ore is suitable for every refining apparatus.
- Refining depends on specialized apparatus, trained operators, containment, technical standards, and distribution infrastructure. The method and resulting standardized energy medium remain a strategic advantage of Tianhai Yunzhou.
- This is a monopoly over the established industrial refining capability and its distribution standards—not ownership of every raw deposit, nor proof that Tianhai controls every natural-state use of the material.
- Do not infer that a raw specimen can be refined locally by an ordinary Arcanist, that another power can reproduce Tianhai's industrial process, or that refined energy can power an incompatible artifact.

See `world/ARTIFICIAL_ARTIFACTS.md` for the established refining apparatus and compatible artifact context, and `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md` for the regional supply relationship.

## 4. Resource Supply, Trade, and Strategic Balance

The resource relationship is interdependent rather than a simple transfer of control:

- **Deposit territories and source holders** provide access to naturally occurring raw material. Their position is affected by actual deposit quality, output, reserves, ownership, extraction capability, local demand, alternative suppliers, and access to trade routes.
- **Tianhai Yunzhou** contributes the established specialized refining process, standardized liquefied-energy output, compatible infrastructure, technical expertise, and major distribution/logistics capacity.
- **Other buyers and specialist users** may value suitable material in its raw state for purposes distinct from industrial fuel production. This creates demand that does not necessarily pass through Tianhai's refining chain.
- Neither geological source access nor refining capability automatically gives one side absolute control. Bargaining power depends on the specific material, viable substitutes, stockpiles, supply continuity, processing capacity, route security, contracts, and the needs of each market.
- Do not invent named mines, exclusive ownership, fixed quotas, supplier contracts, reserves, production volumes, embargoes, or political arrangements unless separately established.

Tianhai's industrial advantage remains substantial because its refining method and compatible infrastructure are difficult to replace. At the same time, disruption to raw imports can affect its industrial and infrastructure systems. The existence of raw-state demand broadens the resource's economic importance without automatically reversing the established balance of power.
