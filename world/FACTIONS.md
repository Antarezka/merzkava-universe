# Merzkava Universe — Factions and Organizations

**Status:** Faction reference module.  
**Scope:** Established political powers, clans, dojos, and named organizations.  
**Authority:** `world/WORLD.md` establishes the world-level identities. This file organizes those entries; it does not invent current leaders, secret alliances, wars, or ownership. Binding continuity decisions and conflict precedence are recorded in `world/CANON_DECISIONS_AND_PRECEDENCE.md`.

## 1. Three Major Powers

### 1.1 Golden Dragon Empire
- **Type:** Centralized eastern empire.
- **Established strengths:** Large population, administration, agriculture, inland trade, industry, cultivation academies, clans, and dojos.
- **Ruler:** Emperor of the Golden Dragon Empire.
- **Martial Soul:** Golden Heavenly Dragon.
- **Spirit Realm:** Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Middle Stage.
- **Political limits:** Vast territory creates administrative, security, border-control, and corruption challenges. Clan loyalty is not automatically absolute.

### 1.2 Tianhai Yunzhou
- **Type:** Maritime power centered on a giant floating island.
- **Established strengths:** Maritime trade, navigation, defense, advanced Thinker knowledge, and a Tier 9 Artificial Artifact that sustains the island.
- **Ruler:** Maharaja of Tianhai Yunzhou.
- **Martial Soul:** Heavenly Sky Whale.
- **Spirit Realm:** Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage.
- **Political limits:** The island artifact is a strategic asset; its existence does not mean the Maharaja can automatically use all of its functions as personal combat abilities.

### 1.3 Beiming Gu
- **Type:** Northern power protected by mountains, cold forests, valleys, highlands, and difficult routes.
- **Established strengths:** Defensive terrain, experience in harsh environments, local resource knowledge, and restricted access routes.
- **Ruler:** Leluhur Utara of Beiming Gu.
- **Martial Soul:** Blue-Flame Silver Tiger.
- **Spirit Realm:** 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage.
- **Established comparison:** Leluhur Utara is described as having a major advantage in direct individual duels against the other two rulers. This does not guarantee victory in every context or make Beiming Gu automatically strongest as a state.

The three rulers' proposed ring configurations are documented in `world/WORLD.md`. Those configurations are labeled proposed and must not be treated as independently verified historical events.

## 2A. Imperial Special Financial Audit Committee

**Official name:** Komite Auditor Keuangan Khusus Kekaisaran  
**Type:** Specialized financial-audit institution of the Golden Dragon Empire.  
**Canonical institutional dossier:** [Imperial Special Financial Audit Committee](../regions/golden-dragon-empire/npcs/IMPERIAL_SPECIAL_FINANCIAL_AUDIT_COMMITTEE.md).

The committee's head is established as clean and honest. The integrity of the deputy and lower-ranking personnel is unknown. Corrupt practices or evidence manipulation are possible concerns, but no such act is confirmed merely by this possibility. Do not presume every member is corrupt, and do not treat the head's integrity as proof that every subordinate is clean.

The committee's exact role in the case, the charges, the evidence, and the identity of any responsible person have not been established. Keep suspicion, belief, and verified evidence separate; do not invent a conspiracy or a culprit.

The committee's precise statutory authority, office location, named officials, internal structure, case procedures, and current assignments remain unestablished. Refer to the linked dossier and the Golden Dragon Empire NPC registry for navigation; do not invent officials or active deployments.

## 6. Spirit Hunters Guild

The Spirit Hunters Guild is a broad contract-and-jobs Guild, not an organization limited to Spirit Beast hunting. Its contract boards may include escort, cargo transport, item searches, reconnaissance, retrieval, protection, verified information gathering, Spirit Beast tracking/hunting, and other clearly defined work. It supports temporary contract registration and mixed parties. The established name remains unchanged unless the owner explicitly renames it; its detailed scope, temporary registration, difficulty tiers, misconduct rules, blacklisting, and Deadpool bounty notices are maintained in `world/SPIRIT_HUNTERS_GUILD.md`.

The Guild is not a unified government or universal police force. Do not assume a branch, legal jurisdiction, permanent membership, uniform rules, or ability to enforce a bounty everywhere. Local presence and influence must follow canon or events.

**Established Tianhai Yunzhou presence:** the exact Guild desks and their local functions are maintained in `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md` §14.1. Treat them as local service points, not proof of uniform jurisdiction, permanent staffing, or authority over Tianhai Yunzhou's government, military, customs, or Lawless Territories.

## 7. Lawless Territory Organizations

Lawless Territories are not a single faction or unified state. The following groups are known by association with their respective areas; exact headquarters, leaders, membership, territory, and current alliances are not established here. The detailed service economy and neutral refuge network are recorded in world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md.

### Ashen Borderlands
- **Blood Vow Sect:** Blood oaths, contracts, and high-risk cultivation methods.
- **Ashen Bone Hall:** Body techniques and unconventional cultivation; some members pursue dangerous experimentation.
- **Black Scripture Sect:** Collection of forbidden techniques, seized texts, and restricted knowledge. It also operates appraisal services in parts of the Lawless Territories. Appraisers may deliberately exploit a customer's ignorance, but deception is a risk rather than a universal trait of every appraiser. Its holdings are not automatically complete or correct.
- **Hollow Fang Alliance:** A shifting alliance of aggressive cultivators, bounty hunters, and local factions, not a stable single sect. Some associated venues operate illegal sparring arenas with betting and prizes that can exceed those offered at sanctioned arenas in the three major powers; prize quality and availability vary by event.

### Black Tide Expanse
- **Dreadtide Sect:** Combat and cultivation adapted to maritime conditions, pressure, and surprise attacks.
- **Abyssal Moon Sect:** Dark-energy techniques and methods considered heterodox by orthodox schools.
- **Sea of Graves Hall:** Expeditions, dangerous sea routes, ruins, and trade in exploration finds.
- **Crimson Keel Brotherhood:** Sailors and cultivators involved in smuggling, piracy, and illegal escort work; not every cell has identical goals.
- **Crimson Sovereign Court (赤尊宫 · Chì Zūn Gōng):** A concealed power group operating above Crimson Keel Brotherhood. Its hidden leader is Ying Wuxin (嬴无心), Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Martial Soul Asura — Myth. The Court's influence from the shadows is comparable in reach to an empire, but it is not a fourth major power or a government of the Lawless Territories. Its membership, bases, chain of command, and control over individual Brotherhood cells remain unknown. See `world/NPC_YING_WUXIN.md` and `world/REGION_BLACK_TIDE_EXPANSE.md`.

### Nine Frost Wastes
- **Frozen Blood Sect:** Cold-energy and blood-based cultivation methods considered dangerous by many orthodox groups.
- **Black Frost Palace:** Fortified organization associated with freezing techniques, strong defense, and secrecy.
- **Corpse Lantern Sect:** Study of corpses, death energy, and remains of life; practices are taboo in many places.
- **Silent Maw Hall:** A discreet broker network connecting clients with independent assassins affiliated with the Hall. The Hall itself is an intermediary, not an organization whose members are automatically the assassins carrying out contracts. Its service follows a code of ethics; the standard revenue split is 60% to the intermediary and 40% to the contracted assassin. Individual assassin identities, contract details, and code enforcement remain compartmentalized. Concealment and low-profile operations support its brokerage work.

## 8. Medicine Hall Society

The Medicine Hall Society is a neutral medical network spread across the world. Its overall hierarchy, world leadership, and universal fee schedule remain unestablished. The central headquarters is in the Golden Dragon Empire; Tianhai Yunzhou also has an established local service network whose exact points and boundaries are maintained in `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md` §14.3.

The Society's neutral intent does not guarantee that neutrality can never be violated, that every point is fully staffed, or that treatment is free. Independent healers, family practitioners, and local herbalists may work outside the Society. Do not assume every healer belongs to it.

## 10. Faction Interaction Rules

- Do not invent faction membership, rank, secret identity, alliance, feud, or current orders as established fact.
- Faction descriptions are general tendencies and areas of influence, not rules that every member behaves identically.
- NPCs should act according to their established knowledge, personality, role, and current interests.
- Social reactions to a person's Spirit Ring configuration follow `world/NPC_MANAGEMENT.md` §6A: use only information the NPC can know, allow differences between ordinary observers and qualified experts, and apply regional outlook only where the NPC's actual training or background supports it.
- Faction reputation is not universal knowledge. A character must have a plausible source for knowing it.
- Political power, military strength, personal combat power, economic influence, and information networks are distinct.
- New factions or relationships may become canon through explicit owner approval or recorded gameplay, but must not be retroactively inserted into prior events.

## 11. Ordo Suci Tianming

**Type:** World-spanning religious organization.  
**Status:** Established organization concept; formal leadership and hierarchy remain undefined.

Ordo Suci Tianming is the world's largest religious organization by follower count in the current setting and is trusted by many powerful individuals and institutions. Its remit includes spiritual services and oversight of its own sacred sites, as well as receiving, verifying, recording, and coordinating responses to reports of high-level anomalies, walking calamities, and threats to the world's continued survival.

Its headquarters is the **Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows**, a dedicated religious precinct in the Wanluo Sacred Expanse. The complex is separate from the World Knowledge Tower. Its facilities include the Great Hall of Vows, World Observation Archive, Observation Tower, Threat Assessment Hall, Order Response Compound, and public pilgrim facilities. The full geographic and institutional description is maintained in `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md`.

The organization maintains a broad network of temples and religious houses, observation halls, assessment halls, response orders, and regional coordination centers. **Ruined Archiver** is an established specialist branch within this network; its full remit and operating rules are recorded in `world/RUINED_ARCHIVER.md`. Other branch names and internal structures remain unspecified.

The Ordo is responsible for helping the world detect dangerous anomalies early. Its early-warning work includes receiving, checking, recording, and escalating credible reports that may threaten the world's continued survival. This mission became essential after an ancient world-calamity in which extreme anomalies opened gates to other worlds and Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9 descended across the world. Ruined Archiver specifically gathers and verifies information from Golden Age Ruined, where an anomaly may be hidden.

Ordo Suci Tianming jointly manages the World Knowledge Tower with an alliance of elite envoys from the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. The alliance's members are an exceptionally powerful intervention group with a mandate to seal or destroy dangerous anomalies when required. Joint management does not make the Ordo or alliance the Tower's owner; the Tower administration council retains its defined responsibilities for access, protection, upkeep, and authorized study. The alliance's formal name, full roster, ranks, and detailed procedures remain unspecified. This threat-response arrangement does not replace the Tower administration council's separate responsibility for the relic's access, protection, upkeep, and authorized study.

The Ordo is not omniscient and does not automatically defeat every threat. Reports must be verified, and response can be affected by distance, delayed information, political interests, and limited resources. Its religious standing does not grant it absolute civil authority over Wanluo or universal law-enforcement power in every territory.


## 12. Tianhai Yunzhou Trade Associations

Tianhai Yunzhou has several independent trade and professional associations. They represent member interests and coordinate commercial services; they are not a single monopoly, government ministry, or authority over customs and harbor security. Their established local remits are:

- **Haiyue Merchants' Association** — merchant representation, commodity-quality disputes, and bulk-order coordination.
- **Cangqiong Shipowners and Freight Union** — freight coordination, cargo-handling standards, and shipping-risk information.
- **Linggong Artisans' Trade Association** — workshop-buyer links and procurement/repair coordination for craft and Artificial Artifact industries.
- **Misty Shoals Fishers and Pilots Association** — fishing-market information, pilot availability, and local-weather observations.

These associations may compete or cooperate. Their exact local placements, leaders, membership rules, fees, prices, exclusive rights, and formal agreements remain unspecified unless established in play or approved canon. Detailed locations, boundaries, and full remits are maintained in `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md` §14.2.
