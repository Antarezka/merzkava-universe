# Merzkava Universe — Factions and Organizations

**Status:** Faction reference module.  
**Scope:** Established political powers, clans, dojos, and named organizations.  
**Authority:** `world/WORLD.md` establishes the world-level identities. This file organizes those entries; it does not invent current leaders, secret alliances, wars, or ownership.

## 1. Three Major Powers

### 1.1 Golden Dragon Empire
- **Type:** Centralized eastern empire.
- **Established strengths:** Large population, administration, agriculture, inland trade, industry, cultivation academies, clans, and dojos.
- **Ruler:** Emperor of the Golden Dragon Empire.
- **Martial Soul:** Golden Heavenly Dragon.
- **Realm:** Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Middle Stage.
- **Political limits:** Vast territory creates administrative, security, border-control, and corruption challenges. Clan loyalty is not automatically absolute.

### 1.2 Tianhai Yunzhou
- **Type:** Maritime power centered on a giant floating island.
- **Established strengths:** Maritime trade, navigation, defense, advanced Thinker knowledge, and a Tier 9 Artificial Artifact that sustains the island.
- **Ruler:** Maharaja of Tianhai Yunzhou.
- **Martial Soul:** Heavenly Sky Whale.
- **Realm:** Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage.
- **Political limits:** The island artifact is a strategic asset; its existence does not mean the Maharaja can automatically use all of its functions as personal combat abilities.

### 1.3 Beiming Gu
- **Type:** Northern power protected by mountains, cold forests, valleys, highlands, and difficult routes.
- **Established strengths:** Defensive terrain, experience in harsh environments, local resource knowledge, and restricted access routes.
- **Ruler:** Leluhur Utara of Beiming Gu.
- **Martial Soul:** Blue-Flame Silver Tiger.
- **Realm:** Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage.
- **Established comparison:** Leluhur Utara is described as having a major advantage in direct individual duels against the other two rulers. This does not guarantee victory in every context or make Beiming Gu automatically strongest as a state.

The three rulers' proposed ring configurations are documented in `world/WORLD.md`. Those configurations are labeled proposed and must not be treated as independently verified historical events.

## 6. Lawless Territory Organizations

Lawless Territories are not a single faction or unified state. The following groups are known by association with their respective areas; exact headquarters, leaders, membership, territory, and current alliances are not established here.

### Ashen Borderlands
- **Blood Vow Sect:** Blood oaths, contracts, and high-risk cultivation methods.
- **Ashen Bone Hall:** Body techniques and unconventional cultivation; some members pursue dangerous experimentation.
- **Black Scripture Sect:** Collection of forbidden techniques, seized texts, and restricted knowledge. Its holdings are not automatically complete or correct.
- **Hollow Fang Alliance:** A shifting alliance of aggressive cultivators, bounty hunters, and local factions, not a stable single sect.

### Black Tide Expanse
- **Dreadtide Sect:** Combat and cultivation adapted to maritime conditions, pressure, and surprise attacks.
- **Abyssal Moon Sect:** Dark-energy techniques and methods considered heterodox by orthodox schools.
- **Sea of Graves Hall:** Expeditions, dangerous sea routes, ruins, and trade in exploration finds.
- **Crimson Keel Brotherhood:** Sailors and cultivators involved in smuggling, piracy, and illegal escort work; not every cell has identical goals.

### Nine Frost Wastes
- **Frozen Blood Sect:** Cold-energy and blood-based cultivation methods considered dangerous by many orthodox groups.
- **Black Frost Palace:** Fortified organization associated with freezing techniques, strong defense, and secrecy.
- **Corpse Lantern Sect:** Study of corpses, death energy, and remains of life; practices are taboo in many places.
- **Silent Maw Hall:** Small groups specializing in ambush, assassination, and techniques that suppress sound or presence.

## 7. Medicine Hall Society

The Medicine Hall Society is described as a neutral medical network spread across the world. Its detailed hierarchy, named branches, leadership, rules of neutrality, fees, and relationship to the three powers have not yet been established.

Do not assume every healer belongs to the Society, that its neutrality can never be violated, or that it offers free treatment.

## 8. Faction Interaction Rules

- Do not invent faction membership, rank, secret identity, alliance, feud, or current orders as established fact.
- Faction descriptions are general tendencies and areas of influence, not rules that every member behaves identically.
- NPCs should act according to their established knowledge, personality, role, and current interests.
- Faction reputation is not universal knowledge. A character must have a plausible source for knowing it.
- Political power, military strength, personal combat power, economic influence, and information networks are distinct.
- New factions or relationships may become canon through explicit owner approval or recorded gameplay, but must not be retroactively inserted into prior events.

## 8. Ordo Suci Tianming

**Type:** World-spanning religious organization.  
**Status:** Established organization concept; formal leadership and hierarchy remain undefined.

Ordo Suci Tianming is the world's largest religious organization by follower count in the current setting and is trusted by many powerful individuals and institutions. Its remit includes spiritual services and oversight of its own sacred sites, as well as receiving, verifying, recording, and coordinating responses to reports of high-level anomalies, walking calamities, and threats to the world's continued survival.

Its headquarters is the **Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows**, a dedicated religious precinct in the Wanluo Sacred Expanse. The complex is separate from the World Knowledge Tower. Its facilities include the Great Hall of Vows, World Observation Archive, Observation Tower, Threat Assessment Hall, Order Response Compound, and public pilgrim facilities. The full geographic and institutional description is maintained in `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md`.

The organization maintains a broad network of temples and religious houses, observation halls, assessment halls, response orders, and regional coordination centers. These are functional categories, not confirmed proper names of specific branches. Named leaders, formal ranks, doctrine, rituals, fees, membership rules, and individual combat capabilities remain unestablished.

The organization is not omniscient and does not automatically defeat every threat. Reports must be verified, and response can be affected by distance, delayed information, political interests, and limited resources. Its religious standing does not grant it ownership of the World Knowledge Tower or absolute civil authority over Wanluo.
