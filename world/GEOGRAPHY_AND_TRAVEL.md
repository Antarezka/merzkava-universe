# Merzkava Universe — Geography and Travel

**Status:** Official foundational worldbuilding module.
**Scope:** World orientation, wild regions, sky mountains, beast habitats, ruins, and transport.

**Connected world tree:** [`world/WORLD_NAVIGATION_TREE.md`](WORLD_NAVIGATION_TREE.md) maps this power into the shared three-power hierarchy and records established inter-power transit. Use it as navigation only; this file remains authoritative for its own subject.

## 1. Major Geographic Orientation

- Golden Dragon Empire lies in the eastern part of the world.
- Tianhai Yunzhou is a giant floating island above the southwestern ocean. The names of the surrounding seas and oceans remain `???` until officially named.
- Beiming Gu lies in the north.
- Large stretches of territory, wilderness, and difficult terrain exist between these powers. They do not share one continuous, fully governed corridor.

This is a relative geographic framework, not a scaled map. Exact coordinates, distances, borders, and travel times remain unknown unless established elsewhere.

## 2. Wild Regions and Sky Mountains

The spaces between major powers include forests, mountain ranges, deep ravines, valleys, remote basins, and high mountain ranges whose summits rise above the sea of clouds. These remote peaks shelter two distinct, exceptionally secluded traditions: the Ancient Shamanism clan/sect and a Buddhist clan. Their established lore, known hierarchy, powers, and encounter boundaries are recorded in `world/ANCIENT_SHAMANISM_AND_BUDDHIST_CLANS.md`. Exact mountain names, coordinates, and routes remain unestablished; do not place either group inside one of the three major powers without canon support.

## 3. Spirit Beast Habitats and Azerion Forest

Forests and other wild territories contain Spirit Beasts. As a general ecological pattern, deeper and harder-to-reach areas tend to contain higher-tier beasts because they are less accessible and more dangerous. This is a tendency, not a guarantee: a high-tier beast may roam outward, and an accessible area is not automatically safe. Do not invent a beast's exact tier or location without evidence, a bestiary entry, or a permitted provisional encounter design.

**Azerion Forest** is a vast, dangerous forest that extends from the Lawless Territories into Golden Dragon Empire territory. It is a paradise for Spirit Beasts and a major human hunting ground for finding compatible Spirit Rings. Humans call the deepest inner/core zone the **Dead Zone** and know not to enter it; Dead Zone is not another name for the entire forest. In the forest's central area lies an enormous lake deeper than one of the world's oceans. Its lakebed rocks mutated after direct exposure to the power of the ancient Heavenly Beast. An entrance in the lake leads to underground ruins and the **Mega Colossal Labyrinth**, whose extent is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire and whose exit connects to the sea along the northern deep-sea coast. The labyrinth is not suitable for beginners. The northern forest also contains **Inner Azerion**, a hidden half-beast city enclosed by extremely dense illusion fog. Only veterans who frequently enter the forest and local hunter tribes know safe routes into the city; others risk being lost in the fog indefinitely. See `world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md` for full canon.

## 4. Ancient Ruins

More than one thousand **Ruined**—ruins from the ancient Golden Age—are scattered throughout the world, including remote wilderness, mountain regions, and deep ocean trenches. Their locations, conditions, contents, dangers, and degree of exploration vary; many are not publicly listed. Other kinds of ruins may exist, but do not automatically classify every ruin as a Golden Age Ruined without supporting evidence. A Ruined's existence does not guarantee that treasure remains, that it is accessible, or that its history is understood. Consult world/RUINED_ARCHIVER.md for the specialist branch that registers expeditions and verifies Ruined intelligence.

### 4.1 Unexplored Ruins: Maps, Discoveries, and Hidden Rewards

- A ruin that has never been explored has no existing complete internal map. Do not fabricate or present a complete map as known before characters survey it. Maps may be built only from areas actually explored and recorded.
- Entering a ruin does not guarantee that a character will find its hidden treasure, even if they explore for a long time or reach a deep section. Some ruins contain a concealed treasure chamber in their deepest reaches.
- Such a chamber opens only when its specific trigger conditions are discovered and fulfilled. Those conditions may be unknown at the outset; do not reveal them without earned in-world evidence or trigger the chamber automatically just because the character reaches the final room.
- Exploration should preserve continuity: once a route, clue, hazard, mechanism, or condition has been discovered and recorded, do not secretly alter it to force success or failure. Keep confirmed facts distinct from a character's hypotheses and unverified rumors.
- A ruin may still be empty, inaccessible, dangerous, or unrewarding. These rules establish an exploration framework, not a guarantee that every ruin contains a treasure chamber.
- Ruined may be fully or partly mapped. A mapped site can serve as a training ground for exploration enthusiasts and early Spirit Realm experts only when its actual hazards and access conditions make that reasonable; mapped status does not mean safe.
- Some mapped Ruined have already had their hidden treasure chamber or principal rewards exhausted. They may still contain ordinary finds or provide exploration practice, and may be used for Spirit Beast hunting where the local ecology supports it.
- A site's map status, remaining-reward status, hazard status, and anomaly status are distinct. A site with exhausted treasure can still contain an anomaly; an unexplored site is not automatically the most dangerous.
- Ruined Archiver pays for reliable information about mapped, unmapped, or unexplored Ruined, even when an expedition finds no treasure. For registration, map verification, advance payment, balance payment, and confidentiality, use world/RUINED_ARCHIVER.md.

## 5. Common Transportation

- Horses and domesticated or trained beasts are common means of individual travel.
- Caravans may be pulled by land beasts; specialized caravans may also use flying beasts.
- Ships serve maritime routes.
- Conventional flying ships commonly use Qi as their propulsion source. A separate artifact-assisted flying-ship design also exists when powered by a **Tier 5 or higher Artificial Artifact of the Utilities type with a Levitation function**; this artifact threshold does not make all flying ships artifact-powered. The label “flying ship” alone does not establish its power source, capacity, fare, or route.
- **Tianhai Yunzhou's long-distance energy-aircraft:** Tianhai Yunzhou operates a separate high-capacity passenger transport class powered by its refined liquefied energy, not by Qi. Regular service hours are **05:00–21:00 local terminal time**. These Artificial Artifact aircraft use dedicated service corridors from Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal in the Golden Dragon Empire and Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal in Beiming Gu to Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal in Tianhai Yunzhou. Their approved standard round-trip fare is **25 xc per passenger**. When this service is closed, ordinary Qi-powered flying ships remain available **24 hours a day**, subject to normal access and operating conditions; a comparable round trip costs **86 xc per passenger**, substantially more than the energy-aircraft fare. Exact flight times, capacity figures, departure frequency, and fleet size remain unknown. These routes depend on Tianhai Yunzhou's specialized refining and distribution system and are not available everywhere.
- **Tianhai Yunzhou's urban transit:** Tianhai Yunzhou has automated trains running on dedicated guideways, powered by Artificial Artifact engines using the same specialized liquefied energy refined from energy-bearing stone/metal ores. This is a regional system, not a world-standard transport mode. It requires compatible engines, guideways, stations, trained maintenance staff, and reliable fuel distribution; only documented Tianhai Yunzhou corridors should be assumed to exist.
- **Tianhai Yunzhou's urban utilities:** Artificial Artifacts also support selected public infrastructure and industrial functions, contributing to a modern, technically dense urban character. Do not infer that every home or settlement has equal access to advanced utilities.
- Other transport systems may exist, but must be established in canon before being treated as standard.

Use `gameplay/TIME_AND_ACTION_RULES.md` for travel-time resolution. Transport type alone does not establish exact speed or travel duration.

## 6. GM Rules

1. Use this module together with `world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md`; do not invent precise coordinates or distances.
2. Keep sea names `???` until the world owner names them.
3. Treat higher-tier beast density in remote wilderness as a tendency, not an absolute rule.
4. Unexplored ruins have no pre-existing complete map. Keep maps limited to surveyed areas. Hidden treasure chambers, where present, require discovery and fulfillment of their trigger conditions; entry alone never guarantees treasure.
5. Do not treat flying ships or energy-aircraft as common household transport. Their existence depends on the specified technology, documented corridor, and passenger access.
6. Tianhai Yunzhou's automated trains, long-distance liquefied-energy aircraft, and refined-energy network are regional technologies; do not automatically place them in other powers or assume compatibility with arbitrary artifacts. The two named inter-power corridors are approved exceptions to the otherwise unknown route network.
7. The Ancient Shamanism clan/sect and the separate Buddhist clan are established hidden groups. Use `world/ANCIENT_SHAMANISM_AND_BUDDHIST_CLANS.md` for their canon. Do not invent their exact mountain coordinates, additional members, names, techniques, or connections beyond that file.

**Canon boundary:** This module records broad geography and travel concepts, not a complete world map, exhaustive bestiary, ruin catalogue, or transport specification.

## 7. Wanluo Sacred Expanse and Pilgrimage Routes

The Wanluo Sacred Expanse is a broad green plain surrounded by ancient forests and mountain ranges, with the World Knowledge Tower at its central landmark. The exact coordinates, dimensions, sovereignty, and relation to the three major powers remain unconfirmed. Refer to `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md` for its districts, religious precinct, and governance.

Roads from multiple directions converge on the Tower because generations of pilgrims and other travelers repeatedly used passable routes through mountain passes, valleys, forests, and river crossings. Tracks widened over time and supported rest stops, markets, scholarly institutions, and administrative services. This network was not established all at once by a ruler, and its convergence is not evidence that the Tower supernaturally created the roads.

Do not infer exact route lengths, travel times, safety, or adjacency from this description. Terrain may force roads to curve or pass through dangerous areas. The wider region is open to visitors under local rules, but entry into Wanluo does not imply access to restricted facilities or inner areas of the World Knowledge Tower.
