# Merzkava Universe — Golden Dragon Empire Administrative Atlas

**Status:** Owner-authorized canonical administrative register.
**Scope:** Seven major regions, twelve prefectures, six county circuits, and thirteen named rural villages.
**Population baseline:** approximately 320 million. The Empire's working area estimate is approximately 11.86 million km², estimated from the 320-million population baseline using region-specific average-density assumptions based on agricultural productivity, urban/port concentration, industrial settlement, frontier buffers, and wilderness. These density assumptions are planning inputs, not measured census data. This is a planning model, not a surveyed census or boundary claim; regional average density is not the same as city density.
**Authority:** This file is the single source for administrative counts, names, and parent relationships.

### 2.1 Population rhythm and route density

Use the following as a default scene guide, not a rigid timetable. Density changes by route type, season, weather, local events, security, and current story conditions. The GM may adjust it when the scene gives a reason.

| Local time | Main urban roads and market streets | Regional roads / caravan routes | Remote roads and frontier tracks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 05:00–07:00 | Low to moderate; vendors setting up, laborers and deliveries arriving | Low to moderate; early carts and caravans depart | Low; patrols, farmers, hunters, and occasional travelers |
| 07:00–09:00 | High to very high near gates, markets, docks, and schools | Moderate to high near towns and junctions | Low to moderate on established routes |
| 09:00–15:00 | Moderate; trade, errands, public business, and deliveries | Moderate; main travel and freight movement | Low to moderate; mostly purposeful travel |
| 15:00–19:00 | High to very high near markets, gates, docks, and residential districts | Moderate to high as workers return and caravans seek lodging | Low to moderate; daylight-dependent traffic |
| 19:00–23:00 | Moderate in commercial quarters, low in ordinary streets; night markets vary | Low to moderate near inns and relay stations | Low; occasional patrols, messengers, or travelers |
| 23:00–05:00 | Low, except guarded gates, night markets, docks, and entertainment quarters | Low; scheduled couriers or protected caravans only | Very low; isolated traffic and patrols, with greater exposure to hazards |

**Quick scale:** very low = rare passers-by; low = occasional individuals or small groups; moderate = regular movement with gaps; high = steady two-way flow; very high = congestion, queues, and delays. Market days, festivals, military movements, disasters, Spirit Beast alerts, rain, flooding, snow, and port arrivals can override the baseline. Density describes traffic, not safety or social status.

## 4. Six County Circuits

A county circuit is a broad administrative-service jurisdiction coordinating two prefectures, not a tiny district nested inside only one prefecture. Each circuit has one hub and coordinates records, petitions, courts/services where established, roads, and inter-prefecture administration.

| County circuit | Hub | Prefectures coordinated | Primary focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperial Heartland Circuit | Huanglongcheng | Huanglong, Jade Seal | Imperial records, petitions, capital roads |
| Riverlands Circuit | Qinghe | Qinghe, Jiangbei | Irrigation, river transport, granaries |
| Mineral Lands Circuit | Yumai | Jadevein, Ironcloud | Mining rights, ore routes, material levies |
| Scholarly Heights Circuit | Tianshu | Heavenly Script, Cloud Summit | Academies, examinations, archives |
| Furnace Mountains Circuit | Honglu | Red Furnace, Ashen Peak | Forges, fuel routes, worker safety |
| Frontier–Maritime Circuit | Beiguan | Northwatch, Eastern Star | Cross-region logistics, frontier and port coordination |

The Frontier–Maritime Circuit is a coordination arrangement, not proof of a direct road or short travel time between Baiyue and Xingluo. The administrative route framework is:

## 4A. Regional and Local Route Logic

Administrative routes are trunks, not a guarantee that every settlement connects directly to every other settlement. Follow the route graphs in each regional module. Local links may require a transfer at a village, ferry, caravan junction, mountain pass, or port. River depth, tides, seasonal floods, snow, landslides, patrol restrictions, and cargo type can close routes. A traveler must follow connected legs; same-region status does not mean direct access.

- **Longyuan:** Imperial Dragon Road links Huanglongcheng and Yuxi; spurs run through Foxbridge and Stone Mill, while Plum Lantern branches from the Zhuque road.
- **Canglan:** Twin River Route connects Qinghe and Jiangbei by river; canals and embankments require transfers.
- **Jinyue:** Ore Vein Road carries heavy cargo; mountain villages connect through caravan junctions.
- **Tianfeng:** Cloudstep Scholar Road links Tianshu and Yunding; upland villages require pack trails.
- **Yanling:** Furnace Supply Road links Honglu and Huifeng; fuel, kiln, and waterwheel villages use separate industrial spurs.
- **Baiyue:** roads converge on patrol posts and passes; Frontier–Maritime Relay is a staged land/sea link, not a direct road.
- **Xingluo:** coastal sailing legs and port roads connect hubs; some villages require small boats and tide-safe landings.

## 7. Residents, Services, and Routes

- Residents continue living in cities, market settlements, villages, hamlets, farms, ports, mines, academies, estates, and other established places. An unlisted place is not assumed empty or nonexistent.
- Household registers remain population-record units, not a separate government tier.
- **Ordinary imperial resident:** a person living under the Empire's civil jurisdiction; this status does not by itself confer noble rank, office, wealth, or cultivation level.
- **Household-registered resident:** a resident recorded in a household register for a recognized locality. Registration supports taxation, petitions, family records, and access to applicable public services; exact legal procedures remain subject to the wider law canon.
- **Temporary resident / traveler:** a person staying away from their registered home or passing through a locality. Temporary presence does not automatically transfer household registration or confer local office.
- **Unregistered, displaced, or undocumented person:** do not assume they are criminals or stateless. Their specific rights, registration process, and access to services depend on established law and local circumstances; details remain open until formally defined.
- Administrative consolidation alone does not change any resident's registration, legal status, rights, obligations, taxes, property, or social standing.
- Local heads, representatives, clerks, and magistrates may exist, but names and appointment procedures must be established in a regional or NPC module.
- Banks, medicine halls, academies, libraries, information exchanges, and government offices exist only where confirmed by the population-services register or a regional module. Do not place a branch in every prefecture or village by default.
- Administrative links establish responsibility, not physical adjacency, roads, exact distances, safe passage, or travel times.
- Consolidation alone does not change a resident's legal status, rights, obligations, tax burden, wealth, cultivation, or social standing. Those remain governed by existing canon.
