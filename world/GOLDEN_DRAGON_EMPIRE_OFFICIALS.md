# Golden Dragon Empire — Named Civil Officials and Political Dispositions

**Status:** Owner-authorized canonical NPC roster.  
**Scope:** Seven central portfolio heads, seven regional governors, all 46 prefects, and all 276 county magistrates.  
**Authority:** Use with `world/GOLDEN_DRAGON_EMPIRE_GOVERNANCE.md` and `world/GOLDEN_DRAGON_EMPIRE_ADMINISTRATIVE_ATLAS.md`. Territorial names and counts remain governed by the atlas.

## 1. Power ceiling and canon rules

- Emperor Jun Haotian remains the highest sovereign and is Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Middle Stage. **No official in this roster exceeds Spirit Realm 8 — Spirit Sovereign.**
- Regional governors are powerful political actors, not independent sovereigns. Their cultivation does not automatically give them military command.
- Spirit Realm and Stage indicate cultivation only; they do not guarantee victory. Martial Souls below are individual identities, not claims that the bearer has every possible ability associated with the creature or object.
- **Political disposition is a tendency, not a fixed alliance or proof of clan membership.** No character is automatically a secret ally, enemy, corrupt official, or loyalist beyond the traits written here.
- Each short background, motivation, Martial Soul, Spirit Realm, and stance below is established as a new worldbuilding addition. Private secrets, exact Spirit Rings, combat techniques, family trees, and crimes are not invented by this roster.
- Central officials: portfolios follow the existing governance file. County magistrates use the county-seat working names in the atlas.

## 5. Relationship and conduct rules

- **Emperor and central offices:** all seven central heads hold delegated portfolios; disagreement over methods is possible, but none is above the Emperor's sovereignty. The Ministry of Military Affairs administers and coordinates military matters; field command remains with the separate High Command.
- **Governors and prefects:** governors coordinate policy across their region. Prefects may resist a governor's proposal through lawful reports or petitions, but do not automatically command the regional military.
- **Prefects and magistrates:** prefects review cross-county matters and local administration; magistrates manage county-level civil work. Their political differences may produce negotiation, audits, obstruction, or cooperation depending on the facts and evidence.
- **Faction ties:** the roster deliberately does not assign every official to a clan. Shared region, Martial Soul motif, or professional focus is not evidence of kinship or secret allegiance. Specific clan ties must be added only if separately established.
- **Public accountability:** reformist or pro-people officials can still make mistakes or pursue self-interest; conservative or pro-stability officials are not automatically villains. NPC behavior should follow their written motives, available information, and consequences.
- **Administrative names:** the atlas remains authoritative for the seven regions, 46 prefectures, and 276 county jurisdictions. If a seat or jurisdiction is renamed, update this roster and the atlas together.
