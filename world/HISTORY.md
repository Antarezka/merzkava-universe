# Merzkava Universe — World History

**Status:** History framework with limited established chronology.  
**Canon policy:** This file records known historical facts and distinguishes them from events whose dates or details remain unknown. It deliberately does not invent a complete ancient timeline.

## 1. Historical Certainty

Merzkava Universe's current world canon includes:
- Three major powers: Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu.
- A world with a vast continent, connected seas, isolated northern territories, and Lawless Territories outside the direct jurisdiction of the major powers.
- A Thinker tradition that originated in Tianhai Yunzhou.
- Ancient artifacts from a golden age of civilization.
- Relics described as inheritances or objects left by deities, including the World Knowledge Tower, an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age.
- Ancient Zun currency associated with a singular appearance of an ancient heavenly material when the Heaven Realm opened in the distant past.
- A Medicine Hall Society with a neutral medical network across the world.

The exact dates, order of most events, founders, wars, dynasties before the current powers, and identities of ancient civilizations are not yet established.

## 2. Broad Historical Layers

These layers are an organizing framework, not a complete official chronology. Their exact dates and boundaries are unknown.

### 2.1 The Ancient Golden Age
An ancient civilization existed and produced artifacts now classified as Ancient Artifacts. Its full identity, political structure, technology, decline, and relationship to the current powers remain unknown.

Do not assume all ruins or unusual artifacts originate from this civilization. Provenance must be supported by evidence.

### 2.2 The Opening of the Heaven Realm
The economy system records that the unique material used for Ancient Zun appeared only once when the Heaven Realm opened in the distant past. The material cannot be reproduced from a known later source, and current-era people cannot process or engrave its surface.

The date, participants, cause, duration, and wider consequences of the opening are unknown. Do not invent them as established history.

### 2.3 The Rise of Thinker Knowledge
The first Thinkers originated in Tianhai Yunzhou. Thinker knowledge became a major source of Tianhai Yunzhou's cultural and technological influence. Knowledge exchange with the Golden Dragon Empire is an established channel for the spread of ideas and technology.

The names of the first Thinkers, the date of the first Artificial Artifact, and the exact stages of technological development have not been established.

### 2.4 The Formation of the Three Major Powers
The current world is organized around the Golden Dragon Empire in the east, Tianhai Yunzhou as a maritime power, and Beiming Gu in the north. Their governments, rulers, territories, and distinct strengths are described in `world/WORLD.md`.

The founding dates, founders, original borders, treaties, wars, and sequence of their rise remain unknown. Do not assign invented dates or claim a specific unrecorded war created the current borders.

### 2.5 The Current Political Balance
The three powers coexist without any one of them controlling the entire world. Their strengths differ: the Golden Dragon Empire has scale and mobilization; Tianhai Yunzhou has maritime reach and advanced Thinker knowledge; Beiming Gu benefits from difficult terrain and defensive geography.

This balance is not permanent or guaranteed. It can change through recorded events, leadership decisions, alliances, economic shifts, discoveries, and conflicts.

## 3. Historical Evidence and Provenance

When a character discovers a ruin, text, relic, artifact, inscription, or historical claim, distinguish:
- **Verified fact:** supported by a reliable source or a directly established event.
- **In-world claim:** what a person, faction, inscription, or tradition says; it may be incomplete or false.
- **Hypothesis:** a plausible interpretation that has not been confirmed.
- **Unknown:** no sufficient evidence is available.

An inscription is evidence that someone recorded a statement; it does not automatically prove the statement is true. A faction's version of history may reflect its interests or incomplete knowledge.

### HIST-001 — The Great Tianhai War
- **Date / period:** Unknown; occurred in the distant past.
- **Location:** A world-spanning incursion; one major battle was fought to defend Tianhai Yunzhou.
- **Status:** Confirmed historical catastrophe; exact chronology and many battle details remain unknown.
- **Participants:** World Knowledge Tower and its active members; Ordo Suci Tianming; representatives and forces from the three major powers; Beiming Han; multiple Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9. Three Tier 9 Demonic Beasts specifically attempted to invade and devastate Tianhai Yunzhou; their identities remain unknown.
- **What is established:** Anomalies that deviated far beyond safe limits caused gates to other worlds to open, with the sky acting as a passage through which Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9 descended across the world. The resulting incursion became an apocalyptic-scale threat. The World Knowledge Tower and its allies worked to contain the disaster. Beiming Han intervened and helped turn the tide. The Maharaja of Tianhai Yunzhou at the time, Ji Haoyuan, was an active World Knowledge Tower member and was away fulfilling that duty during the catastrophe. His wife, Bai Xuanyin (Spirit Realm 7, Thinker Grade 10), died in the Great Tianhai War. She is revered as a great heroine. Her major Artificial Artifact contributions include the world's strongest known weapon-type Artificial Artifact, armor still used by Tianhai Yunzhou soldiers, contributions to Tianhai Yunzhou's giant protective dome, and the original design framework behind a mass-produced emergency concealment artifact used during the catastrophe.
- **The Thinkers' worldwide rescue effort:** During the catastrophe, one Thinker Grade 10 developed a Tier 8 Artificial Artifact designed to conceal a vast area and disguise the life beneath it, reducing the chance that descending Demonic Beasts would target the sheltered area. The design was made and deployed at scale. Thinkers from across the world—including most of Tianhai Yunzhou's Thinkers—spread out across nearly the entire world to activate these artifacts and protect settlements and other inhabited areas. Among those who took part were Thinkers whose Martial Soul had awakened but who had not yet obtained any Spirit Ring; they could still contribute through their Thinker knowledge and the operation of these artifacts. Their intervention was among the largest direct contributions to humanity's survival. The effort saved many areas, but it did not protect every region from the rampage of Tier 8–9 Demonic Beasts.
- **The concealment artifact's flaw and origin:** The mass-produced Tier 8 concealment artifact was a one-use device that destroyed itself after activation; it was considered defective because it could not be reused. The Tianhai Yunzhou Thinker Grade 10 did not reproduce the original blueprint perfectly. They copied only the design framework created by the late Bai Xuanyin, Maharaja Ji Haoyuan's wife. The framework could not be replicated perfectly, and the resulting artifacts were several tiers lower in quality than a perfect reproduction of her original design would have been. Do not infer that a perfect copy was ever made, or assign the original framework a tier unless another canon source establishes it.
- **Public historical legacy:** Bai Xuanyin is taught in academies as one of the great heroes who died during the catastrophe. The concealment artifact and the Thinkers' global deployment effort are part of recorded history; her role as the originator of its design framework is recognized in that historical account. The effort is a lasting public example that Thinker expertise can save lives and protect settlements independently of a person's Spirit Ring configuration. This legacy is particularly prominent in Tianhai Yunzhou, the birthplace of Thinker knowledge, and among people who study Thinker disciplines, Artificial Artifacts, or the catastrophe. It can shape social respect for a known Thinker whose ring configuration appears weak, without making every NPC universally approving or establishing that every wartime Thinker lacked rings.
- **Aftermath:** The immediate apocalyptic threat was suppressed, but surviving or remaining Demonic Beasts became scattered across the world. Ji Haoyuan learned that Bai Xuanyin had died during the catastrophe while he was still serving the Tower. He resolved to end his active service, but remained on duty until the immediate threat was suppressed and the surviving Demonic Beasts had become scattered across the world; only then did he return to Tianhai Yunzhou. Beiming Han subdued one Demonic Beast from this calamity; after its mind was purified, its existence rose to become a Heavenly Beast. It later dwelled deep in Azerion Forest. The ancient Heavenly Beast's power mutated rocks at the bottom of Azerion's central lake.
- **Institutional consequence:** The catastrophe established the need for a lasting early-warning arrangement: Ordo Suci Tianming and its alliance of elite envoys from Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu jointly manage the World Knowledge Tower. The Ordo's Ruined Archiver branch gathers and verifies intelligence from Golden Age Ruined because an anomaly hidden inside a ruin may become a world-level threat if left undiscovered.
- **Criminal exploitation:** Some high-level criminal organizations based in the Lawless Territories exploited the chaos of the catastrophe. The specific organizations, methods, victims, and outcomes have intentionally not yet been developed; do not invent those details until they are defined.
- **Sources / evidence:** world/FACTIONS.md; world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md; world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md; regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/JI_HAOYUAN.md; regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/BAI_XUANYIN.md; regions/beiming-gu/npcs/BEIMING_HAN.md; world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md.
- **What remains unknown:** Exact date and duration; the full count and identities of the invading beasts; the exact sequence of battles; the formal name and complete roster of the Tower alliance; the complete list of anomalies that opened the gates; the precise moment Ji Haoyuan learned of Bai Xuanyin's death; and the name and further capabilities of the Demonic Beast that became the Heavenly Beast.

## 5. Timeline Rules for the GM

- Do not invent historical dates, founders, wars, treaties, rulers, collapses, or dynasties.
- Do not convert a provisional worldbuilding idea into confirmed history without approval.
- Do not treat a legend, rumor, artifact inscription, or NPC claim as omniscient truth.
- Current characters know only what they plausibly learned. The GM may track hidden facts, but must not reveal them without a valid in-world discovery.
- If a new event changes regional borders, faction status, leadership, or artifact ownership, record its consequences consistently.
- Historical time and the current gameplay calendar are separate unless an official source links them.
- Where the chronology is unknown, preserve `???` rather than filling gaps with invented dates.

## 6. World Knowledge Tower and the Growth of Wanluo

The World Knowledge Tower is established as an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age. The identity of its makers, the precise date of its origin, the details of its original purpose, and the full sequence of events surrounding it remain unknown.

The surrounding Wanluo Sacred Expanse developed over generations as pilgrims and other travelers repeatedly converged on the Tower. Its roads, rest sites, and settlement districts grew from sustained use rather than a single known founding plan. The exact chronology, first pilgrimage, founders of the settlements, and date at which the region acquired its current name are not established.

Ordo Suci Tianming and its headquarters, the Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows, are part of the current worldbuilding for the religious institutions associated with Wanluo. Their founding date, founders, formal doctrine, and leadership history remain unestablished. The Ordo's established world-survival mandate is to help the world detect dangerous anomalies early. After the ancient world-calamity incursion, it works with the World Knowledge Tower and an alliance of elite envoys from the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. The alliance's formal name and full roster remain unestablished. The Tower itself remains an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age, not a relic created by the Ordo.


## 7. Azerion Forest and the Northern Ancestor's Hidden Wound

**GM-sensitive established history; not common knowledge.** During the world-calamity incursion described in HIST-001, Beiming Han subdued a Demonic Beast that had descended through the otherworldly gates. After its mind was purified, its existence rose to become a **Heavenly Beast, rank 9**. The encounter left a scar on Beiming Han. The beast is a colossal dragon with a deer-like head, enormous and beautifully branching antlers, and deep-blue eyes. It later dwelled deep within **Azerion Forest**. Humans use **Dead Zone** to refer specifically to the deepest inner/core zone of the forest, not the whole forest. Its personal name, exact chronology, full purification process, present motives, and further capabilities remain unknown.

Azerion Forest is a vast, dangerous forest and major Spirit Beast habitat that extends from the Lawless Territories into Golden Dragon Empire territory. Humans use **Dead Zone** only for its deepest inner/core zone, which they know to avoid. The central forest contains an enormous lake deeper than one of the world's oceans; rocks on its lakebed mutated through direct exposure to the ancient Heavenly Beast's power. An entrance in the lake leads into ruins and the Mega Colossal Labyrinth, whose extent is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire and whose exit connects to the northern deep-sea coast. The northern forest contains Inner Azerion, a hidden half-beast city enclosed by dense illusion fog. Only veteran forest-goers and local hunter tribes know safe routes into it. Full geographic and exploration rules are in world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md and world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md.

Knowledge of the beast's origin, purification, and role in Beiming Han's scar is tightly restricted. Beiming Xenhwa, his adopted daughter, is among the very few who know the truth. Do not expose this secret through public lore, ordinary NPC dialogue, or casual narration; reveal it only if an in-world discovery and the character's knowledge justify it. The beast's history does not establish that every Demonic Beast can be purified, that it is the only Heavenly Beast rank 9, that it is hostile to every visitor, or that it can be defeated by any particular character.
