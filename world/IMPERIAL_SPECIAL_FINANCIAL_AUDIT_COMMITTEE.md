## 1. Identity and Mandate

- **Official name:** Komite Auditor Keuangan Khusus Kekaisaran
- **Short name:** Komite Auditor Keuangan Khusus
- **Organization type:** Special imperial internal-oversight committee
- **Established sphere:** Ministries and government institutions subordinate to them within the Golden Dragon Empire
- **Headquarters / office:** Not established
- **Founding date and legal instrument:** Not established
- **Exact statutory authority:** Not established
- **Current cases or deployments:** None confirmed by this module

The committee exists to provide internal oversight across imperial ministries and their subordinate government agencies. Its remit is broader than checking accounts or public expenditure.

Its general oversight work may include:
- reviewing financial irregularities and public-fund records;
- examining administrative misconduct and alleged abuse of office;
- reviewing complaints or reports concerning government administration;
- checking claims that official records or evidence may have been altered or manipulated;
- documenting findings and referring matters through whatever lawful procedures are established for the case.

These are broad areas of oversight, not a grant of unlimited authority. This module does not establish that the committee can prosecute crimes, impose sentences, overrule courts, command military units, or bypass other lawful institutions. Any such power must be established by separate canon.

## 3. Oversight Boundaries and Procedure

The committee's internal-oversight remit does not make it omniscient or automatically correct. Findings must be grounded in available records, testimony, corroboration, and the procedures established for the specific matter.

- A complaint is not proof of misconduct.
- A discrepancy is not, by itself, proof of deliberate fraud.
- A claim of manipulated evidence must remain an allegation until supported by credible findings.
- The committee's head being honest does not prove every subordinate is honest.
- Uncertainty about a subordinate does not prove that subordinate is corrupt.
- Do not infer the committee's exact investigative powers, access rights, evidentiary standards, referral process, or relationship to the courts beyond what later canon establishes.
- Do not assume every ministry or subordinate agency is under active investigation at any given time.

The committee is an imperial institution, but its exact office, building, public counter, branches, staffing schedule, access rules, and current deployment are not established. Longyuan is the center of imperial administration; this does not establish the committee's exact address there.
