# Merzkava Universe — Intelligent Peoples

**Status:** Official foundational worldbuilding module.

Humans are not the only intelligent people in Merzkava Universe. Many peoples and lineages exist, including humanlike beings with fox features, partial wyvern traits, aquatic traits, butterfly-like wings, fire-associated features, and other animal, spiritual, or elemental characteristics.

This is an open list, not a complete taxonomy. Appearance alone does not establish abilities, weaknesses, lifespan, cultivation compatibility, morality, or culture. Unknown details remain `???` until established.

## Regional Acceptance of Half-Beast Peoples

The following is the general order of social tolerance, from highest to lowest. It describes broad social tendencies, not the guaranteed behavior of every individual, institution, or settlement.

### 1. Tianhai Yunzhou — Knowledge Above Lineage

Tianhai Yunzhou places high value on knowledge, learning, and useful expertise. A person's half-beast lineage is generally less important when they can contribute valuable knowledge, research, craft, or insight.

Acceptance does not mean unconditional equality. Social standing may still depend on a person's abilities, connections, contribution, and circumstances.

### 2. Beiming Gu — A Shared Path

Beiming Gu is relatively receptive to half-beast people whose path aligns with its own. Shared goals, principles, loyalties, or cultivation pursuits can make acceptance easier.

Lineage alone does not guarantee acceptance: conflict of interest or incompatible aims can still lead to rejection.

### 3. Lawless Territories — Usefulness as Value

In the Lawless Territories, a half-beast person's perceived usefulness can strongly influence how much they are wanted. Skills, strength, knowledge, rare traits, or other advantages may attract protection, recruitment, trade, or exploitation.

This acceptance is often transactional. A person may be treated as an asset, weapon, tool, bargaining chip, or commodity rather than as an equal. Local customs and the interests of whoever holds power determine the actual treatment.

### 4. Golden Dragon Empire — Accepted, Yet Considered Impure

The Golden Dragon Empire follows orthodox traditions that place high value on perceived purity. Half-beast people are nevertheless accepted as members of society and are not categorically excluded from living within the Golden Dragon Empire.

However, prejudice remains common in some communities and institutions. They may face racism, social exclusion, unequal treatment, or suspicion because their lineage is considered impure by orthodox-minded people. Legal or social acceptance does not guarantee equal treatment.

## Limits and Exceptions

- These rankings describe general tendencies; they do not determine every person's attitude or every local law.
- Individual communities, clans, sects, employers, and officials may behave differently from the broader regional pattern.
- Some orthodox, heterodox, demonic, and independent factions may welcome, protect, recruit, or exploit half-beast people for different reasons.
- Prejudice and exploitation can occur in any region, even where general tolerance is high.
- Do not assume that every half-beast person has the same experience, status, abilities, or social opportunities.
- Do not invent universal legal rights, formal bans, population figures, or detailed biological rules unless they are established in canon.
