# Lawless Southern Port and Lanternhook Isles

**Status:** Canonical geographic and location module.
**Scope:** The southern maritime access from Lawless Territories, its ordinary-water island leisure network, and the route onward to Guixu Canghai.
**Authority:** This module establishes the named port, archipelago, and their stated functions. It does not establish exact coordinates, distances, sailing durations, timetables, guaranteed safe passage, or ownership by any existing faction.

## 1. Geographic placement

Lawless Territories are the nearest major organized region to Guixu Canghai, and they provide two distinct strategic approaches: the easiest known entrance to Azerion Forest and the closest organized land-side access toward the world's most dangerous ocean.

The southern maritime sequence is:

```text
LAWLESS TERRITORIES
  └── southern coast
       └── Southwake Port (南潮港)
            └── ordinary Black Tide Sea
                 └── Lanternhook Isles (钩灯群岛)
                      └── southern outer boundary of Black Tide Sea
                           └── Guixu Canghai (归墟沧海)
```

This is a geographic sequence, not a claim that every segment has a surveyed road, fixed schedule, or safe passage. Southwake Port is the established port on the southern Lawless coast. Lanternhook Isles lie in ordinary Black Tide Sea, not in Guixu Canghai. The dangerous ocean begins only beyond the southern outer boundary of Black Tide Sea.

## 2. Southwake Port (南潮港)

Southwake Port is a Lawless-facing maritime port on the southern coast of the Lawless Territories. It is the nearest established port for expeditions, illicit trade, and ships heading toward the southern waters and eventual Guixu Canghai approach.

- **Role:** Departure and arrival point for ordinary Black Tide Sea traffic, independent crews, smugglers, and high-risk southern expeditions.
- **Character:** Pragmatic and transactional. A visitor may find legitimate cargo handling beside discreet brokers, ship repair, provisions, guides, and people willing to ask few questions.
- **Authority:** No unified Lawless government controls the port by default. Port-side rules, protection arrangements, fees, and influence depend on the local operator and current circumstances.
- **Access limits:** The port does not make Guixu Canghai safe or directly adjacent. Vessels must cross ordinary Black Tide Sea first; conditions worsen beyond its southern outer boundary.
- **Unspecified details:** Exact position, harbor depth, capacity, local ruler, fees, sailing schedules, and named ship operators are not established.

## 3. Lanternhook Isles (钩灯群岛)

Lanternhook Isles are a small archipelago in ordinary Black Tide Sea, known as a discretionary refuge and leisure stop for pirates, smugglers, mercenaries, fugitives, and other criminals. Their reputation comes from entertainment and discreet commerce rather than from being part of Guixu Canghai.

The isles have no single lawful government recognized by the three major powers. Local operators, crews, brokers, and protection groups maintain their own arrangements. Visiting does not guarantee immunity from betrayal, violence, arrest elsewhere, or conflict between patrons.

### 3.1 Red Lantern Cay (红灯屿) — bars and entertainment

Red Lantern Cay is the best-known social stop in the archipelago. Its waterfront has drinking houses, food stalls, music rooms, private entertainment pavilions, rented lounges, and discreet meeting rooms. These venues cater to crews seeking rest, celebration, companionship, rumors, or negotiations away from mainland scrutiny.

The cay is not a guaranteed safe zone: disputes, extortion, surveillance, and sudden factional clashes can still occur.

### 3.2 Bitterglass Isle (苦璃岛) — black-market physicians

Bitterglass Isle hosts independent physicians and specialist dealers serving clients who avoid ordinary institutions. Their trade includes medicinal powders, poisons, antidotes, sedatives, toxins for research or illicit use, rare ingredients, and treatments for injuries or unusual conditions.

- Quality, purity, effects, and provenance vary by seller; a label or seller's promise is not proof.
- Poison and antidote availability is not universal, and no product is guaranteed to work on every species, cultivation level, or condition.
- Medical competence, prices, stock, legality, and the identity of any practitioner must be established in the scene rather than assumed.
- The island's reputation does not mean every physician is a criminal or every powder is poisonous.

### 3.3 Dicewake Isle (骰潮岛) — gambling and wagers

Dicewake Isle is the archipelago's main gambling stop, with dice halls, card rooms, private wagering tables, high-stakes games, and brokers who arrange bets on contests or other risky ventures. Credit, debt, cheating, rigged games, intimidation, and disputes are recurring risks, not guaranteed outcomes.

No fixed odds, universal house rules, or official exchange rates are established here. Any particular wager must define its stakes and rules before play.

## 4. Travel and canon boundaries

- Southwake Port is on the southern Lawless coast; it is not a port inside Guixu Canghai.
- Lanternhook Isles are in ordinary Black Tide Sea. They do not move the boundary of Black Tide Sea or make the archipelago part of Guixu Canghai.
- Guixu Canghai remains beyond the southern outer boundary of Black Tide Sea and remains the world's most dangerous ocean.
- The port and isles are part of the wider Lawless maritime network but do not replace the four existing settlements of Black Tide Expanse or automatically belong to any of its listed organizations.
- No direct road from any named Lawless village to Southwake Port is established. The exact approach from the existing settlements to the southern coast remains unconfirmed.
- Do not invent precise coordinates, travel times, vessel schedules, guaranteed passage, fees, or the names and profiles of permanent NPCs.
