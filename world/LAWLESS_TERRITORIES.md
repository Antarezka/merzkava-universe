# Merzkava Universe — Lawless Territories

**Status:** Owner-approved canon reference.  
**Scope:** Shared social conditions, underground economy, organizations' service roles, the Dead Zone, and restricted historical continuity.  
**Authority:** world/WORLD.md establishes the world-level identity; world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md and the three regional modules establish the territorial framework. This file records the owner-approved details below without turning the Lawless Territories into one unified state.

## 1. What the Lawless Territories Are

The Lawless Territories are communities, wilderness, routes, and local power centers outside the direct jurisdiction of the three major powers: **Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu**. They are not one country, one sect, or one government. Local customs, informal rules, faction influence, and safety differ from place to place.

The existing regional framework remains:
- **Ashen Borderlands** — outside the western and southwestern borders of Golden Dragon Empire.
- **Black Tide Expanse** — remote islands and sea routes outside Tianhai Yunzhou's direct control.
- **Nine Frost Wastes** — northern wild areas outside Beiming Gu's official defenses.

Regional association does not by itself prove that an organization owns or controls a village, road, auction, or arena. Use the relevant regional file and recorded events for local control.

## 2. General Living Conditions

- **Poor agricultural soil:** Much of the established Lawless Territories has poor soil for crops and similar cultivation. Food is scarce compared with well-supported farming regions, and ordinary food quality tends to be low. Better food can exist where imported, traded, specially cultivated, or reserved for wealthier patrons; it is not universally unavailable.
- **Uneven livelihoods:** Residents may be ordinary farmers, fishers, hunters, craftspeople, traders, transporters, innkeepers, smugglers, fixers, independent cultivators, bounty hunters, or criminals. A person's residence here does not prove guilt or violent intent.
- **Small entertainment houses:** Modest houses of entertainment cater to criminals, heterodox Spirit Realm experts, travelers, and other NPCs. They are local services, not automatically elite establishments or faction headquarters. Specific names, services, fees, and staff remain unestablished until recorded.
- **Risk and opportunity:** Lack of one central authority creates room for independent trade, unusual services, black markets, exploitation, and local protection arrangements. It does not mean every transaction is fraudulent or every encounter is hostile.

## 2.1 Concealed Shadow Authority — Crimson Sovereign Court

The **Crimson Sovereign Court (赤尊宫 · Chì Zūn Gōng)** is a concealed power group whose leader, **Ying Wuxin (嬴无心)**, exercises authority over the **Crimson Keel Brotherhood** from above. His canonical NPC profile is `world/NPC_YING_WUXIN.md`; the Black Tide regional context is recorded in `world/REGION_BLACK_TIDE_EXPANSE.md`.

Ying Wuxin is Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial and possesses the Martial Soul **Asura — Myth**. Cold and sadistic, he deliberately hides his identity and abilities so others cannot establish his weaknesses. His shadow influence is comparable in reach to an empire, but the Court is not a fourth major power or a unified government of the Lawless Territories. The Court's exact membership, bases, territory, chain of command, and influence over individual settlements remain unknown. Do not assume all Crimson Keel cells know his identity, or that every Lawless organization is subordinate to him.

His coercive **Soul Illusion** can invert a target's perception of core personality traits and emotions. A target may recognize the manipulation and try to attack or escape, only for the effect to reassert control and redirect the target into submission and displayed affection. Such outward affection is a product of forced soul/perception manipulation, not consent or proof of the victim's genuine wishes. Women held under his control must not be depicted as voluntarily devoted on the basis of manipulated behavior. The ability's precise triggers, range, costs, countermeasures, and limits remain unknown.

## 3. Established Underground Economy and Services

### 3.1 Assassin contract brokerage — Silent Maw Hall

Silent Maw Hall acts as a **broker/intermediary** between clients and independent assassins affiliated with its network. It is not itself an organization made up entirely of assassins, and an affiliated assassin remains an independent contractor rather than an automatic direct member of the Hall.

- The brokerage has a code of ethics. The full wording, exceptions, sanctions, and enforcement procedure are not yet specified; do not invent a universal code clause or guarantee that every contract is safe.
- Standard share of the contract payment: **60% to the intermediary / Silent Maw Hall, 40% to the independent assassin**.
- The Hall's role includes introducing clients to contractors and brokering the arrangement. This is one of the more established and professionalized livelihoods in the Lawless Territories, but it remains risky and does not imply state recognition.
- Contract details, identities, target verification, payment safeguards, and the Hall's internal vetting procedures are case-specific unless separately established.

### 3.2 Appraisal services — Black Scripture Sect

Black Scripture Sect operates appraisal services in parts of the Lawless Territories. Its appraisers can assess artifacts, materials, Spirit Beast-related items, Formation Engravers (the formation-carving tools used by Thinkers), and other goods when they possess the relevant expertise.

- Some appraisers deliberately exploit customers who appear clueless, inexperienced, or unable to recognize an item's value. They may understate value, conceal uncertainty, or mislead a buyer or seller.
- Deception is a real risk, not a rule that every appraiser always lies. Expertise, honesty, reputation, evidence, and a second opinion still matter.
- An appraisal is an opinion or service, not a guarantee of authenticity, provenance, functionality, or fair market value.
- Fees, appraisal accuracy rates, and the Sect's complete service catalogue remain unspecified.

### 3.3 Illegal sparring arenas and betting — Hollow Fang Alliance

Some venues associated with Hollow Fang Alliance run illegal sparring arenas with betting systems.

- Fighters may compete for money, goods, or other agreed prizes; spectators and patrons may place bets under local rules.
- Prizes are often materially better or rarer than rewards offered at sanctioned sparring arenas in cities under the three major powers. This is a tendency, not a guarantee that every match has superior prizes.
- Illegal status means oversight, dispute resolution, safety standards, and payment enforcement can be unreliable. Match terms, injury risk, cheating, collusion, and prize custody vary by venue.
- Do not invent a fixed betting ratio, universal match rules, or guaranteed reward tier.

### 3.4 Elite underground auctions

Several Lawless Territories organizations sponsor or cooperate in elite auctions for high-level criminal organizations, wealthy underworld patrons, and heterodox Spirit Realm experts. No single organization is established as owning every auction.

Known or plausible catalogue categories explicitly established by this canon include:
- Rare half-beast individuals/lineages, including rare variants, traded illicitly as people rather than ordinary goods.
- Spirit Rings preserved in compact **portable Spirit Ring stasis Artificial Artifacts**, allowing a ring to be transported and absorbed elsewhere.
- Formation Engravers (specialized formation-carving tools used by Thinkers), including rare models with unusual or specialized attributes.
- Other rare items, materials, or specialized equipment when a specific listing is established.

An auction listing does not prove an item's authenticity, legality, safe operation, or availability. Do not create a specific catalogue, price, seller, buyer, or auction schedule unless it is established in a scene or source.

### 3.5 Portable Spirit Ring stasis Artificial Artifact

This is a rare, expensive Artificial Artifact class that holds a Spirit Ring in stasis for transport and later absorption. Its compact form is suitable for carrying in a pocket or similarly small container. It makes Spirit Ring acquisition and absorption more flexible by separating the location of the hunt from the location of absorption.

Such artifacts are normally associated with elite academies, military institutions, major powers, and other well-resourced organizations. They are not ordinary equipment for all cultivators. Exact tier, capacity, preservation duration, price, failure modes, and activation procedure remain unknown. Stasis does not remove the official Spirit Ring compatibility and absorption requirements in core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md.

The artifact catalogue entry is maintained in world/ARTIFICIAL_ARTIFACTS.md.

### 3.6 People-smuggling and illegal border crossing

Some residents and networks provide illegal passage into one of the three major powers for people avoiding capture, including fugitives and people targeted by the **Ordo Suci Tianming** or other authorities. A target being pursued does not by itself establish that the accusation is true.

- **Cheap passage:** lower cost but greater danger, less reliable guides, fewer safeguards, and a higher chance of abandonment, detection, or betrayal.
- **Expensive passage:** may include extra escorts, route planning, decoys, safer staging points, or better intelligence. Higher cost does not guarantee success or immunity from detection.
- Routes, border controls, fees, escorts, and risks vary by destination and current conditions. Do not invent a universal price or guaranteed safe corridor.
- Crimson Keel Brotherhood has an established association with smuggling and illegal escort work in the Black Tide Expanse; other independent or local networks may also participate. Association is not proof that it controls every people-smuggling route.

### 3.7 The Wayfarer Hearth Network — neutral refuge

The **Wayfarer Hearth Network** is a loose, neutral network of outcasts and fugitives who were falsely accused or framed through corrupt relationships and then pursued across one or more of the three major powers.

- The network tries to protect newcomers, especially those who arrive weak, helpless, injured, or without dependable support.
- Its members are not automatically innocent of every possible offense, and the network is not a universal rescue service. Its founding, leadership, membership count, safehouse list, symbols, and internal rules remain unestablished.
- Its neutrality is practical rather than backed by a state: members seek to keep refuge sites from becoming automatic extensions of any one sect or criminal organization.
- It may provide shelter, basic food, warnings, introductions, or a temporary hiding place when capacity and circumstances permit. Specific aid, cost, and risk must be resolved in context.
- Do not equate this network with Medicine Hall Society or assume its members have medical expertise.

## 3.1 Western Tianhai approach and shared Azerion connections

The western side of Tianhai Yunzhou's floating position above the southwestern ocean faces a sea-side approach toward the **Lawless Territories**. From the Lawless Territories, travelers can reach the easiest known entrance to **Azerion Forest**. This is a western/sea-facing approach to the mainland-side Lawless region; it does not mean Tianhai Yunzhou is physically joined to the mainland by a road.

Azerion Forest extends from the Lawless Territories into **Golden Dragon Empire** territory. At the forest–empire boundary, a rushing river creates a trap-like barrier. A small island in the middle of this fast-water route is a prison island owned by Golden Dragon Empire. The river and island are geographic obstacles, not a routine public crossing. The river name, exact banks, crossing method, island coordinates, and prison layout remain unspecified.

The forest also has a distinct northern coastal exit associated with the lake ruins and the **Mega Colossal Labyrinth**. This is relevant to the approach from **Beiming Gu** along the northern coastline: the route is a dangerous ruined/labyrinth exit, not an ordinary surveyed road through Azerion Forest. The labyrinth's interior has no complete map; do not invent its internal corridors or treat the route as safe or guaranteed.

## 4. Azerion Forest and Inner Azerion

**Azerion Forest** is a vast, dangerous forest and a major habitat for Spirit Beasts. It is one of the principal hunting grounds where humans seek Spirit Beasts for compatible Spirit Rings. The forest crosses territorial boundaries and extends from the Lawless Territories into the territory of the **Golden Dragon Empire**. **Dead Zone** is not another name for the whole forest: humans use that name for its deepest inner/core zone, which they know to avoid.

- The easiest known entrance is located in the Lawless Territories.
- The forest's scale, dangerous terrain, and uncertain visibility make travel and exploration hazardous. In its central area lies an enormous lake deeper than one of the world's oceans. Rocks on the lakebed have mutated after direct exposure to the power of the ancient Heavenly Beast. The lake contains an entrance to ruins whose underground passages lead into the **Mega Colossal Labyrinth**; its extent is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire. The labyrinth is not suitable for beginners, and its exit connects to the sea along the northern deep-sea coast. Exact dimensions, corridors, internal geography, safe routes, and travel duration remain unknown.
- The deepest inner/core zone is known among humans as the **Dead Zone**. Humans know not to enter it; this label applies to that core zone, not to Azerion Forest as a whole.
- In the deepest part of the forest dwells an ancient **Heavenly Beast rank 9**: a colossal dragon with a deer-like head, enormous beautifully branching antlers, and deep-blue eyes. During the ancient world-calamity incursion, it was originally a Demonic Beast that descended through an otherworldly gate. Beiming Han subdued it and purified its mind; its existence then rose to become a Heavenly Beast. Its personal name, exact chronology, full purification process, detailed abilities, disposition, and exact location remain unknown.
- The encounter left a scar on **Beiming Han, the Leluhur Utara of Beiming Gu**. Its origin and the beast's purification are restricted historical facts, not common public knowledge. Beiming Xenhwa is among the very few people who know the truth. Do not expose this secret through ordinary public lore or casual narration.

### 4.1 Inner Azerion — The Hidden Half-Beast City

**Inner Azerion** is a large, prosperous, well-structured city in the northern part of Azerion Forest. Half-beast people run the city's economic life. The city is concealed behind an extremely dense **illusion fog**, making it very difficult to find from outside. Its exact coordinates, routes, government structure, population, leadership, districts, and founding history remain unspecified.

- **Concealment and access:** The city is enclosed by extremely dense illusion fog. Only veterans who frequently enter Azerion Forest and the local hunter tribes living there know safe routes into Inner Azerion. Others risk becoming lost in the illusion fog indefinitely. Do not assume a traveler can find the city merely by entering the forest or following an ordinary map.
- **Attitude toward humans:** The city is deeply unwelcoming to humans in general, but it does not automatically seek conflict with a human who is an established **Spirit Realm** expert simply for being human. This is not a guarantee of safety or special privileges.
- **Non-awakened humans:** A human who has not awakened is in extreme danger inside the city. Do not treat entry by a non-awakened human as an ordinary or safe visit.
- **Treatment of half-beast people:** The city's people are generally welcoming toward their own kind. Its internal economy is accessible and relatively affordable for half-beast residents and customers.
- **Human pricing:** Human visitors face substantially higher prices than half-beast customers. Do not invent exact multipliers, tariffs, or price lists until established.
- **Slave market:** A slave market operates in Inner Azerion. Its precise scale, rules, inventory, ownership, and relationship to the city's authorities remain undefined. Its existence does not establish that every half-beast resident supports slavery or that every market transaction involves half-beast people.

Inner Azerion's concealed location and hostile attitude toward non-awakened humans are separate risks: finding the city does not guarantee safe entry, and being a Spirit Realm expert does not automatically reveal the illusion or grant unrestricted access.

### 4.2 Lake Ruins and the Mega Colossal Labyrinth

The central lake of Azerion Forest contains an entrance to ancient ruins. The entrance leads into underground passages and then the **Mega Colossal Labyrinth**, whose overall extent is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire. The labyrinth is a long-form exploration site, not a beginner dungeon. Its exit connects to the sea along the northern deep-sea coast.

- The lakebed's sea rocks have mutated through direct exposure to the power of the ancient Heavenly Beast. Do not assume every rock has identical properties or invent specific effects without discovery or an authoritative rule.
- **No map exists for the unexplored labyrinth.** Do not generate, display, or treat a complete internal map as already known. A map can only reflect routes actually surveyed and recorded in play.
- The labyrinth's full layout, passage connections, hazards, mechanisms, and history are not yet established. Its enormous scale does not prove that every corridor is connected, that the exit is easy to reach, or that the whole structure has been mapped.
- A hidden treasure reward lies in the deepest part, but entering the ruins or reaching deep areas does not guarantee finding it. A particular condition must be discovered and met to trigger the opening of the treasure chamber. The trigger's nature is undisclosed until found through valid exploration; do not reveal it prematurely or award the treasure merely because a character entered the ruin.
- Treat discoveries, mapped segments, clues, failed attempts, and triggered mechanisms consistently once established. Do not retroactively change a discovered route or secretly change a treasure condition to force a desired outcome.

## Half-Beast Slave Trade — Public Secret

The Lawless Territories' criminal organizations and underground networks are the **largest suppliers of enslaved half-beasts to corrupt buyers connected to all three major powers**: Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu. The trade crosses borders through intermediaries, disguised cargo, falsified custody papers, private auctions, and buyers who use respectable titles or fronts to hide their involvement. This is a **public secret** among people who work around the underworld, border commerce, private auctions, and corrupt circles—not a matter unknown to everyone, but rarely acknowledged openly by those who benefit from it.

The three powers officially prohibit half-beast enslavement. The contradiction is sustained by corruption and selective enforcement: some officials, nobles, wealthy patrons, and factional figures condemn the practice publicly while quietly purchasing captives, shielding brokers, or obstructing investigations. This does **not** mean the three governments as a whole officially endorse slavery, nor that every organization or official participates. The Lawless Territories are not one unified organization; different groups may broker, transport, protect, expose, or oppose the trade, and involvement must be grounded in a named group's established actions rather than assumed from its name alone.

This trade helps explain why half-beasts are rarely seen openly in Lawless Territories unless they are strong enough to resist capture or deter would-be captors. It does not mean all half-beasts are enslaved, all are weak, or every unseen half-beast is a captive. See `world/PRISON_AND_BOUND_ITEMS.md` for restraint types and abuse patterns.
