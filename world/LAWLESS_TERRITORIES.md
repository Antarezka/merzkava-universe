# Merzkava Universe — Lawless Territories

**Status:** Owner-approved canon reference.  
**Scope:** Shared social conditions, underground economy, organizations' service roles, the Dead Zone, and restricted historical continuity.  
**Authority:** world/WORLD.md establishes the world-level identity; world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md and the three regional modules establish the territorial framework. This file records the owner-approved details below without turning the Lawless Territories into one unified state.

## 1. What the Lawless Territories Are

The Lawless Territories are communities, wilderness, routes, and local power centers outside the direct jurisdiction of the three major powers: **Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu**. They are not one country, one sect, or one government. Local customs, informal rules, faction influence, and safety differ from place to place.

The existing regional framework remains:
- **Ashen Borderlands** — outside the western and southwestern borders of Golden Dragon Empire.
- **Black Tide Expanse** — remote islands and sea routes outside Tianhai Yunzhou's direct control.
- **Nine Frost Wastes** — northern wild areas outside Beiming Gu's official defenses.

Regional association does not by itself prove that an organization owns or controls a village, road, auction, or arena. Use the relevant regional file and recorded events for local control.

## 2. General Living Conditions

- **Poor agricultural soil:** Much of the established Lawless Territories has poor soil for crops and similar cultivation. Food is scarce compared with well-supported farming regions, and ordinary food quality tends to be low. Better food can exist where imported, traded, specially cultivated, or reserved for wealthier patrons; it is not universally unavailable.
- **Uneven livelihoods:** Residents may be ordinary farmers, fishers, hunters, craftspeople, traders, transporters, innkeepers, smugglers, fixers, independent cultivators, bounty hunters, or criminals. A person's residence here does not prove guilt or violent intent.
- **Small entertainment houses:** Modest houses of entertainment cater to criminals, heterodox Spirit Realm experts, travelers, and other NPCs. They are local services, not automatically elite establishments or faction headquarters. Specific names, services, fees, and staff remain unestablished until recorded.
- **Risk and opportunity:** Lack of one central authority creates room for independent trade, unusual services, black markets, exploitation, and local protection arrangements. It does not mean every transaction is fraudulent or every encounter is hostile.

## 3. Established Underground Economy and Services

### 3.1 Assassin contract brokerage — Silent Maw Hall

Silent Maw Hall acts as a **broker/intermediary** between clients and independent assassins affiliated with its network. It is not itself an organization made up entirely of assassins, and an affiliated assassin remains an independent contractor rather than an automatic direct member of the Hall.

- The brokerage has a code of ethics. The full wording, exceptions, sanctions, and enforcement procedure are not yet specified; do not invent a universal code clause or guarantee that every contract is safe.
- Standard share of the contract payment: **60% to the intermediary / Silent Maw Hall, 40% to the independent assassin**.
- The Hall's role includes introducing clients to contractors and brokering the arrangement. This is one of the more established and professionalized livelihoods in the Lawless Territories, but it remains risky and does not imply state recognition.
- Contract details, identities, target verification, payment safeguards, and the Hall's internal vetting procedures are case-specific unless separately established.

### 3.2 Appraisal services — Black Scripture Sect

Black Scripture Sect operates appraisal services in parts of the Lawless Territories. Its appraisers can assess artifacts, materials, Spirit Beast-related items, Formation Engravers (the formation-carving tools used by Thinkers), and other goods when they possess the relevant expertise.

- Some appraisers deliberately exploit customers who appear clueless, inexperienced, or unable to recognize an item's value. They may understate value, conceal uncertainty, or mislead a buyer or seller.
- Deception is a real risk, not a rule that every appraiser always lies. Expertise, honesty, reputation, evidence, and a second opinion still matter.
- An appraisal is an opinion or service, not a guarantee of authenticity, provenance, functionality, or fair market value.
- Fees, appraisal accuracy rates, and the Sect's complete service catalogue remain unspecified.

### 3.3 Illegal sparring arenas and betting — Hollow Fang Alliance

Some venues associated with Hollow Fang Alliance run illegal sparring arenas with betting systems.

- Fighters may compete for money, goods, or other agreed prizes; spectators and patrons may place bets under local rules.
- Prizes are often materially better or rarer than rewards offered at sanctioned sparring arenas in cities under the three major powers. This is a tendency, not a guarantee that every match has superior prizes.
- Illegal status means oversight, dispute resolution, safety standards, and payment enforcement can be unreliable. Match terms, injury risk, cheating, collusion, and prize custody vary by venue.
- Do not invent a fixed betting ratio, universal match rules, or guaranteed reward tier.

### 3.4 Elite underground auctions

Several Lawless Territories organizations sponsor or cooperate in elite auctions for high-level criminal organizations, wealthy underworld patrons, and heterodox Spirit Realm experts. No single organization is established as owning every auction.

Known or plausible catalogue categories explicitly established by this canon include:
- Rare half-beast individuals/lineages, including rare variants, traded illicitly as people rather than ordinary goods.
- Spirit Rings preserved in compact **portable Spirit Ring stasis Artificial Artifacts**, allowing a ring to be transported and absorbed elsewhere.
- Formation Engravers (specialized formation-carving tools used by Thinkers), including rare models with unusual or specialized attributes.
- Other rare items, materials, or specialized equipment when a specific listing is established.

An auction listing does not prove an item's authenticity, legality, safe operation, or availability. Do not create a specific catalogue, price, seller, buyer, or auction schedule unless it is established in a scene or source.

### 3.5 Portable Spirit Ring stasis Artificial Artifact

This is a rare, expensive Artificial Artifact class that holds a Spirit Ring in stasis for transport and later absorption. Its compact form is suitable for carrying in a pocket or similarly small container. It makes Spirit Ring acquisition and absorption more flexible by separating the location of the hunt from the location of absorption.

Such artifacts are normally associated with elite academies, military institutions, major powers, and other well-resourced organizations. They are not ordinary equipment for all cultivators. Exact tier, capacity, preservation duration, price, failure modes, and activation procedure remain unknown. Stasis does not remove the official Spirit Ring compatibility and absorption requirements in core/SPIRIT_RING_RULES.md.

The artifact catalogue entry is maintained in world/ARTIFICIAL_ARTIFACTS.md.

### 3.6 People-smuggling and illegal border crossing

Some residents and networks provide illegal passage into one of the three major powers for people avoiding capture, including fugitives and people targeted by the **Ordo Suci Tianming** or other authorities. A target being pursued does not by itself establish that the accusation is true.

- **Cheap passage:** lower cost but greater danger, less reliable guides, fewer safeguards, and a higher chance of abandonment, detection, or betrayal.
- **Expensive passage:** may include extra escorts, route planning, decoys, safer staging points, or better intelligence. Higher cost does not guarantee success or immunity from detection.
- Routes, border controls, fees, escorts, and risks vary by destination and current conditions. Do not invent a universal price or guaranteed safe corridor.
- Crimson Keel Brotherhood has an established association with smuggling and illegal escort work in the Black Tide Expanse; other independent or local networks may also participate. Association is not proof that it controls every people-smuggling route.

### 3.7 The Wayfarer Hearth Network — neutral refuge

The **Wayfarer Hearth Network** is a loose, neutral network of outcasts and fugitives who were falsely accused or framed through corrupt relationships and then pursued across one or more of the three major powers.

- The network tries to protect newcomers, especially those who arrive weak, helpless, injured, or without dependable support.
- Its members are not automatically innocent of every possible offense, and the network is not a universal rescue service. Its founding, leadership, membership count, safehouse list, symbols, and internal rules remain unestablished.
- Its neutrality is practical rather than backed by a state: members seek to keep refuge sites from becoming automatic extensions of any one sect or criminal organization.
- It may provide shelter, basic food, warnings, introductions, or a temporary hiding place when capacity and circumstances permit. Specific aid, cost, and risk must be resolved in context.
- Do not equate this network with Medicine Hall Society or assume its members have medical expertise.

## 4. Dead Zone — The Fog-Bound Forest

The **Dead Zone** is a dangerous, mist-shrouded forest and one of the world's largest forest expanses.

- The easiest known entrance is located in the Lawless Territories.
- The forest extends beyond the Lawless Territories and into the territory of **Golden Dragon Empire**.
- Its full official name on the Golden Dragon Empire side is **not established in the current repository**. Until an official name is found or approved, use “Dead Zone” as the established common name and do not fabricate a longer imperial name.
- Fog, dangerous terrain, uncertain visibility, and the forest's scale make travel and exploration hazardous. Exact boundaries, mapped routes, distances, ecology, and ordinary encounter rates remain unestablished.
- The deepest part of the forest is home to an ancient **Heavenly Beast rank 9**: a colossal dragon with a deer-like head, enormous beautifully branching antlers, and deep-blue eyes. The beast's personal name, detailed abilities, disposition, exact location, and history beyond the wound described below remain unknown.
- The beast left a scar on **Beiming Han, the Leluhur Utara of Beiming Gu**, in the distant past. This is a restricted historical fact, not common public knowledge.

### Restricted knowledge and NPC continuity

The beast's role in Beiming Han's scar is known only to a very small number of people, including his adopted daughter **Beiming Xenhwa**. Canonical continuity is recorded in:
- world/HISTORY.md
- regions/beiming-gu/npcs/BEIMING_HAN.md
- regions/beiming-gu/npcs/BEIMING_XENHWA.md

Do not disclose this history through ordinary public exposition or make unrelated NPCs aware of it without a credible in-world discovery. The reason Xenhwa knows, the exact date and circumstances of the encounter, the scar's exact location, and the beast's name are deliberately unspecified.

## 5. Canon and GM Guardrails

- Always consult this file alongside the matching world/REGION_*.md, world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md, world/FACTIONS.md, and world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md when a Lawless Territories scene depends on these facts.
- Do not treat the three regional frameworks as one contiguous state or assume direct routes between all settlements.
- Do not assume all residents are criminals, all appraisers lie, all assassins are Silent Maw Hall members, or all auctions contain every listed item category.
- Do not assume the Wayfarer Hearth Network can protect everyone or that the Dead Zone's Heavenly Beast can be located, provoked, defeated, or escaped automatically.
- Resolve money, item ownership, injuries, travel time, combat, and Spirit Ring absorption through their respective official mechanics.
- Preserve uncertainty where the canon above leaves details open.
