# Merzkava Universe — Map and Regions

**Status:** Regional reference module.  
**Canon policy:** Established facts are separated from geographic details that still require confirmation. This file organizes known regions; it does not establish precise coordinates, distances, travel times, or borders that have not been defined. See `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md` for the broader world layout, wilderness, ruins, and transport; see `world/INTELLIGENT_PEOPLES.md` for the world's diverse peoples.

## 1. World Map: Current Geographic Model

Merzkava Universe is built around one vast continent with connected seas and maritime routes. The current world foundation identifies three major powers:

1. **Golden Dragon Empire** — eastern imperial power.
2. **Tianhai Yunzhou** — maritime power centered on a giant floating island.
3. **Beiming Gu** — northern power protected by mountains and severe cold.

The North is comparatively isolated by terrain and climate. The sea connects distant territories and supports travel, trade, diplomacy, and conflict. Lawless Territories exist outside the direct jurisdiction of the three major powers.

Golden Dragon Empire lies in the east, Tianhai Yunzhou floats above the southwestern ocean, and Beiming Gu lies in the north. Large tracts of wilderness and difficult terrain separate the major powers. The world contains multiple connected seas and maritime routes, but only **Guixu Canghai (归墟沧海)** has been named as a specific sea/ocean system so far. Other sea names, exact coastlines, straits, coordinates, distances, and verified travel durations remain unestablished. Do not infer exact proximity or travel time from this outline alone.

### Sea-map interpretation rules

- **Guixu Canghai is not the name for all oceans**, and Black Tide Expanse is a Lawless region of remote islands and sea routes, not a synonym for Guixu Canghai.
- The Windless / Still-Water Sea's stillness applies across the entire sea, not just around its trench: no wind, waves, or currents. Do not add ordinary surface drift or hidden currents to it unless the owner later changes this rule.
- The silence and cursed ancient Relic are established concepts; the Relic's name, origin, exact mechanism, and full effects remain unknown.
- A vessel's specialized liquefied-energy Artificial Artifact propulsion is distinct from a conventional Qi-powered vessel and from Tianhai Yunzhou's dedicated passenger aircraft. Do not assume every ship can use that propulsion.
- The three major-power perspectives must show the same sea systems and preserve the same unknowns. A sea appearing on a perspective map does not establish a new port, route, border, distance, or guaranteed safe passage.
- Broad sea connectivity is part of the world framework, but exact straits and transitions between named or unnamed waters remain unknown until established in an existing map or route source.

### 2.1 Imperial centralization and rural access

Development follows a gradient around the imperial center. Huanglongcheng and the Longyuan core have the densest infrastructure, the strongest civil presence, the broadest selection of medical and commercial services, and the easiest access to elite education and skilled specialists. Major regional seats retain useful services, but the quality and frequency of service generally decline farther from the capital, especially off major roads, rivers, and trade routes.

Outer areas are not uniformly poor: ports, mines, and furnace towns can prosper from trade or resources. However, their hinterlands may be neglected, with fewer public offices, longer waits for travelling physicians, fewer schools and libraries, limited markets, weaker road maintenance, and greater dependence on family, village elders, local craftsmen, and community cooperation. A remote village must not be treated as if it has the same amenities as a prefectural seat. See the atlas and population-services register for location-specific limits.

The atlas currently names **13 rural villages across the seven regions** as roleplay anchors. This is a compact register, not a census of every hamlet or farmstead. The former counts of 33 prefectures, 268 counties, 361 cities, 1,838 towns, and 22 villages are superseded; use the atlas for the current 12-prefecture / 6-county-circuit / 13-village structure.

## 3. Tianhai Yunzhou — Maritime Power

Tianhai Yunzhou is centered on a giant floating island sustained by a Tier 9 Artificial Artifact. Its power depends on maritime governance, navigation, trade, defense, and advanced Thinker knowledge.

The following regional and clan information is established in `world/WORLD.md`:

- **Twelve established clans:** Haiyuan (sailing and maritime affairs), Tianjing (administration and central interests), Yunhai (trade and transportation), Canglong (combat and defense), Bihai (coastal resources), Xuanchao (security and maritime surveillance), Lingfan (navigation and expeditions), Haijing (information and port relations), Yunzhou (main-island interests), Mingzhu (high-value commodities), Shuijing (materials processing and craftsmanship), and Leiyu (combat techniques and offense). Their spheres are broad influence areas, not proof of exclusive territorial control, fixed clan membership, or automatic subordination to the Maharaja.
- **Five established dojos:** Skywhale, Tidal Current, Stormwake, Azure Sail, and Deepwater. Their current leaders, membership, and formal political relationships remain unconfirmed.

The floating island's precise coordinates, neighboring island routes, and artifact maintenance sites are not yet defined. Its internal regional module now establishes a partial district/settlement route graph and a Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal linked by long-distance liquefied-energy aircraft to Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal in the Golden Dragon Empire and Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal in Beiming Gu. Exact terminal coordinates, distances, and travel times remain unknown.

## 5. Lawless Territories

Lawless Territories are not one unified country. They include communities and groups outside the direct jurisdiction of the three major powers. Some areas are dangerous, but the term does not mean every settlement lacks local laws or ordinary residents. Their established underground economy, neutral refuge network, poor agricultural soil, illegal arenas, elite auctions, cross-border smuggling, and Azerion Forest are documented in world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md. Azerion Forest's easiest known entrance lies in the Lawless Territories, while the forest extends into Golden Dragon Empire territory. It is a major Spirit Beast habitat and human Spirit Ring hunting ground. Humans call its deepest inner/core zone the Dead Zone and know to avoid it. A vast central lake contains mutated sea rocks and an entrance to ruins leading to the Mega Colossal Labyrinth, which is as vast as the Golden Dragon Empire and exits to the northern deep-sea coast. Its northern region contains Inner Azerion, a prosperous hidden city run economically by half-beast people and concealed by dense illusion fog; only veteran forest-goers and local hunter tribes know safe routes in. See the Lawless Territories module for full canon.

### 5.0 Shared Azerion–Lawless–Tianhai–Beiming geographic links

- The western side of Tianhai Yunzhou's floating position above the southwestern ocean faces a sea-side approach toward the **Lawless Territories**. The Lawless Territories contain Azerion Forest's easiest known entrance.
- **Azerion Forest** extends from Lawless Territories into **Golden Dragon Empire** territory. At the empire-facing boundary, a rushing river forms a trap-like barrier, with a small Golden Dragon Empire prison island in the middle of the fast-water route. No river name, crossing, or island coordinates are established.
- From **Beiming Gu**, the Azerion connection is along the northern coast to the forest's ruined/labyrinth exit. That exit connects to the underground ruins and Mega Colossal Labyrinth reached from the enormous central lake. It is not a normal road, and the labyrinth's interior remains unmapped.
- Keep these as three different approaches: **Tianhai west → Lawless Territories → easiest forest entrance; Azerion Forest → rushing-river trap / prison island → Golden Dragon Empire; Beiming northern coast → ruined/labyrinth exit of Azerion Forest.** Do not collapse them into one road or infer a safe crossing.

## 6. Wider Wilderness and Transport

Forests, mountain ranges, ravines, valleys, remote basins, and high ranges rising above the sea of clouds can exist between the major powers. The remote highest peaks shelter two distinct hidden groups: the Ancient Shamanism clan/sect and a Buddhist clan. Their established details are recorded in `world/ANCIENT_SHAMANISM_AND_BUDDHIST_CLANS.md`. Exact coordinates, mountain names, access routes, and territorial boundaries remain unknown; these groups are not automatically part of any of the three major powers. Deep, difficult-to-reach wilderness generally tends to hold higher-tier Spirit Beasts, but this is not an absolute rule. Ruins from past eras may occur throughout the world, including in deep ocean trenches. Unexplored ruins have no pre-existing complete maps; only sections actually surveyed and recorded in play may be mapped. See `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md` for the exploration and hidden-treasure rules.

Horses, trained beasts, and caravans pulled by land beasts are common transport. Some caravans use flying beasts; conventional flying ships and Tianhai Yunzhou's specialized liquefied-energy passenger aircraft are distinct transport classes. The latter has approved corridors from Longyuan and Beiming Heartland to Tianhai Yunzhou and an approved round-trip fare of 25 xc per passenger, compared with 86 xc for a comparable Qi-powered flying ship journey. Exact speeds and travel times remain unknown. See `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md` and `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md` for the full foundation.

## 7. Wanluo Sacred Expanse — World Knowledge Tower Region

The **Wanluo Sacred Expanse** is a sacred region centered on the World Knowledge Tower, an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age. It consists of a broad green plain surrounded by ancient forests and mountains. The exact coordinates, borders, sovereignty, and position relative to the three major powers remain unestablished; do not assign the region to any major power without a later owner decision.

Its pilgrimage roads developed gradually through repeated travel and converge on the Tower across generations. Four connected settlement districts are recognized: Pilgrim, Scholar, Merchant, and Administration. Access to the region is open under local rules with guarding and checks, but access to the region does not grant unrestricted access to the Tower or restricted facilities.

Ordo Suci Tianming, the world's largest religious organization by follower count in the current setting, maintains its headquarters—the Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows—within the wider region, separate from the Tower. The Shen family is one of several parties with rights and responsibilities in administering the Tower; it does not own the relic. See `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md` for the full geographic and institutional reference.
