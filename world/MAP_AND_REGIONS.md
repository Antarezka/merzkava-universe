**Status:** Regional reference module.  
**Canon policy:** Established facts are separated from geographic details that still require confirmation. This file organizes known regions; it does not establish precise coordinates, distances, travel times, or borders that have not been defined. ## 1. World Map: Current Geographic Model

Merzkava Universe is built around one vast continent with connected seas and maritime routes. The current world foundation identifies three major powers:

1. **Golden Dragon Empire** — eastern imperial power.
2. **Tianhai Yunzhou** — maritime power centered on a giant floating island.
3. **Beiming Gu** — northern power protected by mountains and severe cold.

The North is comparatively isolated by terrain and climate. The sea connects distant territories and supports travel, trade, diplomacy, and conflict. Lawless Territories exist outside the direct jurisdiction of the three major powers.

Golden Dragon Empire lies in the east, Tianhai Yunzhou floats above the southwestern ocean, and Beiming Gu lies in the north. Large tracts of wilderness and difficult terrain separate the major powers. The sea names remain `???` pending an official naming decision. A precise world map, scale, compass bearings between all regions, and verified travel distances have not yet been established. Do not draw conclusions about exact proximity or travel duration from this outline alone.

### 2.1 Imperial centralization and rural access

Development follows a gradient around the imperial center. Huanglongcheng and the Longyuan core have the densest infrastructure, the strongest civil presence, the broadest selection of medical and commercial services, and the easiest access to elite education and skilled specialists. Major regional seats retain useful services, but the quality and frequency of service generally decline farther from the capital, especially off major roads, rivers, and trade routes.

Outer areas are not uniformly poor: ports, mines, and furnace towns can prosper from trade or resources. However, their hinterlands may be neglected, with fewer public offices, longer waits for travelling physicians, fewer schools and libraries, limited markets, weaker road maintenance, and greater dependence on family, village elders, local craftsmen, and community cooperation. A remote village must not be treated as if it has the same amenities as a prefectural seat. See the atlas and population-services register for location-specific limits.

The atlas currently names **13 rural villages across the seven regions** as roleplay anchors. This is a compact register, not a census of every hamlet or farmstead. The former counts of 33 prefectures, 268 counties, 361 cities, 1,838 towns, and 22 villages are superseded; use the atlas for the current 12-prefecture / 6-county-circuit / 13-village structure.

## 3. Tianhai Yunzhou — Maritime Power

Tianhai Yunzhou is centered on a giant floating island sustained by a Tier 9 Artificial Artifact. Its power depends on maritime governance, navigation, trade, defense, and advanced Thinker knowledge.

The following regional and clan information is established in `world/WORLD.md`:

- **Shuijing Clan:** materials processing and craftsmanship.
- **Leiyu Clan:** combat techniques and offensive power.
- **Tianhai Dojos:** Skywhale Dojo, Tidal Current Dojo, Stormwake Dojo, Azure Sail Dojo, Deepwater Dojo.

The floating island's precise coordinates, internal district map, neighboring island routes, and artifact maintenance sites are not yet defined. Do not invent these as settled canon.

## 6. Wider Wilderness and Transport

Forests, mountain ranges, ravines, valleys, remote basins, and high ranges rising above the sea of clouds can exist between the major powers. Some remote sky-high mountain communities are associated with ancient shamanic traditions and Buddhist monks; names and specific organizations remain open. Deep, difficult-to-reach wilderness generally tends to hold higher-tier Spirit Beasts, but this is not an absolute rule. Ruins from past eras may occur throughout the world, including in deep ocean trenches.

Horses, trained beasts, and caravans pulled by land beasts are common transport. Some caravans use flying beasts; flying ships require a Tier 5 or higher Artificial Artifact of the Utilities type with a Levitation function. These transport types do not establish exact speed, price, or availability. See `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md` for the full foundation.

## 7. Wanluo Sacred Expanse — World Knowledge Tower Region

The **Wanluo Sacred Expanse** is a sacred region centered on the World Knowledge Tower, an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age. It consists of a broad green plain surrounded by ancient forests and mountains. The exact coordinates, borders, sovereignty, and position relative to the three major powers remain unestablished; do not assign the region to any major power without a later owner decision.

Its pilgrimage roads developed gradually through repeated travel and converge on the Tower across generations. Four connected settlement districts are recognized: Pilgrim, Scholar, Merchant, and Administration. Access to the region is open under local rules with guarding and checks, but access to the region does not grant unrestricted access to the Tower or restricted facilities.

Ordo Suci Tianming, the world's largest religious organization by follower count in the current setting, maintains its headquarters—the Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows—within the wider region, separate from the Tower. The Shen family is one of several parties with rights and responsibilities in administering the Tower; it does not own the relic. See `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md` for the full geographic and institutional reference.
