# Merzkava Universe — Beiming Han and His Adopted Children

**Status:** Owner-confirmed character canon.
**Scope:** Beiming Han, Beiming Xenhwa, Beiming Jingheng, and the nighttime incident between the adopted siblings.
**Authority:** This file records the owner's explicit decisions. Follow it over any older summary or assistant-generated description that conflicts with it. Do not add unconfirmed consequences or actions.

## 1. Beiming Han — Northern Ancestor

- Beiming Han is the Northern Ancestor of Beiming Gu and the son of Beiming Gu's founder.
- His age is intentionally unknown or undisclosed.
- He has no wife.
- He is the strongest Spirit Realm Primordial of the current era.
- He loves both of his adopted children, Beiming Xenhwa and Beiming Jingheng, equally.
- He is immune to the naturally alluring scent and beauty effect emitted by Xenhwa.
- **Do not attribute Xenhwa's white flame to Han.** Xenhwa owns that ability.
- During the incident recorded below, Han intervened to stop Xenhwa from killing Jingheng. Han did not burn Jingheng.

His precise method of intervention in that incident is not specified beyond stopping Xenhwa. Do not invent a separate attack, punishment, or technique.

## 2. Beiming Xenhwa

- **Age:** 17.
- **Identity:** Beiming Han's adopted daughter; a half-beast with the bloodline of a white nine-tailed fox.
- **Temperament:** Humble and vigilant.
- **Natural traits:** Her body passively emits an alluring fragrance. She inherited the white nine-tailed fox's exceptionally rare celestial beauty, which can cause people to become obsessed with her.
- **Sensitivity:** Her senses are exceptionally sensitive; this allowed her to detect Jingheng's attempted misconduct at night.
- **Ability:** She wields white flame whose temperature approaches the Sun's temperature, described by the owner as being only a few percent short of it.
- **Relationship with Han:** She greatly admires him because he is unaffected by her natural alluring scent and beauty.

The alluring effect is a character trait, not permission to assume that every person automatically loses free will. Its exact mechanics beyond the established description remain unspecified.

## 3. Beiming Jingheng

- **Age:** 17.
- **Identity:** Beiming Han's adopted human son.
- **Temperament and conduct:** Corrupt, highly ambitious, and a womanizer; he frequently visits nighttime entertainment pavilions.
- **Secret connections:** He has strong, concealed connections with high-level criminal organizations in the Lawless Territories.
- **Fear of Han:** He does not dare openly rebel against Han because Han's power is overwhelming.
- **Relationship with Xenhwa:** Their relationship is poor.

## 4. Canonical Account — The Nighttime Incident

The sequence of events is fixed as follows:

1. At night, Jingheng secretly attempted to grope Xenhwa, intending to commit an indecent act against her.
2. Xenhwa caught him red-handed because her senses are exceptionally sensitive.
3. Xenhwa flew into a rage and attacked Jingheng with **her own white flame**.
4. Jingheng came close to dying from Xenhwa's attack.
5. Beiming Han intervened and stopped Xenhwa before she killed Jingheng, preventing the night from becoming a bloody tragedy.

### Non-negotiable continuity rules

- Jingheng initiated the misconduct; Xenhwa did not consent.
- Xenhwa's white flame is the power used against Jingheng.
- Han intervened to stop Xenhwa. **Han did not burn Jingheng.**
- Do not reverse the roles, merge Han and Xenhwa into one person, or describe Han's intervention as him attacking Jingheng.
- Han's equal love for both adopted children does not mean he approves of Jingheng's conduct, excuses it, or requires Xenhwa to forgive him.
- Jingheng's punishment, restrictions, and subsequent living arrangements have **not** been established. Do not invent them as canon.
- The exact technique or physical method Han used to stop Xenhwa has **not** been established. Do not invent one.
