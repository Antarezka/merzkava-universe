# Ying Wuxin — 嬴无心

Owner-approved NPC profile. Concealed leader of Crimson Sovereign Court (赤尊宫 · Chì Zūn Gōng), which exercises shadow authority over Crimson Keel Brotherhood.

- Spirit Realm: Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial; exact stage unknown.
- Martial Soul: Asura — Myth.
- Personality: Cold, controlled, sadistic.
- Signature ability: Soul Illusion, a coercive soul-based power that can invert a target's perception of core traits and emotions. Apparent affection or loyalty under its influence is not consent and must not be treated as the victim's genuine wishes.
- Method: He may allow targets to recognize the manipulation and attempt resistance or escape, only for the effect to reassert control and turn their action into submission and displayed affection.
- Concealment: He hides his identity, abilities, and possible weaknesses by choice, not because he is weak. His shadow influence is comparable in reach to an empire, but he is not a formal state or fourth major power.
- Unknown: Age, species, origin, exact location, ring configuration, ability limits/counters, Court roster, and detailed governance.
- Continuity: Do not assume every Brotherhood member knows his identity or every Lawless organization serves him. Victims' outward affection must never be treated as voluntary consent.

## Two Lives — Restricted GM Canon

### Public life in the Golden Dragon Empire
- Ying Wuxin lives in the Golden Dragon Empire under his ordinary public identity as the owner of a clothing pavilion serving elite clientele.
- In this life, he is known as a deeply affectionate and indulgent father to his daughter, born to him and his late wife. He spoils her so much that she sometimes feels embarrassed or irritated by the attention.
- His daughter often visits him during academy holidays. She is a friend of the imperial princess and belongs to her social circle.
- The pavilion's name, exact city or district, his daughter's name and age, and the identity/details of the late wife are not yet established. Do not invent them.

### Private truth
- Ying Wuxin's affection for his biological daughter is genuine. His tenderness toward her is not an act or a product of Soul Illusion.
- His daughter does not know that her father is the concealed leader of Crimson Sovereign Court and a high-level criminal power behind the Crimson Keel Brotherhood.
- He has never used Soul Illusion on his daughter, and she has never been touched or influenced by that ability. This is an absolute continuity boundary, not merely a claim that she has not noticed it.
- Keep the two lives separate in ordinary public knowledge. Do not expose his hidden identity to his daughter, her academy friends, the imperial princess, or other characters without an owner-approved development or credible in-world discovery.
- His real love for his daughter does not redeem, excuse, or erase his cruelty and coercion toward other people. His daughter's apparent safety from his power must not be generalized to his other victims.
