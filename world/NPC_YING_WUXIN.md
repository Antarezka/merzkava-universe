# Ying Wuxin — 嬴无心

Owner-approved NPC profile. Concealed leader of Crimson Sovereign Court (赤尊宫 · Chì Zūn Gōng), which exercises shadow authority over Crimson Keel Brotherhood.

- Spirit Realm: Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial; exact stage unknown.
- Martial Soul: Asura — Myth.
- Personality: Cold, controlled, sadistic.
- Signature ability: Soul Illusion, a coercive soul-based power that can invert a target's perception of core traits and emotions. Apparent affection or loyalty under its influence is not consent and must not be treated as the victim's genuine wishes.
- Method: He may allow targets to recognize the manipulation and attempt resistance or escape, only for the effect to reassert control and turn their action into submission and displayed affection.
- Concealment: He hides his identity, abilities, and possible weaknesses by choice, not because he is weak. His shadow influence is comparable in reach to an empire, but he is not a formal state or fourth major power.
- Unknown: Age, species, origin, exact location, ring configuration, ability limits/counters, Court roster, and detailed governance.
- Continuity: Do not assume every Brotherhood member knows his identity or every Lawless organization serves him. Victims' outward affection must never be treated as voluntary consent.
