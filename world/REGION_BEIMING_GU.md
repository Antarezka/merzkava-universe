## 1. Confirmed Foundation

- **Power:** Beiming Gu, the northern major power.
- **Geographic character:** Northern territory associated with severe cold, mountains, forests, highlands, valleys, and difficult mountain crossings.
- **Known leader:** Beiming Han, the Leluhur Utara and highest known figure of Beiming Gu; Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial; Blue-Flame Silver Tiger Martial Soul. Consult his canonical NPC profile for restricted details.
- **Known family members:** Beiming Xenhwa and Beiming Jingheng are Beiming Han's adopted children. Beiming Nerva is Beiming Han's older sister. Consult their canonical profiles and the regional NPC registry; do not infer their current exact locations.
- **Established regional structure:** Seven major districts, listed in §3.
- **Canonical overview:** `world/WORLD.md` and `world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md`.
- **Regional NPC directory:** `regions/beiming-gu/INDEX.md` → `regions/beiming-gu/npcs/REGISTRY.md`.

Beiming Gu is a single major power with seven established regional divisions. The districts are not seven separate countries or GitHub repositories.

### Northern maritime connection

Beiming Gu's northern coastal waters are the **Frostmirror Sea (霜镜海)**. Its eastern/southeastern maritime margin connects around the mainland's northeastern sea passage to the **Eastern Azure Sea (东青海)** of Golden Dragon Empire. This is a sea route, not a road through the mountain barrier. The Azerion ruins / Mega Colossal Labyrinth exit on the northern deep-sea coast is a dangerous ruined exit, not a normal harbor or public dock. The route framework continues through Eastern Azure Sea toward Yunmeng Sea and Tianhai Yunzhou; Guixu Canghai lies at the far-southern edge of the world beyond ordinary Black Tide Sea, not directly beside Beiming.

- **Broad terrain:** Mountain chains, cold forests, highlands, valleys, and difficult crossings are part of the established northern geographic model.
- **Exact coordinates, borders, area, population, and distances:** Belum ditetapkan.
- **Exact adjacency between the seven districts:** Belum ditetapkan.

- **Travel:** Do not infer a route, direct connection, duration, or transport service from district names or terrain descriptions. Use `world/LOCATION_AND_ROUTE_NETWORK.md`, `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`, and `gameplay/TIME_AND_ACTION_RULES.md`.

A dedicated **Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal** is the established Beiming endpoint for two direct liquefied-energy passenger-aircraft corridors: one to **Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal** in Tianhai Yunzhou and one to **Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal** in the Golden Dragon Empire. The Golden Dragon–Beiming service uses the same Beiming destination terminal as the Tianhai–Beiming service; a transfer through Tianhai Yunzhou is not required. The standard round-trip fare is **25 xc per passenger** on each dedicated energy-aircraft corridor. Service operates 05:00–21:00 local terminal time; exact coordinates, flight duration, detailed schedule, and intermediate stops are not established. The air corridors alone do not establish a road or rail route. Separately, the owner-requested perspective map now records an overland corridor framework from Beiming Heartland through Stonegate March and the Nine Frost Wastes to Beiguan in Baiyue; its exact pass, local road segments, settlements, and duration remain unspecified.
