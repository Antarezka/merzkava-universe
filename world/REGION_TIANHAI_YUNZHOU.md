## 1. Confirmed Foundation

- **Power / territory:** Tianhai Yunzhou, a maritime power centered on a giant floating island above the southwestern ocean.
- **Population estimate:** 85 million for Tianhai Yunzhou as a whole. This is a worldbuilding estimate, not a census or a claim that all residents live on the main island.
- **Ruler:** Maharaja Ji Haoyuan; his canonical family roster is established in `../regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/JI_HAOYUAN.md`, `../regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/BAI_XUANYIN.md`, `../regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/JI_ZHAONING.md`, and `../regions/tianhai-yunzhou/npcs/JI_NINGZHAO.md`.
- **Ruler's established profile:** Martial Soul — Heavenly Sky Whale; Spirit Realm 9 — Spirit Primordial, Early Stage.
- **Strategic strengths:** Maritime trade, navigation, defense, island and sea-route logistics, and advanced Thinker knowledge.
- **Island support:** A Tier 9 Artificial Artifact sustains the giant floating island. It is powered by processed liquefied energy refined from a specific class of energy-bearing stone/metal ore. The processing method and fuel are strategically controlled by Tianhai Yunzhou; the original maker was an ancestor of Maharaja Ji Haoyuan. The artifact's internal mechanism, exact materials, maintenance requirements, weaknesses, and authorized operators remain restricted or unconfirmed.
- **Knowledge tradition:** The first Thinkers originated in Tianhai Yunzhou. This establishes a historical and intellectual advantage, not a monopoly on all Thinker knowledge or automatic superiority in every artifact-making task.
- **Canonical overview:** `world/WORLD.md`; geographic scope and travel cautions: `world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md`; faction summaries: `world/FACTIONS.md`.

## 4. Local Society and Daily Life

- **Population:** Overall estimate 85 million; distribution between the main island, outer islands, ships, and other settlements is not established.
- **Known broad livelihoods:** Maritime trade, navigation, shipping, seafood and coastal resources, processing of marine goods, crafts, Artificial Artifact manufacturing/maintenance, liquefied-energy processing, transit operations, and technical services.
- **Urban character:** The main island's core urban districts are noticeably more modern and infrastructure-dense than the orthodox cities of the Golden Dragon Empire. Automated Artificial Artifact-powered trains, lifts, public lighting, cargo handling, and other utility systems exist where documented below; access and service quality vary by district and income.
- **Social structure:** Specialist technical workers and Thinkers are economically important, but the population also includes sailors, fishers, vendors, food producers, repair crews, porters, clerks, students, guards, and low-income residents. Modern infrastructure does not mean universal comfort or equal access.
- **Local realism:** Do not assume every resident is a sailor, cultivator, Thinker, or member of an elite family. Everyday occupations and access to advanced facilities should vary by district, income, skill, and institutional affiliation.

## 5. Economy and Resources

- **Established economic foundations:** Maritime commerce, navigation and shipping, marine/coastal resources, processing of catches, artifact industries, automated transit, and energy refining.
- **Strategic energy advantage:** Tianhai Yunzhou controls the specialized refining and distribution process that turns certain imported stone/metal ores into a stable liquefied energy medium usable by compatible Artificial Artifact engines. This refining capability—not ownership of every raw deposit—is the basis of its energy monopoly and a major source of wealth.
- **Inter-power dependence:** The ore-bearing stone and metals are sourced from other powers and territories, including suppliers under the Golden Dragon Empire and Beiming Gu. Tianhai Yunzhou provides refined energy, advanced artifact knowledge, shipping/logistics services, and technical expertise in return. Specific mines, supplier contracts, quotas, prices, and named foreign partners remain unassigned until documented.
- **Economic vulnerability:** A disruption to ore imports, refining facilities, specialized containment, or sea routes can constrain trains, industrial machinery, harbor systems, and the floating island's support infrastructure. The monopoly is powerful but not risk-free.
- **Fixed prices, wages, taxes, quotas, and export/import volumes:** Not established; apply `core/10_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md` and do not invent exact values.
- Apply `core/10_ECONOMY_SYSTEM.md` for currency, prices, transactions, and provenance. Do not invent fixed prices or trade volumes as canon without approval.

### Great Tianhai War — retained regional canon

- The Great Tianhai War was one major front of an apocalyptic, world-spanning incursion. Extreme anomalies opened gates to other worlds and the sky became a passage through which Demonic Beasts of tiers 7–9 descended across the world. Three Tier 9 Demonic Beasts specifically sought to invade and devastate Tianhai Yunzhou; their identities remain unknown.

### 12.3 Artificial Artifact transport and energy

Tianhai Yunzhou uses Artificial Artifacts not only for specialist manufacturing but also for public infrastructure and transport. The most visible urban system is an automated train network on dedicated guideways: its engine is an Artificial Artifact, the train follows a prepared route without a conventional animal or human pulling it, and its propulsion is supplied by a specialized energy vessel containing refined liquefied energy. Operators and technicians still inspect guideways, manage stations, load cargo, and maintain the system; “automated” does not mean maintenance-free or universally accessible.

For inter-power travel, Tianhai Yunzhou also operates a distinct class of high-capacity long-distance energy-aircraft through the Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal. These are Artificial Artifact-powered passenger transports that use Tianhai Yunzhou-refined liquefied energy, not the Qi propulsion used by conventional flying ships. They are designed to carry more passengers per journey than an ordinary Qi-powered flying ship and run on the two established corridors from Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal in the Golden Dragon Empire and Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal in Beiming Gu. **Regular energy-aircraft service hours are 05:00–21:00 local terminal time.** The standard round-trip passenger fare is **25 xc** on this dedicated energy-aircraft service, compared with **86 xc** for an ordinary Qi-powered flying ship on a comparable inter-power journey. The fare is per passenger for the return journey, not per aircraft. When the energy-aircraft service is closed, ordinary Qi-powered flying ships remain available **24 hours a day**, subject to normal access and operating conditions; their higher fare makes them the more expensive after-hours alternative. Exact seating capacity, speed, flight duration, departure frequency, fleet size, and ownership arrangements remain unestablished.

The liquefied energy is produced by Tianhai Yunzhou-controlled refining Artificial Artifacts from specific energy-bearing stone/metal ores imported from other territories. The raw ore itself is not a Tianhai Yunzhou-exclusive natural resource. Tianhai Yunzhou's competitive advantage is its specialized refining apparatus, technical knowledge, safe containment, and distribution network. Compatible engines and utility artifacts elsewhere may use the refined energy only if designed for it; no universal compatibility is implied.

The Tier 9 island-support Artificial Artifact uses this energy system at strategic scale. Its detailed mechanism, fuel consumption, reserve capacity, redundancy, and failure conditions remain unknown. It must not be treated as an ordinary engine or as a portable unlimited power source.
