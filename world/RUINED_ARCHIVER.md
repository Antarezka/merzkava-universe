# Merzkava Universe — Ruined Archiver

**Canonical status:** Owner-established organization branch of Ordo Suci Tianming.  
**Scope:** Golden Age Ruined intelligence, expedition registration, mapping, verification, fair compensation, confidential records, and early warning of anomalies that could threaten the world.  
**Related canon:** `world/FACTIONS.md`, `world/HISTORY.md`, `world/WANLUO_SACRED_EXPANSE.md`, `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`, `world/LAWLESS_TERRITORIES.md`, `world/CANON_DECISIONS_AND_PRECEDENCE.md`.

## 1. Purpose and Historical Reason

Ruined Archiver is a specialist branch of Ordo Suci Tianming. It exists because the Ordo's world-survival mandate is to help the world detect dangerous anomalies as early as possible. The ancient world-calamity incursion proved that an anomaly can deviate far beyond safe limits, open a gate to another world, and allow catastrophic Demonic Beasts to descend. A Golden Age Ruined may contain traces, mechanisms, entities, or anomalies connected to such dangers, so even a seemingly ordinary expedition can contribute to the world's early-warning network.

Ruined Archiver does not assume that every Ruined contains an anomaly or that every anomaly is apocalyptic. It collects evidence, verifies reports, preserves uncertainty, and escalates credible warning signs to the Ordo's assessment channels and, when warranted, the World Knowledge Tower alliance.

## 2. Relationship to Ordo Suci Tianming and the World Knowledge Tower

- Ruined Archiver is a branch of Ordo Suci Tianming, not an independent organization or a guild.
- The Ordo oversees the broader mission of early detection and coordination concerning threats to the world's survival.
- Ruined Archiver specializes in information originating from Golden Age Ruined: expedition registration, maps, exploration records, witness reports, artifacts or inscriptions relevant to a site's history, and anomaly indicators.
- The World Knowledge Tower and its alliance of elite envoys from the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu are responsible for high-level world-threat assessment and intervention within their mandate. They may deploy members capable of sealing or destroying a confirmed dangerous anomaly.
- The alliance's formal name, full membership roster, internal ranks, and response procedures beyond this mandate remain unspecified.
- Ruined Archiver does not replace the World Knowledge Tower administration council, which has separate responsibilities for the Tower relic's access, protection, upkeep, and authorized study. The Shen family remains one of several parties entrusted with administrative rights and responsibilities over the relic; the Tower is not owned by the family or by the Ordo.

## 3. Branch Network and Appearance

- Ruined Archiver maintains a branch in every city.
- Members of the organization wear a matching light-brown uniform. Individual rank insignia, exact garment design, materials, and formal dress codes are not yet specified.
- Branches handle local registration, intake of reports, initial recordkeeping, compensation processing, and coordination with field verifiers.
- The existence of a branch does not mean every Ruined in the surrounding area is publicly listed or fully known. Many locations and records are confidential.

## 4. Expedition Registration and Witnessing

- An individual or group registers its Ruined expedition with Ruined Archiver before entering the site if it wants the expedition to count as officially recognized exploration experience.
- Exploration hours and official field experience are credited only for expeditions registered before exploration begins. Retroactive registration does not automatically grant official credit.
- A designated Ruined Archiver officer accompanies the expedition only as far as the Ruined's entrance gate. The officer is not a member of the exploration party and does not accompany the explorers inside.
- The officer's presence establishes the registration and departure record and may provide a legitimate witness to the expedition's start. Subsequent findings still require evidence, testimony, or verification; the organization does not treat an unobserved claim as automatically true.
- Ruined Archiver staff may act as official witnesses for records under their remit. Their witness role does not make them expedition leaders, bodyguards, or guarantors of safety.
- Guilds remain fully independent. A guild or party may organize itself and pursue its own objectives; registering with Ruined Archiver serves the separate purposes of official exploration credit, records, verification, and anomaly monitoring.

## 5. Maps, Reports, and Fair Compensation

Ruined Archiver pays fairly for useful information about Ruined, including information about a site that is still unexplored or unmapped. A report remains valuable even if the expedition discovers no treasure.

Eligible information can include, where relevant:
- A newly located Ruined or reliable evidence of its location.
- Surveyed routes, mapped rooms, measured passages, entrances, exits, and clearly marked unknown areas.
- Hazards, creatures, mechanisms, inscriptions, artifacts, environmental changes, and repeatable observations.
- Evidence suggesting an anomaly or a change in a previously recorded site.

Compensation reflects the information's usefulness, reliability, novelty, and level of detail. No universal price table or fixed bounty is established here.

### 5.1 Map Verification and Payment

- When a map or report is submitted for sale, Ruined Archiver provides an advance payment.
- A Ruined Archiver member then verifies the submitted map or information against available evidence and the claimed level of detail.
- Once verification is completed, the organization pays the full remaining balance owed for the verified material.
- Verification must not require an explorer to reveal unrelated personal secrets or surrender unrelated property.
- A map must distinguish surveyed areas from inferred passages and unknown sections. A partial map must never be presented as a complete map.
- The existence of a payment offer does not guarantee that an unverified claim is accepted at face value.

## 6. Confidentiality and Site Access

- Information about Ruined, expeditions, maps, buyers, informants, and possible anomalies is treated as highly confidential. Access is limited according to operational need and the sensitivity of the material.
- The organization does not publicly disclose every known Ruined. Public notices may be limited, incomplete, or absent when confidentiality or safety requires it.
- Ruined Archiver can advise explorers about hazards, recommend restrictions, and coordinate with relevant local authorities when a site presents a credible danger.
- Access restrictions and enforcement depend on applicable local law and the authority of the relevant territory. Ruined Archiver has no universal power to prohibit everyone from entering every Ruined across the world.
- A warning or recommendation is not itself proof that a Ruined contains an apocalyptic anomaly. Claims must be assessed and verified proportionately to the evidence.

## 7. Ruined and Exploration Continuity

- More than one thousand Golden Age Ruined are scattered throughout the world. Their locations, conditions, contents, and degree of exploration vary.
- An unexplored Ruined has no complete internal map available in advance. Mapping must be based on areas actually surveyed and recorded.
- Entering a Ruined does not guarantee hidden treasure. Where a hidden treasure chamber exists, its specific trigger conditions must be discovered and fulfilled.
- A mapped or partly mapped Ruined may be used as an exploration-training site, including by enthusiasts and early Spirit Realm experts, if its actual hazards and access conditions support that use.
- Some mapped Ruined have already had their hidden chambers or principal rewards exhausted. Such sites may still offer ordinary finds, exploration practice, or Spirit Beast hunting opportunities where the local ecology supports them.
- A Ruined's map status, reward status, hazard assessment, and anomaly status are separate fields. A mapped site is not automatically safe; an exhausted treasure chamber does not prove the site has no anomaly; an unexplored site is not automatically the highest possible risk.
- Ruined Archiver records verified discoveries and preserves earlier records rather than secretly changing site conditions to force an outcome.

## 8. Limits and Unspecified Details

The following remain unestablished and must not be invented as fixed canon without a further owner decision: the Ordo's formal hierarchy; Ruined Archiver's internal rank names; numeric license thresholds; universal minimum Spirit Realm requirements or party sizes; fixed risk-class labels; standard pay rates; exact verification duration; branch staffing numbers; a universal right to seal sites; and the formal name or complete roster of the World Knowledge Tower alliance.

## 9. GM Continuity Rules

- Use this file alongside `world/HISTORY.md`, `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`, and the relevant regional or Lawless Territories module.
- Keep public knowledge, confidential institutional records, individual NPC knowledge, hypotheses, and verified findings distinct.
- Do not grant Ruined Archiver's staff automatic knowledge of every Ruined or every anomaly.
- Do not make guilds subordinate to Ruined Archiver.
- Do not make the organization a universal police force or give it legal powers beyond the applicable local authority.
- Do not use Ruined Archiver to detect or punish meta-level player cheating. It has no access to the GM's anti-cheat log and cannot infer a violation from narration alone.
- If a character submits a false or altered Ruined map/report, handle the submission through the established verification and compensation process. Record what the branch can actually establish; refusal of payment or further reporting depends on evidence and established procedure, not an invented automatic punishment.
- Escalate a case to local authorities only when the observed conduct plausibly violates local law and the branch has a credible basis to report it. Route information to the wider Ordo only when verified evidence indicates a threat within the Ordo's world-survival/anomaly remit.
- When credible signs indicate a world-level anomaly, route the information through Ordo Suci Tianming's assessment network for potential World Knowledge Tower alliance intervention.
