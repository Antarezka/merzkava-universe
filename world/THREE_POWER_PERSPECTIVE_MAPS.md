# Merzkava Universe — Three-Power Perspective Maps

**Purpose:** Three matching route-line maps, one from each major power's perspective, showing internal regional structure, land corridors, mixed land/river/sea journeys, wilderness crossings, and the three established air corridors.
**Map status:** Owner-requested connected geographic framework. This is a relative schematic, not a scale map; it does not define coordinates, distances, journey times, or exact borders.
**Route status:** Existing confirmed connections retain their established status. The land and maritime corridors below are mapped travel frameworks requested for the world map; exact local pathing, access, safety, schedules, and travel times remain conditional or unspecified. They do not establish unnamed settlements, checkpoints, fees, or precise crossings.
**Orientation:** Golden Dragon Empire is east; Beiming Gu is north; Tianhai Yunzhou floats above Yunmeng Sea in the southwest. Frostmirror Sea off Beiming connects around the northeastern maritime margin to the Eastern Azure Sea off Golden Dragon; the eastern shipping lane connects to Yunmeng Sea and Cangqiong Harbor. Wufeng Sea is a distinct still-water basin reached from Yunmeng Sea through Lantern Passage and linked toward Eastern Azure Sea by Three-Lantern Passage. West/southwest of Tianhai lie Lawless Territories and ordinary Black Tide Sea; beyond its southern outer boundary lies Guixu Canghai, the far-southern, literally frigid, most dangerous ocean. Lawless Territories are the nearest major organized region to Guixu Canghai but are separated by Black Tide Sea. Lawless Territories and the Wanluo Sacred Expanse remain outside the direct jurisdiction of the three powers.

## Reading key

- **━━** established internal route / administrative corridor.
- **══** mapped cross-region land corridor; exact local road segments and duration are not specified.
- **~~~** maritime leg or water crossing.
- **⇧ AIR ⇧** established dedicated liquefied-energy passenger-aircraft corridor.
- **···** wilderness or difficult-terrain passage; danger and passability depend on conditions.
- **[EXTERNAL]** territory outside the direct jurisdiction of the three powers.
- A line shows a connected travel corridor, not a straight-line road or guaranteed safe passage.

### Golden Dragon perspective — maritime layer

- **Eastern Azure Sea (东青海):** the empire's eastern coastal waters. Dongxing Port is the primary inter-power wharf within Dongxing, Xingluo; Mingzhu, Lanfan, Chaoguan, Yuhai, Yuandeng, and Longchao are established ports on the same maritime belt.
- **Route to Tianhai:** Dongxing Port → Eastern Azure Sea → eastern open-water passage → Yunmeng Sea → Cangqiong Harbor. This ordinary shipping corridor does not require passage through Wufeng Sea.
- **Local coastal links:** Dongxing → Pearl Shoal Hamlet by small coastal boat; Lanfan → Wind Sail Hamlet by coastal route; Chaoguan → Tideglass Hamlet by small boat. Weather and tides can alter or suspend these local services. Yanfeng is a coastal settlement, but no port status is assumed.
- **Wufeng Sea (无风海):** reached from Yunmeng Sea through Lantern Passage and linked toward Eastern Azure Sea through Three-Lantern Passage. It has no wind, waves, or currents; specialized liquefied-energy Artificial Artifact vessels are favored because ambushes are uncommon.
- **Southern waters:** ordinary Black Tide Sea lies beyond the Lawless-facing approach. Guixu Canghai lies farther south beyond Black Tide Sea's outer boundary, not beside the empire's coast.
- Tianhai's sea-facing approach toward Lawless remains distinct from the Golden Dragon-facing Azerion river trap and prison-island boundary.

### Golden Dragon → Beiming Gu

**Main mapped land corridor:** Beiguan (Baiyue) → outer northern wilderness of the Nine Frost Wastes → mountain foothills and snow valleys → Stonegate March → Frostveil Range → Beiming Heartland.

- The Nine Frost Wastes remain outside Beiming Gu's official defenses; passing through them is not the same as entering Beiming jurisdiction.
- The land corridor crosses exposed, cold terrain. Seasonal conditions, Spirit Beast activity, patrols, and local access can affect passage.
- This land corridor is separate from the direct air service Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal ↔ Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal.
- Exact road alignment, crossings, settlements en route, distance, and duration remain unspecified.

### Golden Dragon → Tianhai Yunzhou

**Connections to Tianhai Yunzhou and the western Lawless approach:** (A) the maritime itinerary from Xingluo / Dongxing to Cangqiong Harbor; and (B) from the western side of Tianhai Yunzhou's floating position, the sea-facing approach toward Lawless Territories and Azerion Forest's easiest known entrance. Route B is distinct from the Dongxing itinerary and does not pass through Golden Dragon's prison-island river trap.

- This does not claim every Golden Dragon region has a direct road to Dongxing. Internal interregional legs must be verified in the relevant regional module; where a connection is not listed, it remains an uncharted local leg rather than an assumed road.
- Dongxing is the mapped mainland maritime departure point for this corridor; Cangqiong Harbor is the receiving harbor on Tianhai Yunzhou.
- The southwestern ocean is Yunmeng Sea. Ship schedules, exact sailing duration, fare, and weather windows remain unestablished. The ordinary Dongxing-to-Cangqiong corridor crosses Eastern Azure Sea and Yunmeng Sea; it does not enter Wufeng Sea or Guixu Canghai.
- The direct Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal ↔ Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal air corridor remains the established scheduled alternative.

### Golden Dragon internal map register

All seven regions remain part of the same imperial branch: **Longyuan, Canglan, Jinyue, Tianfeng, Yanling, Baiyue, Xingluo**. Their prefectures, six county circuits, thirteen official villages, 21 separately classified hamlets/local settlements, and named administrative corridors remain governed by the Golden Dragon administrative atlas and seven regional modules. The tree above is a route overview, not a replacement for that register.

### Tianhai perspective — maritime layer

- **Home waters:** Tianhai Yunzhou floats above Yunmeng Sea (云梦海); Cangqiong Harbor is its main maritime hub.
- **Existing harbor anchors:** Cangqiong Harbor ↔ Westwake Anchorage; Haiyue Market → Pearlstep Landing → Bluefin Cove → Windglass Islet; Xuanchao Ward → Northwatch Quay → Reedmist Fishing Village. These local links retain their established transport modes and constraints in `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md`.
- **Mainland corridor:** Cangqiong Harbor → Yunmeng Sea → Eastern Azure Sea → Dongxing Port in Xingluo, Golden Dragon Empire.
- **Wufeng Sea:** reached from Yunmeng Sea through Lantern Passage; Three-Lantern Passage links it back toward Eastern Azure Sea. Specialized liquefied-energy Artificial Artifact vessels are favored because ambushes are uncommon; the water has no wind, waves, or currents.
- **Lawless and southern approach:** Yunmeng Sea's western/southern approach reaches Lawless Territories, then ordinary Black Tide Sea. Guixu Canghai lies beyond Black Tide Sea's southern outer boundary; no route skips the intervening ordinary sea.

### Tianhai Yunzhou → Golden Dragon Empire

**To Golden Dragon Empire:** Cangqiong Harbor → vessel crossing to Dongxing → Xingluo → confirmed internal routes toward the intended destination. Separately, Azerion Forest reaches the empire-facing frontier through a rushing-river trap corridor with a small Golden Dragon Empire prison island in midstream.

**To Lawless Territories and Azerion Forest:** from the western side of Tianhai Yunzhou's floating position, follow the sea-facing approach toward Lawless Territories, then the easiest known entrance into Azerion Forest. This is not the same route as the Dongxing maritime corridor.

- Dongxing is the mapped mainland maritime access point; it is not a claim that every imperial region is directly adjacent to Xingluo.
- Continue from Xingluo only along route legs confirmed by the Golden Dragon regional sources or established through exploration.
- The established direct air alternative is Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal ↔ Longyuan Sky Transit Terminal.

### Tianhai Yunzhou → Beiming Gu and Azerion Forest

**Azerion connection:** the western side of Tianhai Yunzhou's floating position faces a sea-side approach toward Lawless Territories, where Azerion Forest's easiest known entrance lies.

**Separate Beiming journey:** Cangqiong Harbor → mainland coastal approach (exact landing unassigned) → Lawless Territories / northern wilderness → Nine Frost Wastes → Stonegate March → Frostveil Range → Beiming Heartland.

- Tianhai's western approach to Lawless Territories is not a land bridge from the floating island; the sea-side approach and exact landing remain unspecified.
- Tianhai floats above Yunmeng Sea, not Guixu Canghai or Wufeng Sea. The named passages connecting these sea systems are shown in the maritime layer above.
- The northern coast also links Beiming's side to Azerion Forest's ruined/labyrinth exit. This is distinct from the easier Lawless entrance and is not a surveyed safe road.
- A separate, established direct air corridor connects Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal ↔ Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal.

### Beiming perspective — maritime layer

- **Frostmirror Sea (霜镜海):** Beiming Gu's northern coastal waters and ordinary maritime approach. The Azerion ruins / labyrinth exit is a hazardous deep-sea feature, not a harbor.
- **Eastern connection:** Frostmirror Sea connects around the northeastern maritime margin to Eastern Azure Sea; this is a coastal/open-water passage, not a land road through the mountains.
- **Wufeng Sea:** reachable via Frostmirror Sea → Eastern Azure Sea → Three-Lantern Passage → Wufeng Sea. Do not invent a direct northern shortcut.
- **Guixu Canghai:** lies at the far southern edge of the world beyond ordinary Black Tide Sea; it is not directly adjacent to Beiming's northern coast.

### Beiming Gu → Golden Dragon Empire

**Main mapped land corridor:** Beiming Heartland → Stonegate March → northern wilderness and snow valleys of the Nine Frost Wastes → Beiguan (Baiyue) → onward through confirmed Golden Dragon routes.

- The land corridor is the overland counterpart to the direct air service; it does not replace that service.
- Beiming's seven districts are not yet assigned a full adjacency order. The map therefore shows only the districts directly relevant to this corridor and lists the others without pretending their borders or roads are surveyed.
- Exact pass names, checkpoints, settlements, duration, and seasonal closures remain unspecified.

### Beiming Gu → Azerion Forest and Tianhai Yunzhou

**Azerion coastal/ruined corridor:** Beiming Gu → northern coastal shoreline → the sea-facing northern exit of Azerion Forest's ruins / Mega Colossal Labyrinth. This is a hazardous ruined route associated with the exit from the ruins, not a normal public road or surveyed shortcut through the forest. The ruins' internal route is unknown and must be explored in play.

**Separate route to Tianhai Yunzhou:** Beiming Heartland → Stonegate March → Nine Frost Wastes → northern/western Lawless Territories → mainland coastal approach → vessel crossing to Cangqiong Harbor. Exact mainland landing and maritime path remain unassigned.

- The mainland landing point and maritime route are not yet assigned a proper name; the corridor is a geographic travel framework, not a guaranteed ship timetable.
- The established direct air service Beiming Heartland Sky Transit Terminal ↔ Cangqiong Sky Transit Terminal is the defined scheduled alternative.
- A route through Golden Dragon Empire is another possible itinerary only when its required local edges are confirmed; it is not mandatory for the direct air service.

### Beiming internal map register

The seven districts are **Beiming Heartland, Frostveil Range, Azureflame Basin, Silverpine Wilds, Stonegate March, Hollowmoon Valley, and Ironroot Reach**. The exact adjacency of these districts, settlement locations, and most internal routes remain open. Use the consolidated Beiming regional module as the authoritative source.

### Shared terrain and route logic

- **Mountains and passes** constrain land travel; a corridor may require a pass or a detour, and winter access may change.
- **Forests and valleys** may offer cover, resources, and side paths while increasing the risk of getting lost, ambush, or Spirit Beast encounters.
- **Rivers and lakes** can support boat travel or force a crossing. A named river crossing or ferry is not assumed unless a source establishes it.
- **The central lake of Azerion Forest** and the Mega Colossal Labyrinth beneath it are dangerous exploration features, not a normal public transport interchange. The labyrinth's exit opens to the northern deep-sea coast and is the ruined exit associated with the Beiming coastal approach; its internal passages remain unmapped.
- **Golden Dragon's forest boundary:** a rushing river forms a trap-like barrier; a small prison island owned by Golden Dragon Empire lies in the middle of that rushing-water route. Do not invent a river name, bridge, safe ford, island coordinates, or prison details. The labyrinth's exit opens to the northern deep-sea coast and is the ruined exit associated with the Beiming coastal approach; its internal passages remain unmapped.
- **Golden Dragon's forest boundary:** a rushing river forms a trap-like barrier; a small prison island owned by Golden Dragon Empire lies in the middle of that rushing-water route. Do not invent a river name, bridge, safe ford, island coordinates, or prison details.
- **Yunmeng Sea around Tianhai Yunzhou** must be crossed by a suitable vessel or the established energy-aircraft service; the floating island is not treated as a continuous mainland road.
- **Wufeng Sea (无风海)** has no wind, waves, or currents anywhere in the sea. Its silence is a psychological hazard; it is also a favored corridor for specialized liquefied-energy Artificial Artifact vessels because ambushes are uncommon. This does not make its deep trench or high-tier Spirit Beast habitat safe.
- **Guixu Canghai** is a distinct ocean system and the most merciless named sea. Do not place Tianhai Yunzhou above it or assign a route into it without an approved geographic connection.
- Beiming's northern coastal waters and Golden Dragon's eastern coastal waters are distinct, unnamed maritime areas. Exact straits, port locations, and connections between sea systems remain unknown.
- The route framework never implies that a route is safe, open, free, fast, or accessible to every traveler.
