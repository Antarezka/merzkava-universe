# Tianhai Yunzhou — Four Academies and the High Thinker Institute

**Status:** Canon established from the owner's regional direction.
**Scope:** Cardinal geography of the giant floating island, placement of its twelve clans, four Cultivator Academies, and the exclusive high-level Thinker research institution.
**Related sources:** `world/REGION_TIANHAI_YUNZHOU.md`, `world/NPC_TIANHAI_POWER_STRUCTURE.md`, `world/FACTIONS.md`, `core/CULTIVATOR_ACADEMIES.md`, `core/THINKER_AND_FORMULATION.md`.

## 1. Four cardinal areas of the floating island

For regional planning, the main island is divided into exactly four broad directional areas: **North, South, West, and East**. These are large geographic/cultural areas, not separate countries or independent jurisdictions. The seven districts remain the formal district structure and are a different layer of the map. Neither the central location of a landmark nor a maritime district creates a fifth cardinal area.

| Cardinal area | Districts and placement | General character |
|---|---|---|
| North | Xuanchao Ward; the northern approaches to the island | Security, maritime surveillance, defensive preparation, and monitoring access to outer waters |
| South | Yunxiao Terrace and Linggong District | Government, major learning institutions, crafts, advanced workshops, and Thinker development |
| West | Cangqiong Harbor; western-facing maritime access | Shipping, fleet logistics, sailors, navigation, and the movement of people and cargo |
| East | Haiyue Market; eastern-facing commercial approaches | Trade, specialist markets, high-value goods, information exchange, and commercial services |

**Tianji Core** is the centrally situated district associated with the floating island's support system. “Central” describes its position and function, not a fifth directional division; it sits within the four-area geographic framework. **Misty Shoals** is the seventh district, an outer maritime zone of small islands, misty waters, navigation routes, and exploration areas. It is not a fifth land area of the main island and may involve routes approaching different sides of the island. Its precise geographic span and safe passages remain undefined.

This is a regional framework, not a detailed street map. Exact borders, elevation, neighborhoods, transit links, and travel times remain undefined until entered in the route network. Do not assume every district is directly connected to every other district.

## 3. Four Cultivator Academies

Tianhai has **four established Cultivator Academies**, one associated with each cardinal area. All four teach the foundational subjects relevant to a cultivator, including Martial Souls, Spirit Realms, Spirit Rings, Spirit Beasts, practical preparation, combat fundamentals, and relevant world knowledge. Their curricula may also introduce the social impact and basic history of Artificial Artifacts, but ordinary academy study does not itself confer Thinker Grade or formulation mastery.

Tianhai's academies are more flexible in teaching methods than the orthodox central academies of the Golden Dragon Empire. Flexibility means that instructors may adapt lesson sequence, practical exercises, mentoring, and learning paths to student aptitude and circumstances. It does **not** mean that an academy may ignore established cultivation requirements, guarantee Spirit Ring absorption, award unearned mastery, or bypass the official combat and Thinker mechanics.

| Cardinal area | Academy | Standing and focus |
|---|---|---|
| North | **Beichen Cultivator Academy** | Emphasizes disciplined readiness, threat awareness, defensive practice, and preparation for maritime and environmental hazards. |
| South | **Tianlan Supreme Academy** | **The most outstanding and prestigious of Tianhai's four academies.** Its strength lies in its breadth of instruction, flexible teaching, intensive practical study, and strong pathway toward advanced Thinker education. It is highly competitive in developing students with the aptitude and preparation to pursue high Thinker Grades; admission or attendance does not guarantee a high grade. |
| West | **Cangfan Maritime Academy** | Emphasizes navigation awareness, maritime life, movement, teamwork, and practical preparation for travel and sea-related risks. |
| East | **Haiyue Academy** | Emphasizes commercial-world literacy, applied problem-solving, resource awareness, and the relationship between cultivation, trade, and specialist craft. |

These are institutional identities and broad educational emphases, not complete curricula. Exact admission ages, fees, examination formats, class sizes, ranking rules, scholarships, graduation standards, and campus layouts remain unspecified. Do not create a fifth Tianhai academy or treat the four as the only academies in the entire world.

The Tianlan Supreme Academy is the leading academy overall; that reputation does not mean every graduate is stronger than graduates from the other three, nor does it establish that it wins every competition.

## 4. Tianhai Thinker Institute — exclusive high-level research institution

The **Tianhai Thinker Institute** is Tianhai's exclusive high-level institution devoted to research across the Thinker discipline. It is the world's sole institution established at this level and breadth of specialization; other regions may still have individual Thinkers, workshops, schools, or research groups, but no second institution of equivalent scope is established in canon.

Its remit includes:

- **Formation Engraver development:** research into tools, instruments, precision practices, and methods used to engrave formations onto Artificial Artifacts.
- **Materials research:** testing, classifying, preparing, processing, and evaluating materials used in formulations and artifact construction.
- **Formulation and mixing methods:** research into compatible combinations, formulation procedures, repeatability, stability, and ways to reduce avoidable production errors.
- **Complex formation engraving for Tier 6 and above:** specialist research and technical work on complex engraving patterns required for high-tier Artificial Artifacts. Such work remains limited by the Thinker's actual Grade, knowledge, materials, tools, and the official production mechanics.
- **Technique optimization for Tier 5 and below:** developing and refining methods that make formation engraving and artifact production more practical, efficient, consistent, and accessible at lower tiers. Optimization is not a guarantee of success and does not raise a Thinker's Grade or remove the mechanics' limits.
- **Testing and quality assurance:** controlled trials, failure analysis, material provenance, documented results, safety procedures, and review of artifact performance.
- **Education and standards:** advanced study, specialist assessment, credential records, and dissemination of approved knowledge where access permits.

The institute is a research and standards center, not a magical source of automatic success. It cannot make an underqualified Thinker capable of a task merely by providing a tool, nor can it guarantee every formulation or artifact will succeed. Grade, focus, mental capacity, stamina, materials, patterns, tools, and the official resolution rules continue to apply.

### Relationship to the four academies

The four academies provide broad cultivator education and may identify students suited to further Thinker study. The Tianhai Thinker Institute provides advanced specialist research, training pathways, assessment, and technical standards. Admission to an academy does not automatically grant admission to the institute, access to restricted research, the title of Formation Engraver, or a Thinker Grade.

The Tianhai Thinker Institute and its existing roster of officials remain governed by `world/NPC_TIANHAI_POWER_STRUCTURE.md`. Its previously established directorates for formulation examination, materials and provenance, applied research, artifact quality inspection, workshop safety, and credentials are interpreted as parts of this expanded mandate; this document does not create extra officeholders or assign new Thinker Grades.
