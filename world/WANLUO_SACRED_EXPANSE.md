# Merzkava Universe — Wanluo Sacred Expanse

**Status:** Owner-requested worldbuilding entry recorded from the current design session.  
**Scope:** Geography surrounding the World Knowledge Tower, pilgrimage routes, settlement districts, religious precinct, access, and governance boundaries.  
**Related modules:** `world/WORLD.md`, `world/MAP_AND_REGIONS.md`, `world/GEOGRAPHY_AND_TRAVEL.md`, `world/FACTIONS.md`, `world/HISTORY.md`.

## 1. Identity and Naming

- **Regional name:** Wanluo Sacred Expanse (万络圣域), rendered in-world as the Wanluo Sacred Expanse or Sacred Domain of Wanluo.
- **Name meaning within the setting:** Wanluo evokes countless interconnected routes converging on a shared center. It symbolizes pilgrimage, the exchange of knowledge, and contact between civilizations.
- **Central relic:** World Knowledge Tower, an ancient divine relic dating to the Early Golden Age.
- **Religious organization (working name):** Ordo Suci Tianming.
- **Central religious complex (working name):** Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows.

The Wanluo Sacred Expanse is the surrounding sacred region, not another name for the Tower itself. The Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows is the religious organization's headquarters and is not part of the Tower relic.

The names Ordo Suci Tianming and Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows are the current recorded names for this design and may be revised by the owner. Their names do not imply ownership of the Tower.

## 2. Physical Geography

The Tower stands on a broad, green plain surrounded by ancient forests and mountain ranges. The surrounding landscape includes grassland, wooded slopes, valleys, streams and rivers, small lakes, rest sites, old monuments, and scattered ruins. The precise coordinates, dimensions, climate details, and exact position relative to the three major powers have not been established.

The landscape is understood in three broad belts:

1. **Outer mountains and ancient forests:** Natural barriers, valleys, passes, rivers, and wooded corridors shape approach routes. Remote areas may remain dangerous and are not assumed safe merely because the Tower is nearby.
2. **The broad green plain:** An open transitional landscape containing converging roads, rest facilities, monuments, green spaces, and settlement approaches.
3. **The central Tower precinct:** The Tower and its immediate managed grounds, ceremonial approaches, courtyards, and access-control points. The surrounding region is not itself part of the relic.

These are functional geographic zones, not measured borders. No coordinates, exact distances, travel durations, or additional supernatural properties are established by this description.

## 3. Pilgrimage Roads and Their Origin

The road network formed gradually through repeated pilgrimage and travel, rather than being laid out all at once by one ruler or created by an unexplained magical effect.

- Early visitors followed passable routes through mountain passes, valleys, forests, and river crossings.
- Repeated use gradually widened the most practical tracks. Pilgrims established markers, shelters, and places to rest.
- As scholars, merchants, religious visitors, and delegations began using the same corridors, some tracks became major roads and crossroads developed into permanent settlements.
- Old paths remain uneven in age, width, upkeep, safety, and popularity. Roads are not all straight; terrain may force them to curve or converge only after many junctions.

The roads' convergence is a historical and geographic result of sustained movement toward the Tower. It is not proof that every road was designed by the Tower, that all routes are safe, or that the Tower controls the travelers using them.

## 4. Settlement Districts

The settlements around the Tower developed along the road network. Four principal functional districts are recognized:

### 4.1 Pilgrim District

Inns, guesthouses, public courtyards, bathing facilities, food stalls, tea houses, and spaces for rest and worship. It accommodates visitors from many backgrounds and becomes busier during major pilgrimage periods. No exact population or seasonal calendar is established.

### 4.2 Scholar District

Libraries, academies, study halls, research spaces, and scholars' residences. Access to a particular collection or research facility is determined by its responsible institution; residence in this district does not grant unrestricted access to the Tower.

### 4.3 Merchant District

Markets, trading houses, supply shops, equipment vendors, tea houses, and lodging serving pilgrims and institutions. Economic influence does not automatically grant political authority over the Tower or the sacred region.

### 4.4 Administration District

Administrative offices, public-service facilities, access-record offices, guard posts, meeting halls, and representatives of parties involved in Tower administration. It coordinates practical regional matters but does not independently own or control the Tower.

These are connected districts within one wider settlement area, not four separate cities. Gardens, waterways, ceremonial grounds, and open green spaces remain between and around built-up areas. Specific street names, settlement populations, prices, and boundaries are unestablished.

## 5. Access and Security

The Wanluo Sacred Expanse is open to visitors under regional rules. Entry is not restricted exclusively to nobles, powerful cultivators, one kingdom, or one religious affiliation. Guards conduct reasonable checks at major entrances and sensitive sites to maintain order, prevent infiltration, and protect people and relics.

Access to the region does not equal access to every facility:

- **Public roads and pilgrim facilities:** Generally accessible to visitors who follow local rules.
- **Residential and commercial districts:** Open under ordinary local regulations.
- **Specialized research institutions:** Governed by the institution's own authorization rules.
- **Inner Tower courtyards and restricted areas:** Governed by the official Tower administration's access protocols.
- **Deeper Tower chambers:** Access depends on rules and mechanisms established for the relic; no additional criteria are invented here.

Security measures do not mean that every visitor is treated as a suspect. Enforcement is expected to follow regional rules rather than automatically favoring a visitor's wealth, title, or political affiliation. Exact inspection procedures and penalties remain unestablished.

## 6. Governance and Jurisdiction

Three distinct kinds of authority must remain separate:

1. **Wanluo regional administration:** Handles roads, districts, markets, public facilities, local disputes, and ordinary public order. Its detailed structure and appointment process remain unestablished.
2. **World Knowledge Tower administration council:** Handles matters directly concerning the Tower, including its access, protection, upkeep, and authorized study. The Shen family is one of several major parties entrusted with rights and responsibilities in this administration. The Tower is an ancient divine relic from the Early Golden Age and is not owned by the Shen family.
3. **Ordo Suci Tianming:** The world's largest religious organization by follower count in the current setting. It oversees its own religious institutions and sacred activities, maintains a broad network, and has a major role in monitoring and coordinating responses to high-level anomalies, walking calamities, and threats to the world's continued survival. Its religious influence does not automatically grant it ownership of the Tower or absolute control of all civil administration in Wanluo.

The organization's central headquarters, Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows, is a dedicated religious precinct within the wider Wanluo region. It is separate from the World Knowledge Tower and is not itself an ancient relic unless a future owner-approved entry establishes otherwise.

The three authorities may cooperate while retaining different responsibilities. The regional administration may handle a road closure, the Tower council may restrict research access because of a relic-related danger, and the religious organization may suspend its own pilgrimage rites. None of these actions automatically grants one institution unlimited authority over the others.

The exact legal charter, representation, voting system, founding history, and boundaries of each authority remain unestablished.

## 7. Ordo Suci Tianming

### 7.1 Position and Mandate

Ordo Suci Tianming is the world's largest religious organization by number of followers and is trusted by many powerful individuals and institutions. Its influence comes from its religious standing, broad reach, and role in observing threats that may affect the world as a whole.

Its established design mandate includes:

- Spiritual guidance, religious observances, and care for its own sacred sites.
- Receiving, recording, and verifying reports of unusual phenomena.
- Maintaining an early-warning network so the world can detect dangerous anomalies before they escalate into existential threats.
- Monitoring high-level anomalies, walking calamities, and threats that may endanger the world's continued survival.
- Operating Ruined Archiver, its specialist branch for collecting, registering, verifying, and preserving intelligence from Golden Age Ruined.
- Assessing threat reports and coordinating information or responses with the World Knowledge Tower and its alliance of elite envoys from Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, and Beiming Gu.
- Maintaining confidential records and regional early-warning channels.

The organization is not omniscient and cannot automatically defeat every threat. Reports require verification; distance, delayed communication, political interests, limited resources, and uncertainty can affect its response. A religious interpretation is not automatically proof of a phenomenon's cause.

### 7.2 Central Headquarters: Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows

The Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows is the central headquarters and dedicated religious complex of Ordo Suci Tianming. It occupies its own precinct within Wanluo, separate from the World Knowledge Tower.

Its principal facilities are:

- **Great Hall of Vows:** Public rites, major ceremonies, formal vows, and religious gatherings.
- **World Observation Archive:** Records verified anomaly reports, disaster accounts, witness testimony, and historical threat records. Sensitive materials are access-controlled.
- **Observation Tower:** Supports observation and analysis of unusual phenomena. It is not assumed to predict every disaster.
- **Threat Assessment Hall:** Reviews reports, compares evidence, classifies risk, and coordinates recommendations or responses. A claim is not accepted as fact solely because a powerful person makes it.
- **Order Response Compound:** Training, preparation, and deployment facilities for members assigned to field operations. It is one division of the organization, not the whole organization.
- **Pilgrim Court and Guest Houses:** Public-facing spaces for visitors, petitions, rest, and contact with the organization.

### 7.3 Precinct Zones

The headquarters precinct contains functional zones:

- **Public zone:** Visitor courtyards, public halls, guest houses, and rites open to the public.
- **Administrative zone:** Offices, records, meetings, and routine operations.
- **Observation zone:** Specialized observation and research facilities, entered by authorized personnel or guests.
- **Internal zone:** Members' residences, senior staff facilities, restricted archives, and confidential meetings.
- **Protection zone:** Facilities and routes for protecting people and organizing evacuation during a dangerous event.

The precinct is not entirely closed to the public. Restrictions apply to facilities that require protection.

### 7.4 World Network

The organization operates through a broad network rather than relying solely on its headquarters:

- **Temples and religious houses:** Worship, spiritual services, and community contact.
- **Observation halls:** Receive reports and monitor unusual events in local areas.
- **Assessment halls:** Check information and forward cases requiring specialist review.
- **Response orders:** Conduct field assignments within their capability and authority.
- **Regional coordination centers:** Connect branches and coordinate cross-regional responses.
- **Ruined Archiver branches:** A named Ordo branch with a branch in every city. It specializes in Ruined expedition registration, map and report verification, fair compensation, official exploration records, and confidential anomaly intelligence. Its detailed operating rules are in world/RUINED_ARCHIVER.md.

Ruined Archiver members wear matching light-brown uniforms. Its internal rank names and staffing structure remain unspecified. Other branch names, leaders, rank structure, doctrine, ritual specifics, fees, formal membership requirements, and combat capabilities remain unestablished unless recorded in a specific authority file.

## 8. Lore and GM Consistency Rules

- Do not conflate the World Knowledge Tower, Wanluo Sacred Expanse, and Sanctum of Ten Thousand Vows. They are respectively the ancient divine relic, the surrounding sacred region, and the religious organization's headquarters.
- Do not state that the Shen family owns the Tower; it is one of several parties with administrative rights and responsibilities.
- Do not assign Wanluo to the territory of the Golden Dragon Empire, Tianhai Yunzhou, or Beiming Gu until the owner establishes its exact geopolitical placement and sovereignty.
- Do not invent coordinates, distances, travel times, population, prices, route safety, or specific settlements beyond the facts in this module.
- The pilgrimage-road network formed gradually from repeated use; it was not established all at once by a ruler and is not confirmed to be a supernatural construction.
- Open access to Wanluo does not imply unrestricted access to the Tower, private institutions, or restricted religious facilities.
- The religious organization's large following and trusted status do not make it omniscient, politically absolute, or guaranteed to win conflicts.
- The exact founders, dates, doctrine, highest leader, formal ranks, and historical events of the religious organization remain unestablished.
- Details in this module describe the currently accepted design concept. Any future revisions by the owner supersede this version.
